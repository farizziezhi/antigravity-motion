# Felt Storybook
3D animation
Every hill, character, and star is crafted from fuzzy matte felt fabric inside a physical open picture book whose italic serif story text sits beneath each page illustration.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Sky | ≈#182E59 | Deep calming bedtime night blue |
| Ground | ≈#3D7A47 | Soft fuzzy felt meadow green |
| Accent | ≈#F7D154 | Warm yellow crescent moon and smiling star props |
| Garment | ≈#D9433B | Bright red ribbon and shoe accents |
| Page | ≈#F6F1E4 | Warm off-white physical book paper border |

## Materials & texture
- Soft fuzzy felt and flannel fabric shaders across hills, foliage, buildings, and figures.
- Zero specular highlights or plastic shine; every surface scatters light softly like wool felt.
- Subtle stitch details and softly rounded fabric edges on characters and props.

## Typography
- Narrative prose sits directly below each illustration inside the white book page margin in warm dark italic serif (`18px` to `22px`, e.g., `"One quiet night, a tiny light drifted into the garden."`).
- Opens on an illustrated hardcover title screen (`"The Fallen Light: a bedtime tale"`).
- Exception to 3D family rule: printed italic serif book lines below the frame replace dark bottom subtitle pills.

## Composition
- Framed as a physical picture book: opens from the closed cover into open double or single page spreads with subtle page numbers.
- One clear scene per page (e.g., cottage, white fence, rolling hills).
- Transitions glide smoothly by zooming into and out of pages rather than using hard cuts.

## Mood
Soothing, tender, sleepy bedtime fairytale.

## Signature elements
- Physical picture-book framing with an opening cover, page margins, and printed serif narrative below the art.
- All-felt surface texture with visible soft wool fuzz and zero gloss across every 3D asset.
- Calming night-blue, felt-green, and warm star-yellow contrast.
- Soft felt doll archetype (simple stitched proportions, polka-dot dress, red shoes and ribbon, gentle dot eyes) alongside expressive smiling felt props.
- Smooth zoom-in and zoom-out page transitions instead of abrupt scene cuts.

## Recipe
1. Cover every 3D asset with a high-roughness (`0.98`) wool felt shader and fine strand fuzz silhouette at `1px` to `2px`.
2. Pair deep night blue (`≈#182E59`) skies with felt meadow green (`≈#3D7A47`) hills and warm yellow (`≈#F7D154`) moon and stars.
3. Frame each shot inside a physical open book margin (`≈#F6F1E4`) occupying roughly 75% upper art and 25% lower text band.
4. Set story lines beneath the art in dark italic serif (`≈#2B2623`) with generous line height (`1.5`).
5. Transition between scenes via smooth 1.4-second camera zoom-outs and zoom-ins across successive book spreads rather than hard cuts.

## Don'ts
- Do not use glossy plastics, hard metallic surfaces, or sharp low-poly facets.
- Do not use dark semi-transparent subtitle pills or modern sans UI overlays.
- Do not cut sharply between shots when a zoom page-turn fits.
- Do not break the cozy picture-book frame into full-bleed realistic landscapes.

## One-frame test
- [ ] Every hill, prop, and figure visibly reads as fuzzy matte felt fabric with zero shine.
- [ ] The illustration is framed inside a physical storybook cover or page spread.
- [ ] Italic serif story text sits cleanly beneath the artwork without a dark subtitle pill.

## Base prompt
3D children's bedtime storybook crafted entirely from soft fuzzy matte felt and flannel fabric, deep night-blue sky (≈#182E59) dotted with warm yellow crescent moon and stars (≈#F7D154) over fuzzy green felt hills (≈#3D7A47), cute felt doll figure in polka-dot dress and red ribbon (≈#D9433B) beside a smiling felt star, framed inside an open physical picture book page (≈#F6F1E4) with italic serif story text printed cleanly below the illustration, zero gloss, gentle soothing mood.
