# Red Planet Base
3D animation
A silent rust-orange desert planet holds stark white modular domes traced with glowing cyan trim, warm amber windows, and a single glass greenhouse holding the only patch of living green.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | ≈#C95D2E | Rust orange regolith dunes and dusty sky haze |
| Habitat | ≈#EFF3F6 | Clean smooth white composite base modules |
| Trim | ≈#36E2F5 | Crisp glowing cyan module seam and airlock strips |
| Window | ≈#FF9A3C | Warm amber interior habitat light glow |
| Life | ≈#4CB944 | Single intentional green plant patch inside glass dome |

## Materials & texture
- Realistic hard-sci-fi surfaces: wind-rippled fine sand, craggy iron-oxide rock, smooth satin white composite shells, and curved low-reflection dome glass.
- Subtle atmospheric dust scattering across distant ridges.

## Typography
- Exception to 3D family rule: zero large on-screen titles, zero callout labels, and zero subtitle pills; visual framing alone tells the story.

## Composition
- Vast, empty horizon framing a spider-like radial habitat complex (central hub dome, extending tubular connectors, solar wing arrays, and translucent botanical dome).
- One tiny white spacesuit figure stands outside on the dunes solely to establish monumental scale against the desert.
- Slow, unhurried spacecraft-like camera drift with no abrupt cuts.

## Mood
Quietly awe-inspiring, vast, solitary, yet quietly hopeful.

## Signature elements
- Strict five-hue hard sci-fi palette: rust desert orange cut only by white habitats, cyan light strips, amber windows, and one deliberate green dome.
- Spider-like modular outpost architecture linked by radiating pressurized tubes and solar arrays.
- Glass biodome containing lush green plants as the solitary symbol of life on an empty world.
- Tiny faceless white spacesuit figure acting strictly as a scale benchmark against huge terrain.
- Slow, continuous orbital and low-hover camera glides with zero text clutter.

## Recipe
1. Sculpt wind-rippled rust dunes (`≈#C95D2E`) beneath a hazy butterscotch-rust sky (`45%` distant atmospheric fog).
2. Assemble a central white geodesic or faceted dome (`≈#EFF3F6`) connected radially by tubular corridors and solar wings.
3. Accent airlock seams with `2px` emissive cyan strips (`≈#36E2F5`) and interior portholes with warm amber (`≈#FF9A3C`).
4. Populate one clear glass dome with vibrant green foliage (`≈#4CB944`) and leave zero other vegetation anywhere outdoors.
5. Glide a 35mm virtual drone slowly across the base (`0.4m/s`, zero cuts) past one small white astronaut silhouette.

## Don'ts
- Do not add big title banners, technical HUD callouts, or narration subtitle pills.
- Do not scatter green grass, trees, or blue Earth skies outside the sealed greenhouse dome.
- Do not use low-poly cartoon shapes, comic outlines, or fast comedic cuts.
- Do not crowd the screen with busy space battles or extra neon rainbow colors.

- [ ] One sealed glass dome holds the single intentional splash of living green.
- [ ] Zero large text overlays appear while a tiny spacesuit figure provides scale.
