# Product Teardown
3D animation
One glossy hero product floats centered over an empty warm cream cyclorama, exploding symmetrically into numbered parts tracked by a live counter and single pointer labels.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Backdrop | ≈#F4EFE6 | Plain warm cream studio cyclorama with zero scenery |
| Hero | ≈#D92929 | Glossy painted cherry-red outer body panels |
| Hardware | ≈#222428 | Matte black rubber and dark charcoal mechanical parts |
| Metal | ≈#8C929A | Brushed neutral steel and alloy internal fasteners |

## Materials & texture
- Realistic commercial product shaders: high-gloss clearcoat lacquer on hero shells, brushed steel, and soft matte vulcanized rubber.
- Soft studio area-light reflections and clean contact shadows beneath floating assemblies.
- Strictly zero background props, rooms, or environmental textures.

## Typography
- Small red all-caps header (`"TEARDOWN"`, `14px`, letter-spaced) stacked directly above a bold black product title (`"Commuter Vehicle"`, `36px`) at top center.
- Live mechanical counter (`"6 / 90 parts"` climbing to `"90 / 90 parts"`) tracking disassembly progress.
- Single thin pointer label (`"Seat"`) highlighting one focal part at a time, plus brief closing prompts at bottom center (`"90 parts, one machine."`, `"What should we open next?"`).

## Composition
- Single mechanical object locked dead-center on a boundless warm cream studio floor/void.
- Symmetrical exploded-view choreography: outer panels and internal components drift outward along clean axes with equal spacing, then snap smoothly back together.

## Mood
Crisp, curious, polished commercial precision.

## Signature elements
- Strict three-tone palette (warm cream backdrop, glossy hero red, matte black/gray hardware).
- Symmetrical floating exploded view where separated parts hover at even intervals before reassembling.
- Live ascending part counter (`X / 90 parts`) providing visual progression without clutter.
- Minimalist top title stack (tiny red uppercase kicker + bold black noun) and single thin pointer callouts.
- Open-ended serial closing question inviting viewers to pick the next object.

## Recipe
1. Place one detailed mechanical product centered on a curved warm cream studio cyclorama (`≈#F4EFE6`) with soft three-point area lighting.
2. Restrict hero paint to glossy red (`≈#D92929`, clearcoat `0.9`) and internal hardware to dark gray/black (`≈#222428`) and brushed steel (`≈#8C929A`).
3. Animate a clean radial and axial disassembly over 4 seconds so every bolt, frame, and panel hovers with uniform spacing.
4. Pair a ticking top-corner or upper counter (`6 / 90` to `90 / 90`) with one crisp `1px` callout line when spotlighting a key sub-assembly.
5. Reverse-snap all parts back into a solid unit and close on a bottom-center prompt question.

## Don'ts
- Do not add streets, garages, workbenches, or any environmental scenery behind the cream backdrop.
- Do not scatter exploded parts randomly; spacing and alignment must stay geometric and symmetrical.
- Do not introduce bright neon colors or comic outlines beyond the three-tone cream, red, and black/gray scheme.
- Do not crowd the frame with dozens of simultaneous callout labels; show one clean highlight at a time.

## One-frame test
- [ ] A single glossy red-and-black mechanical product floats exploded or assembled on a plain warm cream studio void.
- [ ] Parts hover symmetrically with a live part counter (`X / 90`) or a single thin callout label.
- [ ] Zero environmental props or extra colors break the cream, red, and black/gray trio.
