# Archviz Timelapse
3D animation
A modern two-story residence rises cleanly from an empty grass plot across five numbered construction chapters, finishing in a full sunlit orbit around the completed garden home.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Lawn | ≈#6EA34D | Natural sunlit meadow and finished yard grass |
| Concrete | ≈#B5B8BA | Realistic poured architectural slab and frame gray |
| Timber | ≈#9E6B43 | Warm natural wood slats and louver accents |
| Glass | ≈#C8DFEB | Large architectural glazing reflecting daylight sky |
| UI Text | ≈#FFFFFF | Crisp white numbered chapter header and progress rule |

## Materials & texture
- Realistic architectural visualization shaders: poured matte concrete, large reflective double-pane glass, warm timber cladding, and manicured lawn grass.
- Soft, consistent daylight illumination free of theatrical color grading or flashy neon accents.

## Typography
- Numbered chapter label anchored cleanly in the upper-left corner (`"01 EMPTY LOT"`, `"02 FOUNDATION"`, `"03 STRUCTURE"`, `"04 LIVING SHELL"`, `"05 FINISHED HOME"`) paired with a thin horizontal progress bar.
- Exception to 3D family rule: no bottom subtitle pill or expressive pop-up cards; the clean architectural HUD carries pacing.

## Composition
- Consistent elevated three-quarter architectural site angle across early build chapters so structural growth reads like a true timelapse.
- Zero human characters; equipment like steel rebar, concrete frames, and a tower crane act as stage indicators before vanishing into landscaped walkways and garden lights.
- Culminates in a smooth 360-degree orbital sweep around the finished property.

## Mood
Orderly, grounded, deeply satisfying craftsmanship.

## Signature elements
- Five-chapter numbered construction sequence anchored at top-left with a synchronized progress line.
- Natural construction palette strictly limited to field green, concrete gray, warm timber, and clear glass.
- Authentic stage-by-stage structural build props (excavated trenches, steel rebar cages, concrete columns, crane, glazing).
- Complete absence of human characters, keeping the building itself as the sole protagonist.
- Smooth full-orbit reveal around the finished house, stone garden path, and glowing yard lamps.

## Recipe
1. Establish an empty sunlit grass plot (`≈#6EA34D`) with consistent natural daylight (`5500K`, soft directional shadows).
2. Lock a top-left chapter template (`20px` bold sans numeral + uppercase phase name + thin progress line advancing `20%` per chapter).
3. Animate construction stages sequentially across concrete (`≈#B5B8BA`) footings, rebar cages, framing with a crane, timber slats (`≈#9E6B43`), and full glass panes (`≈#C8DFEB`).
4. Transition smoothly from timelapse build angles into manicured landscaping, stone walkways, and warm garden bollards.
5. Finish with a continuous 360-degree orbital camera sweep around the completed residence.

## Don'ts
- Do not populate the site with cartoon characters, talking guides, or emotional facial close-ups.
- Do not inject bright primary comic colors, neon accents, or dark thriller contrast.
- Do not skip intermediate structural stages like rebar foundations and raw framing.
- Do not cover the architectural reveal with bottom narration subtitle pills.

## One-frame test
- [ ] Realistic concrete, timber, glass, and grass surfaces show a clearly recognizable stage of home construction.
- [ ] A numbered chapter label with a thin progress line sits neatly in the upper-left corner.
- [ ] Zero human characters appear; natural daylight showcases architectural craft.
