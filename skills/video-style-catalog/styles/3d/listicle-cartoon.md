# Listicle Cartoon
3D animation
A persistent green header and numbered red-highlighted listicle cards introduce big-headed 3D cartoon commuters performing fast 5-second visual gags on a sunlit city street.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Header | ≈#229954 | Persistent vivid green top listicle banner |
| Asphalt | ≈#5E646B | Sunlit neutral street gray |
| Sky | ≈#5BB5F0 | Bright clear midday sky blue |
| Highlight | ≈#E6302A | Red keywords inside cards and primary jacket accents |
| SFX | ≈#FFD426 | Bright comic yellow sound-effect text |

## Materials & texture
- Smooth, clean stylized 3D cartoon materials with bright midday specular highlights on helmets and vehicles.
- Distinct urban street markings: black-and-white painted curbs, simple storefronts, and traffic signals.

## Typography
- Persistent green banner locked at the top edge featuring a small white vehicle icon and bold all-caps category title (`"EVERYDAY RIDER TYPES"`).
- Pop-up numbered badge cards (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`) in bold white sans-serif with key punchline words highlighted in red (`≈#E6302A`), e.g., `"Signals left, turns RIGHT"`.
- Comic speech bubbles (`"SAFE!"`) and slanted yellow comic SFX lettering (`"SCREEECH!"`). Closing prompt card (`"Which type are you?"`).

## Composition
- Bright urban street cross-section framing vehicles and commuters at eye level or slight three-quarter angles.
- Rapid listicle rhythm: one numbered archetype card triggers one visual gag lasting 5 seconds or less.
- Oversized character heads keep exaggerated bug-eyed expressions legible alongside vehicles.

## Mood
Upbeat, observational, brisk everyday street comedy.

## Signature elements
- Persistent green top title bar with a small category icon visible throughout the video.
- Sequentially numbered listicle cards (`1` to `4`) pairing bold white sans text with red keyword highlights.
- Big-headed 3D commuter archetypes wearing colorful helmets with wildly exaggerated eyes and wide grins.
- Comic sound-effect callouts in bright yellow and crisp speech bubbles over sunlit asphalt.
- Direct closing viewer-poll question designed to spark comment debate.

## Recipe
1. Lock a `68px` bright green header bar (`≈#229954`) across the top of the frame with a flat white vehicle icon and bold title.
2. Light a clean city street (`≈#5E646B` road, striped curbs, `≈#5BB5F0` sky) with sharp midday sunlight (`5500K`).
3. Model riders with enlarged heads (`1.5x` body width), colorful glossy helmets, and elastic facial rigs for wide eyes and grins.
4. Pop in a numbered card (`1` to `4`) for each 4-to-5 second gag, highlighting the twist word in red (`≈#E6302A`).
5. Snap yellow comic SFX text (`≈#FFD426`) onto brake or impact frames, then finish on a centered viewer question card.

## Don'ts
- Do not spend more than 5 seconds on any single numbered item or drag out long dialogues.
- Do not use moody night lighting, desaturated grays, or realistic human proportions.
- Do not drop the persistent green top banner during standard list items.
- Do not tell a continuous single plot when discrete numbered archetypes define the format.

## One-frame test
- [ ] A persistent green top banner labels the listicle theme above a bright sunlit city street.
- [ ] A numbered card with red-highlighted keywords or yellow comic SFX accompanies a big-headed cartoon commuter.
- [ ] Exaggerated eyes, grin, and primary-colored helmet or vehicle colors pop against neutral gray asphalt.
