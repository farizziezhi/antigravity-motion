# Comic 3D
3D animation
Expressive 3D cartoon characters are coated in halftone dots, thick black ink outlines, speed lines, and oversized comic speech bubbles inside 3-panel cream paper layouts.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | ≈#1E5FD9 | Bold primary comic blue |
| Secondary | ≈#E63228 | Punchy primary comic red |
| Accent | ≈#FFD426 | Bright primary yellow garments and narrator tags |
| Paper | ≈#F7EED7 | Warm cream comic-page panel gutter backdrop |
| Ink | ≈#111111 | Thick black outlines, halftone dots, and borders |

## Materials & texture
- Cel-shaded 3D character surfaces layered with 2D print halftone dot patterns in midtones and shadows.
- Bold uniform black ink outlines (`3px` to `5px`) around silhouettes and props.
- Simplified interior backgrounds so characters and graphic overlays stay dominant.

## Typography
- Bold italic comic-book display lettering with thick black stroke (`"WAIT?!"`, `"OKAY"` inside giant white speech bubbles).
- Jagged starburst action callouts (`"THE END!"`) and slanted yellow narrator banner boxes (`"TURNS OUT..."`).
- Exception to 3D family rule: speech bubbles, slanted yellow narrator boxes, and starburst SFX replace standard bottom subtitle pills.

## Composition
- Alternates between close-up reaction shots and multi-panel comic grids (3 panels side-by-side or stacked on a cream paper page).
- Heavy radial speed lines converge on oversized faces during punchlines.
- Minimal background clutter behind characters to keep ink borders readable.

## Mood
Energetic, exaggerated, playful, punchline-driven comic strip.

## Signature elements
- 3D stylized cartoon archetypes (oversized expressive eyes, elastic mouths, bright primary-colored outfits) fused with 2D print comic ink.
- Printed comic overlay package: Ben-Day halftone shading, heavy black outlines, and radial speed lines.
- Multi-panel 3-to-4 panel strip layout appearing directly inside a single video frame on cream paper.
- Oversized speech bubbles, yellow slanted caption tags, and jagged action starbursts.
- Punchy twist ending delivered by one bold final-panel word or exclamation.

## Recipe
1. Render stylized 3D figures with cel shading, then composite `45-degree` halftone dots (`22%` opacity) and `4px` solid black (`≈#111111`) ink outlines.
2. Saturate clothing and prop accents to pure primaries (`≈#1E5FD9`, `≈#E63228`, `≈#FFD426`) against simplified rooms.
3. Add converging black/white speed lines behind peak reaction shots (12 frames per punchline).
4. Arrange 3 distinct beats onto a cream paper page (`≈#F7EED7`) separated by `10px` black panel gutters.
5. Finish every panel beat with a single oversized comic word, slanted yellow tag, or starburst callout.

## Don'ts
- Do not leave 3D renders looking plain, softly lit, or photorealistic without comic ink overlays.
- Do not use muted pastels or dark moody thriller palettes.
- Do not pack detailed realistic scenery behind the characters.
- Do not replace comic speech bubbles with standard dark bottom subtitle pills.

## One-frame test
- [ ] 3D characters clearly wear thick black outlines, halftone shading, or radial speed lines.
- [ ] Primary colors pop against a cream comic gutter or simplified stage.
- [ ] A speech bubble, slanted yellow caption, starburst SFX, or 3-panel layout drives the frame.

## Base prompt
3D cartoon animation stylized as a bold print comic book, expressive exaggerated cartoon characters in bright primary yellow (≈#FFD426), red (≈#E63228), and blue (≈#1E5FD9) coated with thick black ink outlines (≈#111111) and Ben-Day halftone dot shading, radial speed lines, giant comic speech bubbles, slanted yellow caption tag, and starburst action text arranged inside a 3-panel cream paper comic strip layout (≈#F7EED7), lively comedic energy, minimal clean background.
