# Arcade Escalation
3D animation
A pastel low-poly neighborhood turns into a chaotic arcade game as an escalating HUD counter and multiplier badges track bright orange critters multiplying endlessly past a defeated resident.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | ≈#F27D24 | Bright orange critters and HUD highlight color |
| Pastel A | ≈#F6D6C8 | Soft sunlit pastel house walls |
| Pastel B | ≈#A9DCE3 | Light pastel sky and street trims |
| Garment | ≈#2E6FD9 | Resident's blue wrap accent against white tank top |
| HUD | ≈#FFFFFF | Bold white arcade counter text and outlined subtitles |

## Materials & texture
- Clean matte low-poly houses, picket fences, and neighborhood lanes under cheerful daylight.
- Smooth bright orange stylized cats/critters standing out as the sole saturated repeating color mass.
- Crisp flat 2D arcade HUD graphics anchored over the top corners.

## Typography
- Bold orange-and-white arcade HUD at top: live numerical counter (`"CRITTERS 1"` -> `"2"` -> `"16"` -> `"1,024"`), multiplier badges (`"×2"`, `"×4"`, `"×8"`), and escalating stage tags (`"LEVEL 1 BROOM"`, `"LEVEL 2 HOSE"`, `"LEVEL 3 VACUUM"`, `"LEVEL 4 TRUCK"`).
- Exception to 3D family rule: thick bold white outlined bottom subtitles (no pill box) with keywords highlighted in orange (`≈#F27D24`).

## Composition
- Mid-shot neighborhood yard and doorway framing the resident against rising waves of orange critters.
- Each level escalates tool scale (broom to water hose to vacuum to mini fire truck) while critter density floods the frame.
- Final beat holds on the resident's tired reaction surrounded wall-to-wall by orange shapes.

## Mood
Absurd, escalating, lighthearted slapstick defeat.

## Signature elements
- Full arcade-game HUD (live entity counter, multiplier chips, numbered tool level tags) acting as the core joke engine.
- Exponential visual escalation from `1` to `1,024` bright orange critters flooding pastel daylight sets.
- Resident archetype (mustached adult in white tank top and blue wrap) whose facial expression grows progressively more resigned at every tier.
- Bold white black-outlined bottom subtitles with orange keyword highlights.
- Comedic surrender punchline (`"Okay, fine..."`) instead of a heroic victory.

## Recipe
1. Set up a bright pastel low-poly neighborhood (`≈#F6D6C8` walls, `≈#A9DCE3` sky) so the single saturated orange (`≈#F27D24`) critter color pops instantly.
2. Anchor top HUD elements with bold condensed sans (`"LEVEL 1 TOOL"` top-left, counter + `×2`/`×4` multiplier badges top-right).
3. Double or multiply the spawned orange critter count at each 3-to-4 second level bump until they cover the ground plane.
4. Animate the mustached resident's brows and posture drooping further each level from confident to exhausted resignation.
5. Style bottom subtitles as `34px` bold white sans with `3px` dark outline and orange (`≈#F27D24`) keywords, ending on a quiet defeated sigh.

## Don'ts
- Do not make the protagonist succeed or heroically clear the swarm at the end.
- Do not use multi-colored critters; keep the multiplying mass strictly one bright orange hue.
- Do not treat the arcade HUD as subtle corner decoration; keep level tags and multipliers big and punchy.
- Do not use rounded dark translucent subtitle pills behind the bottom captions.

## One-frame test
- [ ] Bold arcade HUD level tags, entity counters, or multiplier badges dominate the top of the frame.
- [ ] Bright orange stylized cats or critters flood a cheerful pastel low-poly yard around a resigned resident.
- [ ] Bottom captions appear as bold outlined white text with orange keywords, not inside a dark pill.

## Base prompt
Bright pastel low-poly 3D neighborhood in cheerful daylight with soft peach walls (≈#F6D6C8) and light blue sky (≈#A9DCE3), exasperated mustached resident archetype in white tank top and blue wrap surrounded by dozens of identical bright orange stylized cats (≈#F27D24) multiplying across the yard, crisp orange-and-white arcade game HUD at top displaying level tags, live counter, and multiplier badges, thick white outlined bottom subtitles with orange keywords, absurd comedic escalation.
