# Building Cutaway
3D animation
A simplified clean white multi-story building sits sliced open in cross-section among stylized green diorama trees so moving internal machinery, dotted callouts, and live floor badges play out in one continuous camera move.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | ≈#F5F7F8 | Clean clinical white architectural concrete shell |
| Accent A | ≈#7CD9BC | Friendly soft mint background and UI highlights |
| Accent B | ≈#F47B2A | Bright safety orange mechanical accents and badges |
| Glass | ≈#A9D6EC | Simplified light blue architectural window glazing |
| Foliage | ≈#58B368 | Stylized rounded green diorama trees |

## Materials & texture
- Simplified, friendly architectural materials: smooth satin white concrete, clean light blue glass panes, and brushed steel cables and guide rails.
- Matte stylized rounded foliage orbs framing the base of the building.

## Typography
- Small crisp uppercase mechanical labels anchored with dotted pointer lines (`"STEEL CABLE"`, `"GUIDE RAIL"`, `"COUNTERWEIGHT"`).
- Live dynamic status badges tracking moving components vertically (`"▲ FLOOR 3"`, `"▲ FLOOR 1"` in bright orange/mint pills).
- Clean chapter header (`"SECTION · 05: Inside a lift shaft"`). Standard bottom subtitle pill optional, or let clean callouts lead.

## Composition
- Single diorama-like multi-story building ringed by simple round green trees, sliced cleanly down the vertical front plane to reveal the interior shaft.
- Continuous uncut camera journey: starts outside the building, glides smoothly straight inside the exposed cross-section to inspect cables and pulleys, then pulls smoothly back outside.

## Mood
Clinical yet friendly, curious, mechanically transparent.

## Signature elements
- Clean architectural cross-section slice exposing hidden interior machinery inside a simplified white structure.
- Crisp white, soft mint, and safety-orange color coding separating shell, diagram backdrop, and active parts.
- Dotted leader-line callouts pointing cleanly to mechanical components as the camera passes.
- Animated live status badges (e.g., floor indicators) riding alongside moving internal parts.
- Single continuous uncut camera glide from exterior diorama into the sliced shaft and back out.

## Recipe
1. Model one clean multi-story white building (`≈#F5F7F8`) with light blue windows (`≈#A9D6EC`) and stylized round green perimeter trees (`≈#58B368`) over a soft mint backdrop (`≈#7CD9BC`).
2. Apply a vertical boolean cross-section slice across the front facade to expose the working internal shaft, pulleys, and orange-accented cabin (`≈#F47B2A`).
3. Choreograph one uncut 35mm camera path gliding from exterior wide shot into tight mechanical close-ups and retreating smoothly outside.
4. Attach `2px` dotted leader lines with circular anchor dots (`6px` diameter) pointing to moving parts as they enter frame.
5. Parent small live status pills (`"▲ FLOOR 3"`, orange `≈#F47B2A` accent) directly to the moving cabin's vertical position.

## Don'ts
- Do not use hard jarring cuts when a continuous exterior-to-interior camera glide works.
- Do not add gritty photoreal concrete dirt, rust, or dark thriller lighting.
- Do not populate the cutaway with cartoon comedy characters or comic speech bubbles.
- Do not hide active mechanical parts behind opaque exterior walls.

## One-frame test
- [ ] A clean white multi-story building sits sliced open in cross-section among stylized round diorama trees.
- [ ] Dotted pointer-line labels and live status badges track moving internal machinery in mint and orange accents.
- [ ] The aesthetic feels bright, simplified, and mechanically transparent without dark grime.
