# Stick Slapstick
3D animation
Oversized round-headed 3D stick figures with dot eyes and thin limb tubes trade snappy physical comedy inside warm wooden street-market stalls underscored by bold black-outlined white subtitles.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | ≈#8C5832 | Warm market timber and wooden barrel brown |
| Accent A | ≈#E63B2E | Bright comedic red fabric and stall trim |
| Accent B | ≈#2A7BE4 | Vivid primary blue garment and umbrella hue |
| Accent C | ≈#F7C93B | Cheerful primary yellow vendor accents |
| Specular | ≈#E4F6FC | Glossy ice and crystalline salt highlights |

## Materials & texture
- Stylized simple geometric forms for figures paired with rich warm wood grains, woven cloth awnings, and glossy crystalline ice or salt props.
- Clean bright daylight illumination across bustling open-air stall props (wooden buckets, balance scales, umbrellas).

## Typography
- Exception to 3D family rule: thick, bold white sans-serif subtitles with a heavy black outline (`3px` to `4px` stroke), centered at lower third with zero pill box.
- Subtitles appear as punchy single words or tiny phrases (`"wait:"`, `"frozen!"`, `"laughed"`), often hanging briefly before a cut.

## Composition
- Tight medium and wide setups across lively open-air market stalls crowded with recognizable everyday props.
- Fast 2-to-3 second cuts timed tightly to physical reactions and single hanging subtitle words.
- Oversized spherical heads keep facial expressions readable even in wide group shots.

## Mood
Warm, loud, playful, fast-paced street-market slapstick.

## Signature elements
- 3D stick-puppet character archetype: giant spherical head, thin cylindrical torso and arms, simple two-dot eyes and elastic mouth capable of huge laughs and shock.
- Thick black-outlined white bottom subtitles with no background pill container.
- Warm wooden street-market environment contrasted against bright primary cloth accents and glossy ice/salt details.
- Snappy 2-to-3 second comedic pacing where single punchline words linger right before each cut.

## Recipe
1. Model characters with spherical heads (roughly 40% of total height) mounted on thin cylindrical torsos and noodle-like limbs with zero finger clutter.
2. Animate mouths and eyes cleanly from neutral dots to huge open laughs or wide-eyed shock on 2-frame snaps.
3. Build a warm wooden market set (`≈#8C5832`) accented with bright red (`≈#E63B2E`), blue (`≈#2A7BE4`), and yellow (`≈#F7C93B`) awnings and shiny crystal props (`≈#E4F6FC`).
4. Cut every 2 to 3 seconds on peak physical slapstick poses.
5. Center heavy white sans subtitles (`36px` bold) with a `4px` black stroke at bottom center, leaving the punchline word hanging for `0.4s` before cutting.

## Don'ts
- Do not enclose subtitles inside rounded dark translucent pill boxes.
- Do not model anatomically realistic proportions, skin pores, or detailed fingers.
- Do not slow down into long lingering thriller takes or solemn documentary pacing.
- Do not strip out the warm wood and everyday market clutter into an empty studio void.

## One-frame test
- [ ] Characters clearly have giant spherical heads on thin stick-like 3D bodies with two-dot eyes and big comic mouths.
- [ ] Warm wooden stalls, bright primary fabrics, and tactile everyday props fill the frame.
- [ ] Thick white black-outlined single-word or short subtitles sit centered at the bottom without a pill box.

## Base prompt
