# Map Documentary
3D animation
Dark ocean cartography and smoking relief terrain carry giant changing measurement numbers, glowing white-outlined human silhouettes, and cited historical quotes in an austere teal-and-orange documentary palette.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | ≈#0B2C38 | Deep dark teal ocean map and calm baseline |
| Secondary | ≈#164454 | Cartographic landmass and relief midtone |
| Accent | ≈#F26B1D | Intense eruption orange sky and hazard glow |
| Line | ≈#F5F7F8 | Crisp white silhouette outlines, rings, and numbers |
| Text | ≈#E6ECEF | Clean light documentary serif and header type |

## Materials & texture
- Stylized dark matte cartographic terrain and sea surfaces overlaid with luminous 2D vector lines.
- Soft volumetric smoke plumes rising from 3D relief peaks lit from below by orange glow.
- Human figures rendered purely as flat dark silhouettes with bright white contour edges (`2px`).

## Typography
- Bold condensed all-caps headers for location and era markers (`"REGION · APRIL 1815"`, `"ISLAND RIDGE"`).
- Giant crisp numerals integrated directly above 3D terrain (`"3,380 m"` morphing to `"2,850 m"`).
- Italic serif source citations with attribution (`"The sound resembled distant cannon fire. JOURNAL ENTRY, 1830"`), plus a `"TO BE CONTINUED"` closing card with brief reference list. No bottom subtitle pill.

## Composition
- Alternates between top-down dark ocean maps with expanding concentric circles and dashed route lines, and low-angle architectural or mountain diorama shots.
- Silhouetted figures stand strictly in the foreground to establish monumental scale.

## Mood
Solemn, investigative, monumental historical documentary.

## Signature elements
- Strict two-color emotional contrast: deep dark teal cartography vs fierce eruption orange glow.
- Animated map overlays combining expanding concentric shockwave rings and dashed trajectory paths.
- Giant changing measurement numerals floating directly over 3D landscape models.
- Human presence limited to glowing white-outlined silhouettes used purely as scale markers.
- Explicit italic serif source citations and an episodic `"TO BE CONTINUED"` bibliography end card.

## Recipe
1. Restrict the entire scene to deep teal (`≈#0B2C38`) for sea/map states and blazing orange (`≈#F26B1D`) for disaster/sky beats.
2. Draw concentric expanding shockwave rings (`2px` white, fading `80%` to `0%` opacity) and dashed vector route paths across top-down maps.
3. Place flat black silhouettes with a `2px` glowing white rim (`≈#F5F7F8`) in front of forts, villages, or mountain slopes.
4. Animate oversized measurement numerals (`72px` bold sans) ticking or morphing smoothly above the 3D terrain model.
5. Display quoted primary sources in italic serif with author and year, closing on a centered condensed `"TO BE CONTINUED"` slate.

## Don'ts
- Do not model detailed expressive 3D human faces or cartoon characters.
- Do not introduce bright greens, pastels, or multi-color comic palettes.
- Do not use bottom narration subtitle pills or playful social-media UI cards.
- Do not present historical claims or quotes without an on-screen source citation.

## One-frame test
- [ ] The palette splits sharply between dark map teal and bright eruption orange.
- [ ] Concentric map rings, dashed routes, giant changing numbers, or white-outlined silhouettes anchor the shot.
- [ ] Condensed caps headers or cited italic serif quotes appear without cartoon characters or subtitle pills.

## Base prompt
Austere 3D historical documentary map visualization in a strict two-tone palette of deep ocean teal (≈#0B2C38) and glowing eruption orange (≈#F26B1D), dark relief cartography overlaid with expanding white concentric wave rings and dashed trajectory lines, smoking 3D mountain model beneath giant crisp elevation numerals, foreground human figures shown solely as black silhouettes with glowing white contour outlines for scale, bold condensed uppercase titles and italic serif cited quotes, solemn monumental tone.
