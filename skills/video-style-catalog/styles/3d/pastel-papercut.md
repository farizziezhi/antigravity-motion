# Pastel Papercut
3D animation
Every character, prop, and doorway looks hand-cut from thick matte pastel cardstock, stacked in shallow layers with soft paper drop shadows under even daylight.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | ≈#FBF5E8 | Soft warm cream paper base |
| Secondary | ≈#F29A8F | Coral pink cardstock accent |
| Accent | ≈#F5CE5D | Warm pastel yellow scarves, badges, and circles |
| Cool Fill | ≈#AEE2D2 | Soft mint foliage and architectural layers |
| Sky | ≈#B7DDF5 | Light pastel sky blue |

## Materials & texture
- Thick matte construction paper shader with subtle fibrous paper grain and zero specular shine.
- Slightly rough die-cut edges with crisp 1mm to 2mm paper thickness visible on silhouettes.
- Soft ambient occlusion and gentle layered drop shadows separating foreground from background sheets.

## Typography
- Small crisp paper-tag labels (e.g., `"5 MINS"` with red numeral and dark charcoal text on a white rectangular tag).
- Circular numbered badges (`"1"` inside a soft yellow disk) and flat play-button icons mounted directly on doors.
- Standard 3D subtitle pill optional when voiceover is present; keep container light or unobtrusive.

## Composition
- Shallow stage-like depth constructed from parallel parallax paper planes.
- Framed around centered doorways that act as portals from one miniature diorama scene to the next.
- Even, shadowless daytime illumination across the full canvas.

## Mood
Gentle, storybook-light, whimsical, and dreamy.

## Signature elements
- Layered flat cardstock geometry with tactile cut edges and soft inter-layer shadows.
- Door-to-door journey structure where each doorway opens into a new pastel micro-world.
- Paper-doll character archetype (bob hair silhouette, bright red coat, yellow scarf, simple dot eyes) with a stylized yellow paper cat wearing a bow tie.
- Clean miniature paper tags and circular yellow number tokens integrated on props.
- Completely matte, shine-free pastel surfaces bathed in soft daylight.

## Recipe
1. Build all assets from extruded 2D silhouettes (`2px` to `4px` edge thickness) with slightly irregular cut contours.
2. Apply a matte paper roughness (`0.95`) and subtle paper-fiber bump map across cream (`≈#FBF5E8`), mint (`≈#AEE2D2`), and coral (`≈#F29A8F`) layers.
3. Light the stage with broad softbox daylight (zero harsh directional shadows; ambient shadow offset `4px` down, `18%` opacity).
4. Place a centered pastel door with a play-icon motif and zoom straight through the opening (1.2s ease-in-out) to transition scenes.
5. Pin small white paper tags and yellow badge circles onto key props for pacing markers.

## Don'ts
- Do not use glossy plastics, metallic reflections, or realistic skin shaders.
- Do not introduce dark night lighting, harsh black shadows, or neon colors.
- Do not build smooth spherical 3D models that hide the flat layered paper construction.
- Do not cut abruptly between unrelated spaces when a doorway transition fits.

## One-frame test
- [ ] Every object reads as stacked matte pastel cardstock with visible cut edges and soft drop shadows.
- [ ] Even, bright daylight lights a cream-and-pastel palette with zero dark corners.
- [ ] A centered doorway, paper tag, or simple paper-cut figure anchors the frame.
