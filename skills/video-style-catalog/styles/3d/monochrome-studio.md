# Monochrome Studio
3D animation
A glossy white consumer device floats in generous negative space over a smooth white-to-gray studio gradient, separating into a symmetrical vertical stack with tiny tracked uppercase callouts.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Backdrop A | ≈#FFFFFF | Pure studio highlight white |
| Backdrop B | ≈#D4D7DC | Soft cool-neutral studio gradient gray |
| Shell | ≈#F7F8FA | Glossy molded white product exterior |
| Internals | ≈#16181C | Tiny deep black contrast solely on chips and drivers |

## Materials & texture
- Hyper-clean commercial product shaders: glossy pearl-white molded plastic with soft studio reflections, matte white silicone tips, and fine dark silicon micro-chips.
- Soft ambient studio shadows with zero environmental grit or surface clutter.

## Typography
- Tiny, elegant wide-tracked uppercase sans-serif callouts paired with hairline pointers (`"03 CLEAN HOUSING"`, `"05 FINE TOLERANCE"`).
- Closing brand/tagline card centered over pure negative space with wide letter-spacing (`"MODEL 01: SOUND, REFINED."`).
- Exception to 3D family rule: zero voiceover and zero subtitle pills; quiet negative space and minimal labels convey luxury.

## Composition
- Single product centered vertically in generous breathing room (50%+ empty studio gradient around the subject).
- Vertical axial exploded view: components separate straight up and down in a crisp column (silicone tip -> acoustic driver -> chip -> battery -> outer case), then reunite smoothly.

## Mood
Clinical luxury, calm engineering precision, unhurried minimalism.

## Signature elements
- Near-monochrome white and soft gray studio palette punctuated only by tiny black internal chips and drivers.
- Smooth infinite white-to-gray studio cyclorama with zero external environment.
- Symmetrical vertical exploded-view stack hovering along one clean central axis.
- Tiny numbered uppercase callouts with hairline leaders floating inside wide negative space.
- Silent, narration-free pacing driven by slow precision motion and a wide-tracked closing wordmark card.

## Recipe
1. Build an unbroken vertical studio gradient backdrop transitioning smoothly from white (`≈#FFFFFF`) to soft gray (`≈#D4D7DC`).
2. Shade the product shell in glossy white (`≈#F7F8FA`, subtle clearcoat, soft area-light highlights) and restrict deep black (`≈#16181C`) strictly to internal micro-components.
3. Animate a slow vertical exploded disassembly along the Y-axis over 4 seconds so each layer hovers evenly spaced, leaving 50% or more open frame margin.
4. Connect `1px` light-gray leader lines (`≈#8E949E`) to `11px` wide-tracked (`letter-spacing: 0.25em`) numbered uppercase labels.
5. Reassemble the shell smoothly and end on a centered wide-tracked uppercase tagline slate with zero subtitles.

## Don'ts
- Do not add rooms, wooden tables, nature, or any external props to the studio void.
- Do not introduce bright saturated colors, yellow highlights, or red accents.
- Do not add voiceover narration or bottom subtitle pills.
- Do not crowd the frame with big bold headlines; keep type small, airy, and wide-tracked.

## One-frame test
- [ ] A glossy white precision device floats against an unbroken white-to-gray studio gradient with generous negative space.
- [ ] The only dark contrast appears in tiny internal electronics or hairline typography.
- [ ] Small wide-tracked numbered uppercase labels sit beside a clean vertical exploded stack without subtitles.
