# Cutaway Guide
3D animation
Clean low-poly 3D tropical structures sit on bright sunlit grass with soil cross-sections, thin white pointer-line technical labels, and a tiny near-faceless guide figure.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Grass | ≈#63B847 | Bright sunlit tropical meadow green |
| Sky | ≈#72BBE8 | Clear daytime sky blue |
| Earth | ≈#7D5438 | Cutaway soil and foundation earth brown |
| Straw | ≈#E6BE53 | Warm golden thatched roof yellow |
| Label | ≈#FFFFFF | Crisp white leader lines, diagrams, and subtitles |

## Materials & texture
- Clean low-poly faceted forms paired with instantly readable natural materials: woven bamboo wall patterns and textured straw thatch.
- Matte soil strata slices beneath the ground plane showing distinct rock and dirt layers.
- Bright, crisp daylight shading with clean soft shadows.

## Typography
- Small crisp white sans-serif technical labels connected to structural parts by thin white leader lines (`"Post"`, `"Footstone"`, `"Diagonal brace"` alongside clean white triangle geometry overlays).
- Exception to 3D family rule: plain white unboxed bottom subtitles (`22px` sans, subtle drop shadow, no dark pill box).

## Composition
- Centered hero structure elevated slightly or sliced open at the base to expose subterranean footings in a clean soil cutaway block.
- One miniature guide figure stands beside the structure purely to give human scale and directional focus.
- Step-by-step camera cuts isolate one structural component per beat.

## Mood
Clear, sunny, instructive, driven by problem-and-solution curiosity.

## Signature elements
- Architectural and soil cross-section cutaway revealing hidden underground layers and joints.
- Thin white pointer lines with crisp structural terminology and geometric vector overlays (e.g., white force triangles).
- Bright low-poly tropical daylight palette blending woven bamboo and straw textures.
- Tiny near-faceless guide archetype wearing a wide conical woven hat, serving as scale indicator rather than character drama.
- Plain unboxed white subtitles paired with a twist-reveal structure ("looks safe at first, until...").

## Recipe
1. Build a clean low-poly structure on a rectangular terrain slab lit by bright 5500K midday sun (`≈#72BBE8` sky, `≈#63B847` grass).
2. Slice the foreground edge of the ground slab vertically to expose layered brown soil (`≈#7D5438`) and foundation piers.
3. Place one small scale figure in a conical hat beside the structure with minimal blank facial geometry.
4. Animate `1.5px` white leader lines (`≈#FFFFFF`) extending `40px` to `80px` from each dissected layer to a crisp label and optional geometric triangle diagram.
5. Render bottom narration as plain unboxed white text with a soft `2px` drop shadow instead of a dark pill container.

## Don'ts
- Do not add dark semi-transparent pill boxes behind the bottom subtitles.
- Do not use dark moody night lighting or hyper-detailed photoreal grime.
- Do not give the guide figure exaggerated cartoon close-ups or complex emotional plots.
- Do not omit the cross-section cutaway or pointer-line labels.

## One-frame test
- [ ] A clean low-poly structure reveals underground or interior layers via a crisp cross-section slice.
- [ ] Thin white pointer lines and geometric overlays label individual engineering parts.
- [ ] Bright tropical daylight lights the scene alongside plain unboxed white bottom subtitles.

## Base prompt
Clean low-poly 3D technical explainer in bright tropical daylight, sunlit green grass (≈#63B847), clear blue sky (≈#72BBE8), and a rectangular terrain block sliced open to reveal brown soil layers (≈#7D5438) beneath a woven bamboo and yellow straw-roofed stilt structure (≈#E6BE53), tiny near-faceless guide figure in a wide conical hat for scale, crisp thin white leader-line labels and geometric triangle diagrams pointing to joints and footings, plain unboxed white subtitles, clear instructive tone.
