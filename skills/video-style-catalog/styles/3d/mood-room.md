# Mood Room
3D animation
Smooth feature-film 3D character animation inside a single bedroom whose ambient color shifts from calm blue to anxious purple-pink while glowing glass thought orbs float beside the character.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Calm Wall | ≈#4A7094 | Soft slate-blue bedroom backdrop |
| Calm Accent | ≈#2E6F7E | Teal headboard and furniture |
| Anxious Shift | ≈#A34B8C | Purple-pink ambient takeover during worry beats |
| Character | ≈#F4C542 | Bright yellow casual shirt against cool walls |
| Warm Lamp | ≈#FFD580 | Soft bedside practical lamp glow |

## Materials & texture
- Smooth stylized feature-animation shaders: soft subsurface skin, woven matte cotton fabrics, and subtle hair strands.
- Translucent glossy glass spheres for hovering thought bubbles containing miniature 3D vignettes.
- Soft studio-style rim and key lighting that blends smoothly during color transitions.

## Typography
- Standard 3D family subtitle pill centered at bottom (`rgba(15, 18, 26, 0.78)` rounded box, `20px` radius).
- Crisp white sans-serif text with key emotional words highlighted in bright yellow (`≈#FFD43B`).
- No floating header banners or technical callouts.

## Composition
- Locked to a single bedroom interior throughout the piece.
- Relies heavily on tight close-ups and medium close-ups of the character's face to read subtle brow and eye shifts.
- Glowing spherical thought bubbles float in shallow depth-of-field pockets near shoulder or head level.

## Mood
Intimate, emotionally responsive, moving gently between quiet worry and grounded calm.

## Signature elements
- Single-location lighting shift where walls turn from calm blue to anxious purple-pink to mirror internal emotion.
- Floating translucent glowing orbs containing miniature 3D scenes inside them (e.g., figures meeting, a ticking clock, a falling silhouette).
- Expressive smooth-shaded character archetype (rounded stylized proportions, dark hair, yellow shirt, detailed eyes and brows).
- Dark semi-transparent bottom subtitle pill with yellow keyword highlights.
- Shallow cinematic depth of field isolating facial expression against soft room bokeh.

## Recipe
1. Set up one cohesive bedroom set with a teal headboard (`≈#2E6F7E`), soft blue walls (`≈#4A7094`), and a warm bedside lamp (`≈#FFD580`).
2. Frame the subject at 50mm to 85mm close-up so facial micro-expressions fill 40% of frame height.
3. Keyframe global room fill light over 2.5s from calm blue (`≈#4A7094`) to anxious purple-pink (`≈#A34B8C`) as tension rises.
4. Spawn 2 to 3 hovering glass spheres (IOR 1.45, soft outer glow, `12%` opacity rim) holding miniature animated 3D vignettes.
5. Place the bottom dark pill subtitle (`78%` opacity) with yellow highlighted keywords (`≈#FFD43B`) timed cleanly to beats.

## Don'ts
- Do not switch locations or cut out of the single bedroom set.
- Do not use low-poly faceted faces or flat 2D outlines that flatten facial expression.
- Do not use harsh thriller contrast or stark neon signage.
- Do not clutter the screen with UI bars, graphs, or multi-panel grids.

- [ ] Glowing translucent thought spheres containing miniature scenes float near the character.
- [ ] The single bedroom's ambient palette clearly signals calm blue or anxious purple-pink.
