# Social Sim
3D animation
Bright low-poly 3D worlds grow live on screen while floating social-media comment cards, rising population counters, and construction progress bars act directly inside the scene.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | ≈#55C947 | Vivid bright sunlit meadow green |
| Sky | ≈#87CEFA | Clear cheerful light sky blue |
| UI Alert | ≈#E63232 | Crisp notification red for counters and status badges |
| UI Card | ≈#FFFFFF | Clean white social comment cards and progress panels |
| Prop | ≈#F28B30 | Warm bright orange tents and starter props |

## Materials & texture
- Crisp low-poly faceted 3D geometry with bright, clean matte-to-satin shading and soft daytime shadows.
- Flat, high-contrast 2D UI cards (`12px` rounded corners, crisp drop shadows) layered directly over 3D structures as part of the world.

## Typography
- Bold geometric UI sans-serif throughout headers, counters, and cards.
- Persistent top banner (`"DAY 1: COMMENT TOWN"`), oversized live numerals (`"POPULATION 1"` climbing to `"12,592"`), and red status pills (`"FOLLOW = CITIZEN"`, `"TOP COMMENT = BUILD"`).
- Floating comment cards show handle, timestamp, request line, and heart count (`"@viewer_01 · 2h · build a gazebo! ♥ 4,812"`), paired with progress bars (`"BUILDING GAZEBO 58%"`) and world signposts (`"CITIZEN No. 003 @viewer_02"`).

## Composition
- Starts on a wide open green field with a single bright orange tent and one tiny leader figure.
- Structures, houses, and citizen markers pop in dynamically across the field as numbers tick upward.
- UI panels hover anchored cleanly over corresponding 3D build zones rather than hiding in corners.

## Mood
Playful, interactive, gamified community-building energy.

## Signature elements
- Social-media interface fused directly into the 3D world as plot mechanics rather than passive decoration.
- Persistent top header and rapidly ticking population counter driving visual town expansion.
- Chibi town-leader archetype (oversized head, compact body, stylized formal cap and patterned tunic, earnest wide-eyed expression).
- Floating viewer comment cards with heart counts linked to active 3D construction progress bars.
- Interactive cliffhanger prompt closing each episode (`"What should we build next? COMMENT!"`).

## Recipe
1. Build a flat low-poly green field (`≈#55C947`) beneath a light blue sky (`≈#87CEFA`) with one bright orange starter prop (`≈#F28B30`).
2. Place a persistent top UI bar (`24px` bold sans) with a red badge (`≈#E63232`) and an animated rolling population counter.
3. Pop in white rounded comment cards (`≈#FFFFFF`, `14px` radius, `8px` shadow) directly above empty build plots with spring-scale ease.
4. Animate new low-poly buildings rising out of the ground alongside horizontal red/white progress bars (`0%` to `100%`).
5. End on a wide overhead shot of the expanded village with a bold call-to-comment prompt banner.

## Don'ts
- Do not use dark moody night lighting, heavy realism, or gritty textures.
- Do not hide or minimize the social UI; the counters and comment cards are core storytelling props.
- Do not omit the direct link between viewer rules (`follow` / `comment`) and visible 3D world changes.
- Do not swap the bright red-and-white notification palette for muted earth tones.

## One-frame test
- [ ] A bright low-poly green town or field sits directly behind crisp red-and-white social UI cards and counters.
- [ ] A live population number, citizen badge, or construction progress bar anchors the action.
- [ ] A small chibi leader figure stands inside the growing game-like diorama.

## Base prompt
Bright cheerful low-poly 3D game-like town simulation on vivid green grass (≈#55C947) under a light blue sky (≈#87CEFA) with an orange starter tent (≈#F28B30), tiny chibi mayor character in formal patterned tunic and cap, crisp red-and-white floating social-media comment cards (≈#FFFFFF, ≈#E63232), rising live population counters, construction progress bars, and numbered citizen signposts hovering directly inside the 3D scene, playful interactive mood.
