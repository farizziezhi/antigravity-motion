# Timeline Pedestal
3D animation
Evolving consumer hardware devices rotate centered on an identical circular white gallery podium above a scrolling 1990-to-2100 timeline scrubber and rigid era tag headers.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Backdrop | ≈#F3EFE7 | Clean warm museum-gallery cream background |
| Podium | ≈#FFFFFF | Crisp circular matte-white display pedestal |
| Accent A | ≈#7ED6B8 | Soft studio mint timeline and era highlight |
| Accent B | ≈#F28C38 | Warm gallery orange scrubber marker |
| Hardware | ≈#2A2E35 | Dark metallic, glass, and matte polymer device tones |

## Materials & texture
- Photorealistic product materials: reflective OLED glass screens, brushed anodized aluminum, and satin matte molded plastics.
- Soft overhead museum spotlighting casting gentle radial shadows onto the circular podium.

## Typography
- Rigidly standardized era header at upper center: oversized light-weight year (`"2010s"`, `"2090"`, `64px` thin sans) stacked over three short uppercase material or feature keywords separated by middle dots (`"TOUCHSCREEN · GLASS · APPS"`).
- Horizontal bottom timeline bar (`1990` to `2100`) with clean tick marks and a sliding orange/mint indicator dot.
- Closing reflection line centered cleanly (`"Technology never stops evolving."`). No standard dark subtitle pill.

## Composition
- Locked, identical gallery camera framing across every era: one circular white podium dead-center, one device per era, active screen graphics illuminated.
- Timeline track anchored horizontally along the lower 12% of the frame.
- Consistent negative space around the podium keeps comparison effortless as hardware shapes morph or swap.

## Mood
Curated, calm, forward-looking museum exhibition.

## Signature elements
- Identical circular white museum podium framing one device per era under uniform soft gallery lighting.
- Persistent horizontal bottom timeline (`1990` to `2100`) with a sliding marker advancing chronologically.
- Strict two-line era header format: thin giant year above three dot-separated uppercase keywords.
- Working in-device screen UIs tailored to each decade (monochrome messages, icon grids, folding/floating concepts).
- Escalation from historical reality into speculative future hardware concepts at the closing tick marks.

## Recipe
1. Lock camera distance and angle onto a centered circular white pedestal (`≈#FFFFFF`) over a warm gallery cream cyclorama (`≈#F3EFE7`).
2. Render each era's hardware model with realistic glass, metal (`≈#2A2E35`), and lit display textures under soft overhead spotlighting.
3. Anchor a horizontal timeline ruler (`1990` to `2100`) along the bottom with a mint track (`≈#7ED6B8`) and moving orange marker (`≈#F28C38`).
4. Lock the top header template identically for every beat: `64px` thin year over `13px` tracked uppercase 3-keyword summary.
5. Advance smoothly through past, present, and speculative future concepts (`2090`, `2100`) before fading to a single closing sentence.

## Don'ts
- Do not change camera framing or podium design between eras; identical framing makes hardware evolution readable.
- Do not clutter the gallery void with rooms, desks, or human hands holding the devices.
- Do not break the rigid year-plus-three-keywords header structure with chatty paragraphs.
- Do not end with a flat summary chart instead of a speculative future concept.

## One-frame test
- [ ] A single realistic device rests on a circular white gallery podium against a warm cream backdrop.
- [ ] A horizontal decade timeline spans the bottom while a thin oversized year plus uppercase keyword line sits above.
- [ ] The device screen glows cleanly with era-appropriate UI details.
