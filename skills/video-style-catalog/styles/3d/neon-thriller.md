# Neon Thriller
3D animation
High-contrast realistic 3D late-night interior bathed in cold blue-gray shadows and slashed by red neon signage, hiding the protagonist's face while in-world receipts and security screens tell the story.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | ≈#1B2A38 | Cold blue-gray night ambient and tile shadows |
| Secondary | ≈#2F4354 | Cool fluorescent interior midtones |
| Accent | ≈#E61E2A | Sharp neon red storefront signage and shirt accent |
| Alert Glow | ≈#D9383A | Glowing smartphone screen and recording dot |
| Practical | ≈#DCE8F0 | Sterile overhead fluorescent tube white |

## Materials & texture
- Realistic physically based materials: glossy ceramic floor tiles, clear glass doors, stocked plastic-and-foil retail shelves.
- Zero cartoon smoothing or toy-like proportions; surfaces carry realistic specular reflections and grime.
- Subtle handheld or security-cam grain on monitor inserts.

## Typography
- In-world diegetic text carries plot evidence: thermal paper receipt lines (`"STORE · PAYMENT OK / 1x PASS / 02:14"`), stark doorway signs (`"STORAGE"`, `"COUNTER"`), and security HUD (`"CAM 02 · REAR EXIT · REC"`).
- Standard 3D family subtitle pill at bottom (`rgba(10, 14, 18, 0.82)` rounded box, white text, keywords in `≈#FFD43B`).

## Composition
- Single night-time retail interior shot in long, deliberate takes with very few cuts.
- Subject framed almost exclusively from behind, in silhouette, or at a distance so the face stays concealed.
- Foreground door frames, aisle shelves, and glass reflections create tense depth layers.

## Mood
Cold, suspenseful, isolated, late-night mystery thriller.

## Signature elements
- Cold blue-gray cinematic lighting cut by one piercing neon red accent.
- Concealed-identity framing showing only the subject's back, shoulders, and long cast shadows.
- Diegetic storytelling through printed receipts, glowing phone displays, and CCTV monitor feeds.
- Realistic glass, tile, and shelf textures creating grounded physical tension.
- Extended lingering shots punctuated by bottom dark subtitle pills with yellow keywords.

## Recipe
1. Light a realistic tiled interior with cool blue-gray overhead fluorescents (`≈#2F4354`, 5600K) against deep shadows (`≈#1B2A38`).
2. Add one sharp neon red sign (`≈#E61E2A`) bleeding across glass doors and wet tile reflections.
3. Position the camera behind the red-shirted figure (35mm lens, slow 4-second push-in), keeping the face turned away or in shadow.
4. Cut briefly to macro close-ups of in-world evidence: a thermal receipt, a lit smartphone screen, or a corner security feed (`REC` blinking at 1Hz).
5. Overlay the dark rounded bottom subtitle pill (`82%` opacity) with yellow highlighted keywords (`≈#FFD43B`).

## Don'ts
- Do not show bright daylight, pastel colors, or cheerful cartoon proportions.
- Do not reveal clear front-facing close-ups of the main character's face.
- Do not use rapid 1-second comedy cuts or floating UI badges.
- Do not replace in-world evidence props with explanatory narrator title cards.

## One-frame test
- [ ] Realistic glass, tile, and shelf surfaces sit inside cold blue-gray night contrast with red neon bleed.
- [ ] Any visible person is framed from behind or obscured in deep shadow.
- [ ] An in-world receipt, phone screen, or security camera feed serves as a story clue.

## Base prompt
Realistic cinematic 3D thriller inside a quiet late-night store, cold blue-gray shadows (≈#1B2A38) and sterile fluorescent reflections cut by bright red neon window signage (≈#E61E2A), glossy tiled floor and glass aisles, lone figure in a red shirt framed strictly from behind with face concealed, long shadows, close-up of a printed thermal receipt or security camera monitor overlay, tense suspenseful atmosphere, bottom dark rounded subtitle pill with white text and yellow keywords.
