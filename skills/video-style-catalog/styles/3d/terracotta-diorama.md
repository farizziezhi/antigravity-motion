# Terracotta Diorama
3D animation
An entire ancient Mediterranean city built as a detailed miniature diorama of terracotta roofs, cream stone colonnades, and aqueducts transitions from sunlit daytime into glowing golden dusk.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | ≈#C9643B | Warm baked terracotta clay roof tiles |
| Stone | ≈#EFE3CE | Sun-bleached cream limestone walls and plazas |
| Sky Day | ≈#6EAED9 | Clear Mediterranean afternoon blue |
| Sunset | ≈#F2A84B | Rich golden-hour dusk sky and warm streetlamp glow |
| Water | ≈#4A90A4 | Clear reflective bathhouse and canal water |

## Materials & texture
- Tactile miniature architectural materials: ridged clay roof tiles, weathered travertine stone, and clear rippling pool water.
- Subtle tilt-shift-friendly scale cues that preserve physical material realism without looking like cheap plastic toys.

## Typography
- Wide-tracked uppercase location title centered cleanly over sweeping panoramas (`"A N C I E N T  C I T Y"`, light-to-regular serif or clean sans).
- Paired architectural callouts combining tracked all-caps noun plus an italic serif subtitle (`"T E N E M E N T  B L O C K: multi-story urban housing"`).
- Exception to 3D family rule: no bottom subtitle pill or chatty UI; minimal architectural titles carry the tour.

## Composition
- Continuous sweeping scale tour planned as a geographic route: high aerial overview -> skimming over terracotta rooftops -> gliding down stone streets and bathhouses -> rising back to aerial panorama.
- Tiny faceless draped citizen figures move through plazas solely to convey architectural scale.

## Mood
Awe-inspiring, sun-warmed historical travelogue with nostalgic golden-hour grandeur.

## Signature elements
- Full-city miniature diorama rendered with realistic stone, clay tile, and water shaders.
- Warm Mediterranean palette shifting deliberately from clear afternoon blue to glowing amber sunset at the finale.
- Wide-tracked uppercase location titles paired with concise italic serif architectural descriptions.
- Tiny faceless draped scale figures populating markets, colonnades, and steps.
- Multi-altitude aerial-to-street-to-aerial camera choreography ending as warm dusk lanterns ignite.

## Recipe
1. Construct a dense interconnected city grid of terracotta roofs (`≈#C9643B`), cream stone columns (`≈#EFE3CE`), aqueduct arches, and water basins (`≈#4A90A4`).
2. Populate avenues with miniature draped figures (`1.8m` scale, blank facial geometry) walking in small groups for kinetic scale.
3. Choreograph one sweeping multi-altitude camera path descending from 200m aerial view down to eye-level courtyard plazas and back up.
4. Display one wide-tracked uppercase label (`letter-spacing: 0.35em`) with a single italic serif subtitle line per landmark beat.
5. Animate solar angle dropping from midday (`≈#6EAED9`) to low golden sunset (`≈#F2A84B`) over the final third as warm point-light lamps glow across the roofs.

## Don'ts
- Do not show modern vehicles, sci-fi tech, or dark neon thriller lighting.
- Do not zoom into close-ups of detailed individual character faces.
- Do not clutter the panorama with multiple overlapping callouts, counters, or dark subtitle pills.
- Do not end under flat midday light; the golden dusk lamp-lighting shift is the emotional climax.

## One-frame test
- [ ] Dense miniature terracotta roofs and cream limestone temples read as a tactile physical diorama.
- [ ] Tiny faceless draped figures walk along stone streets, plazas, or aqueducts for scale.
- [ ] A wide-tracked uppercase title with italic serif subtitle or a warm golden dusk transition anchors the view.
