# Ransom Collage
Motion graphics
Dense paper-cutout tabloid collage with torn white edges, ransom-note cutout typography, halftone dots, and bold verdict rubber stamps.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| turmeric backdrop | ≈#F2B705 | high-contrast scene backdrop (rotates each cut) |
| electric blue | ≈#1E5BFF | alternate full-bleed scene backdrop |
| tabloid magenta | ≈#FF3B8D | alternate bright scene backdrop |
| mint green | ≈#8EE4AF | alternate pastel-bright backdrop |
| kraft & black | ≈#B68D5E | kraft paper cutouts, paired with `≈#141414` black scenes |
| verdict green & red | ≈#1FA65A | `TRUE` stamp, paired with `≈#E03131` `FALSE` stamp |
| marker highlight | ≈#FFE14D | yellow highlight behind handwritten bottom captions |

## Materials & texture
Tactile zine and scrapbook paper collage featuring jagged torn paper edges with white borders, translucent masking tape strips, halftone dot patterns, grey-line newspaper columns, and drop shadows beneath stacked clippings.

## Typography
Mixed ransom-note lettering where each word or letter switches font style, background swatch, and angle (heavy serif, condensed sans, handwritten). Tilted rubber stamps declare `TRUE` (`≈#1FA65A`) or `FALSE` (`≈#E03131`), while bottom captions use black handwritten marker with `≈#FFE14D` highlights.

## Composition
Maximalist, tightly packed, overlapping layers with almost no empty margins. Every piece sits slightly tilted; each scene jump-cuts to a completely different saturated backdrop color.

## Mood
Loud, urgent, tabloid-chaotic, and darkly humorous, like a sensational newspaper wall brought to life.

## Signature elements
- Multi-font, multi-color ransom-note cutout words pasted over mismatched paper swatches.
- Jagged torn paper edges with white borders, adhesive tape strips, and halftone dot shading.
- Tilted `TRUE` (green) and `FALSE` (red) rubber verdict stamps slammed onto clips.
- Full-screen backdrop color flips on every single scene cut.

## Recipe
1. Rotate full-bleed high-contrast backgrounds across every cut (`≈#F2B705`, `≈#1E5BFF`, `≈#FF3B8D`, `≈#8EE4AF`, `≈#141414`).
2. Assemble headlines as ransom notes: vary typeface, tilt, and paper-swatch backing per word or letter, adding white torn borders, tape strips, and halftone dots.
3. Layer newspaper-line cutouts, silhouette photos, cartoon icons, and phone mockups with overlapping drop shadows, leaving minimal empty space.
4. Stamp tilted `TRUE` (`≈#1FA65A`) or `FALSE` (`≈#E03131`) verdict seals on claims and set bottom captions in black handwritten marker with `≈#FFE14D` highlights.
5. Animate cutouts flying rapidly into frame and slam verdict stamps down with a subtle camera shake at fast pacing, one claim per scene.

## Don'ts
- Do not use clean minimal layouts with generous whitespace or uniform typography.
- Do not keep the same background color across consecutive scenes.
- Do not flatten cutouts without torn edges, tape, halftone dots, or layer shadows.

## One-frame test
- Does the frame pack torn paper cutouts, tape, and halftone textures edge-to-edge?
- Do headline words mix different fonts and colored paper swatches like a ransom note?
- Is there a tilted red/green verdict stamp or bright backdrop shift marking the claim?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 chaotic paper-collage motion graphics frame over a saturated electric blue backdrop (#1E5BFF), densely layered with torn white-edged newspaper clippings, halftone dot patches, masking tape strips, a cartoon icon and silhouette cutout with drop shadows, ransom-note headline words mixing serif and condensed sans on colored paper swatches, a tilted red rubber FALSE stamp (#E03131), and a handwritten black marker bottom caption with yellow highlight (#FFE14D).
