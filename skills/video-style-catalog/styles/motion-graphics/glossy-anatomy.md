# Glossy Anatomy
Motion graphics
Glossy pink interior-body world populated by cute uniformed chibi cells under a ceiling of smiling teeth and yellow portal doors.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tissue pink | #F7A8B8 | polka-dot tongue/interior floor and wall surfaces |
| deep palate red | #C94F5E | rich inner-cavity ceiling and shadows |
| outer sky blue | #A8D8EA | bright clean backdrop for exterior human shots |
| flame orange | #FF6B35 | fiery sprites, warning tears, thermal alerts |
| portal yellow | #FFC93C | doors, warning badges, temperature callouts |
| guard blue | #5AA9E6 | uniformed protector character accents |

## Materials & texture
Clean, glossy digital cartoon finish featuring subtle polka-dot surface texture across pink tissue planes, shiny specular highlights, and bold dark cartoon outlines (`≈#1E1216`).

## Typography
Uppercase bold white sans-serif category labels inside square brackets with thick black outlines (`[GUARD]`, `[SIGNAL]`, `[GLAND]`), yellow small callout badges (`HOT!`), and white bottom captions highlighting one keyword inside a crisp red pill (`≈#D93848`).

## Composition
Alternates cleanly between two scales: exterior daylight character shots (`#A8D8EA`) and bustling interior-body village rooms (`#F7A8B8`) framed permanently along the top edge by a row of cheerful smiling white teeth; climax moments punch into an extreme full-frame face close-up with a circular zoom inset returning inside.

## Mood
Playful, endearing, and "gross-cute", turning biological mechanics into a bustling miniature cartoon town.

## Signature elements
- Row of cheerful smiling white teeth framing the upper edge like a bright ceiling.
- Polka-dot `#F7A8B8` pink interior floor/walls contrasted against `#C94F5E` deep red palate shadows.
- Uniformed chibi cell-characters (blue `#5AA9E6` guard officers, `#FF6B35` flame sprites, helmeted gland workers) with thick dark outlines.
- Yellow `#FFC93C` portal doors transitioning scenes and square-bracketed `[TERM]` labels above.

## Recipe
1. Build an interior anatomical village using polka-dot `#F7A8B8` pink surfaces, `#C94F5E` deep red ceilings lined with smiling white teeth along the top edge, and `#A8D8EA` light blue for exterior character shots.
2. Turn biological mechanisms into uniformed chibi characters (blue `#5AA9E6` guards, `#FF6B35` orange sprites, firefighter-helmeted glands) drawn with thick dark outlines and glossy highlights.
3. Place `#FFC93C` yellow doors as scene portals and float flame-shaped tears or tiny tool props (thermometers, milk bottles) for visual storytelling.
4. Set top category tags in bold black-outlined white uppercase inside square brackets (`[TERM]`) and bottom captions in white sans with one keyword inside a red pill (`≈#D93848`).
5. Transition via camera zooms through `#FFC93C` doors and insert one extreme full-screen character face close-up with a circular portal inset at the climax.

## Don'ts
- Do not render gritty medical realism, gruesome organs, or sterile dark lab glass.
- Do not strip away the thick dark cartoon outlines, glossy shine, or polka-dot pink tissue texture.
- Do not forget the upper smiling-teeth ceiling on interior scenes.

## One-frame test
- Does a glossy polka-dot `#F7A8B8` pink world sit beneath smiling white teeth or beside `#FFC93C` doors?
- Are microscopic functions portrayed by thick-outlined costumed chibi characters?
- Is there a bracketed `[TERM]` top label and a red-pill keyword in the bottom caption?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 glossy cute cartoon motion graphics frame set inside a polka-dot pink (#F7A8B8) and deep red (#C94F5E) anatomical village ceilinged along the top by cheerful smiling white teeth, featuring thick-outlined chibi characters including a blue-uniformed guard officer (#5AA9E6) holding a thermometer beside a yellow portal door (#FFC93C) and glowing orange flame sprites (#FF6B35), a bold black-outlined white bracketed [GUARD] tag above, and a white bottom caption with one keyword inside a red pill.
