# Dark Dot Matrix
Motion graphics
Ultra-clean dark data-essay dashboard pairing giant thin white hero numbers with cyan dot matrices, coral collision markers, and lime progress bars.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| night canvas | #0A0E14 to #0D1117 | deep dark slate canvas with subtle dot-grid |
| unit cyan | #4DD0E1 | primary dot entities and active network nodes |
| collision coral | #FF6B5E | matching/colliding pairs and bottom caption underline |
| unique lime | #B8E62E | positive comparison bars (`ALL UNIQUE`) |
| hero white | ≈#FFFFFF | giant thin numerals, thin connecting lines, captions |

## Materials & texture
Razor-sharp, zero-grain digital data-dashboard aesthetic featuring a faint background dot matrix and ultra-thin technical ruler ticks; zero paper texture and zero character art.

## Typography
Light-weight thin sans-serif hero figures at 120 to 200 px (`253`, `>50%`, `02`) anchoring the upper left, paired with tiny uppercase monospace headers, legends, and small inline formulas (`[A × B ÷ 2]`). Bottom captions sit in clean white sans-serif with a single `#FF6B5E` red underline beneath one keyword.

## Composition
Strict unchanging dashboard grid across every scene: thin mono header and legend along the top, giant thin white figure at upper left, dot-matrix/network/bar visualization centered cleanly in the middle, and mono footer plus red-underlined caption at the bottom.

## Mood
Cool, analytical, elegant, and quietly intriguing, like a high-craft statistical essay.

## Signature elements
- Strict three-accent palette (`#4DD0E1`, `#FF6B5E`, `#B8E62E`) over `#0A0E14`–`#0D1117` dot-grid darkness.
- Giant light-weight sans-serif hero figures (120 to 200 px) paired with tiny inline math equations.
- Numbered `#4DD0E1` cyan dots (`01`, `02`, `03`) acting as the sole "actors" across grids, histograms, and networks.
- Pure white sans-serif bottom caption with a single `#FF6B5E` coral underline.

## Recipe
1. Fill the canvas with `#0A0E14` to `#0D1117` overlaid with a faint dot-matrix grid and fine ruler lines; avoid any character art or decorative flourishes.
2. Lock every scene to one dashboard template: small mono header and dot legend on top, 120 to 200 px thin white hero number at upper left with a tiny math formula beside it, and centered data visualization.
3. Enforce the three-accent rule strictly: `#4DD0E1` for individual numbered dots, `#FF6B5E` for colliding/matching dots and links, and `#B8E62E` for full-difference bars.
4. Set bottom captions in clean white sans-serif with one keyword underlined in `#FF6B5E` coral.
5. Animate through live counting, sequential dot pop-ins, filling comparison bars, and crisp hard cuts between analytical chapters.

## Don'ts
- Do not add human characters, cartoon icons, gradients, or ornamental textures.
- Do not introduce a fourth accent color outside cyan, coral red, and lime green.
- Do not break the fixed header-hero-chart-footer dashboard template.

## One-frame test
- Does the frame stay strictly within `#0A0E14`–`#0D1117` plus cyan, coral, lime, and white?
- Are numbered cyan dots and a 120 to 200 px thin white figure the entire visual cast?
- Does the bottom caption feature a single `#FF6B5E` underline without a heavy pill container?

Vertical 9:16 minimalist dark data-essay motion graphics dashboard on #0A0E14 slate with a faint background dot grid, showing a huge 160 px thin white sans-serif hero number at upper left beside a tiny monospace formula, a centered network visualization of numbered cyan (#4DD0E1) dots with two connected coral-red (#FF6B5E) matching nodes and a lime-green (#B8E62E) progress bar, small uppercase monospace header and dot legend above, and a clean white sans bottom caption with one coral-red underlined word.
