# Retro Tape Duel
Motion graphics
70s television game-show versus battle wrapped in a permanent camcorder PLAY overlay, scanlines, CRT blur, and a live scoreboard.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deep studio blue | ≈#14214D | primary dark-blue background after the split intro |
| red faction | ≈#E0352B | Side A diagonal split, icons, and bars |
| blue faction | ≈#2667FF | Side B diagonal split, icons, and comparison bars |
| warm accent | ≈#FFA62B | orange-yellow highlights paired with `≈#FFD43B` yellow titles |
| data panel black | ≈#111111 | crisp black-and-white stat pills and comparison tables |
| broadcast white | ≈#FFFFFF | uppercase condensed headers and camcorder HUD text |

## Materials & texture
Authentic analog CRT television tape recording featuring horizontal scanlines, subtle film noise/grain, dark edge vignetting, and slight optical softness across thick-outlined flat retro props.

## Typography
Heavy condensed uppercase sans-serif display titles with hard drop shadows in white or yellow (`≈#FFD43B`) evoking 1970s print posters. Permanent monospace camcorder HUD text (`PLAY ▶ SP 1:02:14` top left, channel `9` top right, `YEAR 197X 21:04:21` bottom left) frames every scene alongside black-and-white table pills.

## Composition
Permanent camcorder TV frame hugs the outer edges of every shot while the middle hosts the head-to-head duel (red vs blue diagonal split intro, followed by round titles above and horizontal comparison bars or stacked props below). A live two-side scoreboard (`SIDE A 0  SIDE B 1`) sits at top right once rounds begin.

## Mood
Nostalgic 1970s broadcast rivalry, playful and competitive like a retro TV trivia showdown.

## Signature elements
- Permanent analog camcorder HUD (`PLAY ▶ SP`, timecode, channel number) plus CRT scanlines, grain, and vignette.
- Two-tone red (`≈#E0352B`) vs blue (`≈#2667FF`) diagonal split screen opening the duel.
- Live top-right versus scoreboard incrementing across three structured rounds.
- Thick-outlined flat retro cassette/hardware props and black-and-white horizontal bar tables.

## Recipe
1. Lock a persistent CRT overlay over the entire canvas: horizontal scanlines, subtle noise grain, dark edge vignette, and monospace HUD (`PLAY ▶ SP 1:02:14`, channel `9`, bottom-left timecode).
2. Open on a red (`≈#E0352B`) vs blue (`≈#2667FF`) diagonal split, then shift to deep blue (`≈#14214D`) with `≈#FFA62B` and `≈#FFD43B` accents.
3. Draw simple retro props (two-reel cassettes, shelves, player buttons) with bold dark outlines and structure data inside `≈#111111` black-and-white table pills.
4. Set round headers in heavy condensed uppercase sans with drop shadows and track scores (`A 0 | B 1`) in the top right from round one onward.
5. Animate bars growing horizontally, props popping cleanly in, and scoreboard digits flipping across a three-round progression.

## Don'ts
- Do not remove the scanlines, vignette, or PLAY/timecode camcorder HUD for clean digital frames.
- Do not use sleek modern rounded glassmorphism or hand-drawn pencil sketches.
- Do not introduce a third competing team color outside the red-vs-blue duel framing.

## One-frame test
- Are CRT scanlines, grain, dark edge vignette, PLAY marker, and timecode visible?
- Does the scene pit a red faction against a blue faction with bold shadowed condensed titles?
- Are round labels, black-and-white data pills, or a top-right versus scoreboard present?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 retro 1970s analog tape television motion graphics frame with CRT scanlines, subtle film grain, dark vignette, and permanent monospace camcorder HUD (PLAY SP, channel 9, bottom-left timecode) over deep blue (#14214D), showing a red (#E0352B) versus blue (#2667FF) retro two-reel cassette duel with bold dark outlines, horizontal comparison bar tables in black-and-white pills, a top-right scoreboard, and heavy condensed uppercase yellow (#FFD43B) shadowed round title.
