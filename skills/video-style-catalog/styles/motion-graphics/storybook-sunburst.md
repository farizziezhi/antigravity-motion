# Storybook Sunburst
Motion graphics
Bright daylight children's-book chibi landscapes with marching repeated-head crowds, gold-trimmed red name ribbons, and heroic sunburst reveals.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| day sky | #7EC8F7 | bright clear sky filling the upper half of exterior scenes |
| hill green | #A8D88A | rolling flat grass slopes |
| warm sand | #F2D8A0 | beaches, courtyards, and stone paths |
| brick crimson | #B03A2E | ornate gates, ribbons, and opposing robes |
| royal gold | #F5B942 | radial sunburst backdrops, crowns, ribbon borders |
| sea & ink | ≈#3B82C4 | deep sea blue, paired with `≈#1A1A1A` cartoon outlines |

## Materials & texture
Flat clean children's-storybook vector art with zero paper grain and zero gradients outside the dramatic radial `#F5B942` gold sunburst reserved for heroic character introductions.

## Typography
Bold outlined sans-serif display titles with drop shadows, paired with `#B03A2E` red-and-gold ribbons for character or faction names, cream pills (`≈#F7EEDB`) for time markers (`YEARS EARLIER`), and white comic speech bubbles for one-word exclamations. Bottom captions use bold black-outlined sans with colored keywords (`#F5B942` or `#B03A2E`).

## Composition
Full-bleed panoramic landscape split cleanly across the middle (sky top half, rolling land or ocean bottom half), anchoring the hero chibi figure in the lower center, ribbon or title above, and bold outlined captions at the bottom; heroic reveals switch to a full radial gold sunburst backdrop.

## Mood
Playful epic: sweeping scale told with the charm and light humor of an illustrated storybook.

## Signature elements
- Crowd scale depicted as tightly packed rows of repeated round chibi heads rather than full bodies.
- Brick-red `#B03A2E` name banners trimmed in `#F5B942` gold.
- Full-bleed radial `#F5B942` gold sunburst backdrop reserved solely for hero introductions or climax moments.
- Soft rounded white clouds recurring across every bright `#7EC8F7` exterior sky.

## Recipe
1. Paint flat bright daylight landscapes (`#7EC8F7` sky top half, `#A8D88A` hills, `#F2D8A0` sand, `≈#3B82C4` sea) with zero textures or gradients, repeating puffy white clouds across outdoor scenes.
2. Draw big-headed dot-eyed chibi protagonists in lower-center position and iconic flat props (brick gates, wooden ships, hourglasses, thrones).
3. Depict large armies or crowds as neat overlapping rows of repeated tiny round heads to signal scale efficiently.
4. Label characters and realms on `#B03A2E` red ribbons edged in `#F5B942` gold, mark time jumps with cream pills, and use bold black-outlined sans bottom captions with colored keywords.
5. Reserve full-screen radial `#F5B942` sunburst frames strictly for character introductions or dramatic turning points.

## Don'ts
- Do not render shaded 3D volumes, gritty textures, or dark moody lighting across regular scenes.
- Do not draw every background crowd member with full articulated limbs.
- Do not overuse the radial gold sunburst on routine exposition frames.

## One-frame test
- Does the scene split cleanly into bright `#7EC8F7` daylight sky above and `#A8D88A`/`#F2D8A0` flat terrain below?
- Are characters big-headed chibis, with large groups shown as rows of repeated heads?
- Is there a gold-trimmed red name ribbon or radial `#F5B942` sunburst framing a dramatic beat?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 bright flat children's-book motion graphics frame split evenly between clear blue sky (#7EC8F7) with rounded white clouds above and green hills (#A8D88A) and warm sand (#F2D8A0) below, featuring a cute big-headed dot-eyed chibi hero in ornate patterned attire standing in front of an army rendered as dense rows of repeated tiny chibi heads, a brick-red (#B03A2E) name ribbon bordered in gold (#F5B942), a white comic speech bubble, and bold black-outlined bottom captions with one gold highlighted word.
