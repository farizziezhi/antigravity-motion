# Notebook Sketch
Motion graphics
Wobbly black hand-drawn stick figures and doodles sketched onto cream ruled notebook paper with yellow highlighter strokes.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| notebook paper | #F7F3E6 | cream lined page base across every scene |
| highlighter yellow | #FFE14D | behind titles, caption keywords, and translucent cones |
| ruled lines | ≈#A7C7E7 | horizontal blue notebook lines |
| margin line | ≈#E88B98 | single vertical pink-red margin rule on the left |
| ink black | ≈#1E1E1E | wobbly hand-drawn outlines and text |
| spot accents | ≈#6BAED6 | light blue fills, paired with `≈#FF7B54` orange-pink numbers |

## Materials & texture
Ruled school-notebook page texture with horizontal blue lines and a left pink vertical margin, paired with a colored masking-tape tag at top right. All strokes mimic slightly imperfect ink markers.

## Typography
Bold casual handwritten titles backed by broad `#FFE14D` highlighter swipes, paired with small clean sans-serif bottom captions with `#FFE14D` keyword highlights. A category label sits on a colored tape strip (`≈#7BC96F`) in the top right corner, and a tiny page number (`p. 1` to `5`) sits at bottom right.

## Composition
Loose, airy notebook layout with one or two hand-drawn scenes or simple charts per page and ample cream margin space, arranged sequentially like live lecture notes.

## Mood
Warm, candid, and self-deprecating, like a friend sketching an explanation in a personal notebook.

## Signature elements
- Cream `#F7F3E6` ruled notebook background with blue horizontal lines and a pink left margin.
- Repeated translucent `#FFE14D` spotlight/highlight cone (~35% opacity) anchoring scenes visually.
- Simple stick figures with round heads, dot eyes, curved mouths, and small whisper/thought bubbles.
- Colored tape category badge at top right and small page counter at bottom right.

## Recipe
1. Build a `#F7F3E6` notebook canvas with `≈#A7C7E7` horizontal rules and a left `≈#E88B98` vertical margin; never use a plain flat background.
2. Draw props and stick figures in 2 to 3 px slightly wobbly `≈#1E1E1E` ink lines, limiting fills to sparse spot accents (`≈#6BAED6` clothing, `≈#FF7B54` numbers).
3. Anchor scenes with a recurring translucent `#FFE14D` geometric cone at ~35% opacity as the visual metaphor.
4. Add thick handwritten headings over `#FFE14D` highlighter strokes, a taped category tag at top right, tiny `p. N` numbering at bottom right, and small sans captions with `#FFE14D` keyword highlights.
5. Animate elements drawing on sequentially with rough stroke ends, letting each doodle finish before sliding or turning to the next page.

## Don'ts
- Do not use a plain unlined background or sleek geometric vector precision.
- Do not fully color-fill scenes or add complex anatomical character details.
- Do not rush cuts before the draw-on sketch animation settles.

## One-frame test
- Are the `#F7F3E6` horizontal blue rules and left pink margin clearly visible?
- Do figures use minimal wobbly 2 to 3 px stick-figure ink strokes with `#FFE14D` highlighter accents?
- Is there a top-right tape label, bottom-right page number, and generous empty paper space?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 hand-drawn notebook sketch motion graphics frame on cream ruled paper (#F7F3E6) with blue horizontal lines (#A7C7E7) and a pink vertical left margin (#E88B98), featuring minimal wobbly 2 px black ink stick figures and simple doodles, a translucent yellow spotlight cone at 35% opacity (#FFE14D), a bold handwritten title backed by yellow highlighter, a green tape category tag at top right, tiny page numbering at bottom right, and a small sans bottom caption with one yellow highlighted word.
