# Shadow Puppet
Motion graphics
Perforated black shadow-puppet silhouettes staged over glowing amber dusk gradients with nailed cream wood title plaques.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| amber sunset sky | #E8963C to #C96A1E | warm radial glow behind the center stage |
| deep dusk vignette | #3A1F0D | dark brown perimeter framing the radial light |
| puppet silhouette | ≈#0D0907 | perforated black figures, mountain layers, ground floor |
| wood plaque cream | ≈#F0DFC2 | rounded pill plaques for titles and bottom captions |
| carved serif ink | ≈#A63A24 | brick-red/brown lettering and highlighted keywords |
| cool storm break | ≈#1C2636 | dark desaturated blue-grey for dramatic rain pauses |

## Materials & texture
Traditional shadow-puppet theater feel with fine regular dot perforations punched through dark leather-like silhouettes, backlit by a warm radial lamp glow and anchored by cream wooden label boards with tiny rivets at both ends.

## Typography
Classic carved-feeling serif text in brick red-brown (`≈#A63A24`) inside elongated cream pill plaques (`≈#F0DFC2`) pinned with two end rivets. Bottom captions share the exact same cream wood pill with red keyword highlights, while a small top-right pill (`PART 1` / `PART 2` or `MYTH` / `SCIENCE`) marks narrative shifts.

## Composition
Symmetrical horizontal puppet stage: at least 40% open glowing dusk sky above, layered triangular mountain ranges across the middle, and perforated figures standing on a dark floor strip above the bottom caption pill.

## Mood
Sacred, storybook, and gently melancholic, shifting from mythic warmth to cool stillness during storm breaks or scientific reveals.

## Signature elements
- Regular geometric dot-perforation patterns across every silhouette figure and mountain layer.
- Thin puppet control rods visible beneath geometric black character silhouettes.
- Elongated cream wood pill plaques with twin end rivets for titles and captions.
- Oversized glowing sun disc behind silhouettes during closing or analytical acts.

## Recipe
1. Paint a warm radial gradient from `#E8963C`–`#C96A1E` center glow out to a heavy `#3A1F0D` vignette, keeping at least 40% of the frame open sky.
2. Construct geometric faceless figures and layered triangular mountains in `≈#0D0907` silhouette filled with uniform perforation dot patterns, adding thin control rods under characters.
3. Place titles and bottom captions inside rounded `≈#F0DFC2` wood pills with end rivets and `≈#A63A24` serif text, plus a small top-right chapter pill.
4. Insert one dark cool storm scene (`≈#1C2636`) and one title-divider frame as narrative punctuation before closing on a choice prompt.
5. Animate with gentle lateral puppet glides, subtle sways, multi-layer mountain parallax, soft dissolves, and unhurried 7 to 9 second tableaux.

## Don'ts
- Do not render facial features, bright cartoon outlines, or glossy digital plastic surfaces.
- Do not crowd the upper sky below 40% open space.
- Do not cut rapidly between scenes or use bouncy modern UI transitions.

## One-frame test
- Do the black silhouettes show regular dot perforations and puppet control rods?
- Is the stage backlit by a warm `#E8963C` to `#3A1F0D` radial glow with >= 40% open sky?
- Are both titles and captions set in riveted cream wood pill plaques with brick-red serif type?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 shadow-puppet theater motion graphics frame backlit by a warm amber radial glow (#E8963C) fading into a deep brown vignette (#3A1F0D), showing perforated black silhouettes of layered triangular mountains and a faceless geometric puppet figure on a thin control rod, open dusk sky above, a riveted cream wood pill title plaque with brick-red serif text, a small top-right chapter pill, and a matching bottom caption plaque with a red highlighted word.
