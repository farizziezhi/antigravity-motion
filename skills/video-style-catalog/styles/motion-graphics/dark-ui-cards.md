# Dark UI Cards
Motion graphics
Sleek dark-navy app-interface simulation pairing floating white shadow cards, yellow donut meters, and oversized numbers with a black-and-gold bottom pill.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deep dark navy | #0B1030 to #141B3D | primary canvas (also `#0A0F2E` in strict dark runs) |
| simulation gold | #FFC531 | donut rings, emphasized display words, caption highlights, top tag |
| panic coral | #FF5A4E | emergency buttons, tilted alert stamps, `HARDEST` badges |
| UI card white | ≈#FFFFFF | floating shadow cards, primary bold titles, watermark text |
| optional break cream | ≈#F5EFE2 | light UI chapter-break cards (plus `≈#5B2C82`–`#FF6F61` dawn intro) |
| arched door blue | ≈#2A4FA8 | stylized three-door choice motif with gold handles |

## Materials & texture
Clean, sharp modern mobile-UI flat aesthetic with soft drop shadows beneath elevated white notification/status cards and zero paper or analog grain. Optional subtle 5 to 10% opacity gigantic numeric watermarks sit behind strictly dark scenes.

## Typography
Strict three-tier UI hierarchy: oversized cream/white sans or mono digital clocks and donut percentages (60 to 90 px), bold white sans headlines scaling up single `#FFC531` yellow or `#FF5A4E` red punchline words, and wide-tracked small uppercase monospace tags (`● SERIES TAG` top left, `WHAT IF · SIMULATION` in `#FFC531` top right). Bottom captions sit in a dark pill with `#FFC531` keyword highlights.

## Composition
One bold centered UI element per dark navy screen (a lock-screen clock, floating notification stack, yellow donut gauge, three blue arched doors, or dual red-vs-navy choice buttons) above the consistent dark-and-gold bottom caption pill. Allowed variation across episodes: either open with a brief purple-magenta-orange dawn gradient and insert 1 to 2 contrasting light-cream (`≈#F5EFE2`) UI break cards to pivot chapters, or stay strictly dark navy (`#0A0F2E`) end-to-end backed by 5 to 10% opacity giant percentage watermarks and dense multicolor dot-crowds.

## Mood
Suspenseful, contemplative, and darkly witty, like watching a slick social simulation score your private decisions in real time.

## Signature elements
- Deep navy (`#0B1030`–`#141B3D` / `#0A0F2E`) UI canvas with wide-tracked mono top-corner series badges.
- Elevated white shadow UI cards, lock-screen clocks, notification stacks, or `#FFC531` bold yellow donut rings (60 to 90 px white percentages inside).
- Tilted `#FF5A4E` red warning stamps (`DETECTED`) and oversized punchline buttons (`PANIC`, `CHOOSE`).
- Massive multicolor faceless dot-crowds or three `≈#2A4FA8` arched choice doors with gold handles.

## Recipe
1. Set a full `#0B1030`–`#141B3D` (or `#0A0F2E`) navy background, optionally layering 5 to 10% opacity giant numeric watermarks or inserting 1 to 2 light-cream UI break cards for dynamic chapter contrast.
2. Lock wide-tracked uppercase mono badges across the top corners (`● [TAG]` left, `#FFC531` `[MODE]` right) and a dark caption pill with `#FFC531` keyword highlighting at the bottom.
3. Build scenes strictly from crisp UI components (shadowed white cards, lock clocks, `#FFC531` progress donuts, pill buttons, dot-crowd grids, three blue arched doors) rather than scenic landscapes or detailed portraits.
4. Emphasize punchlines by scaling single headline words dramatically in `#FFC531` or `#FF5A4E` and dropping tilted red stamps with brief shake.
5. Animate cards with layered slide-fades, fill donut arcs, count numbers up/down, stagger dot-crowds, and hold closing choice buttons or punchlines for 1 to 2 seconds.

## Don'ts
- Do not draw full realistic human characters, scenic outdoor backgrounds, or hand-drawn textures.
- Do not swap the `#FFC531` yellow and `#FF5A4E` red UI accents for pastel rainbow palettes.
- Do not clutter a single screen with multiple competing UI modules at once.

## One-frame test
- Does a centered elevated UI module (card, clock, yellow donut, or choice buttons) float over `#0B1030`–`#0A0F2E` navy?
- Are wide-tracked uppercase mono top badges and a gold-highlighted dark bottom pill present?
- Are human subjects shown strictly as circular avatars, faceless dot-crowds, or absent altogether?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 sleek dark UI-simulation motion graphics frame on deep dark navy (#0B1030) with faint 8% opacity oversized percentage watermarks in the background, centering an elevated crisp white shadow UI card alongside a bold yellow (#FFC531) donut progress ring framing a large 75 px white sans percentage and a coral-red (#FF5A4E) tilted pill button, wide-tracked small uppercase monospace corner badges at the top, and a dark bottom caption pill with one gold (#FFC531) highlighted keyword.
