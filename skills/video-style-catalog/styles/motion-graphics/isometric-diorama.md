# Isometric Diorama
Motion graphics
Clean 30-degree pastel isometric city and floating island dioramas populated by tiny faceless pawn-like figures under bold navy titles and black-gold caption pills.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| soft day sky | ≈#9ED8FA | primary bright sky (pairable with `≈#F5EFE2` cream or `≈#FFD53D` breaks) |
| pastel architecture | ≈#F7B2BD | pink buildings, paired with `≈#9CC8F7` blue, `≈#FFE28A` yellow, `≈#C7B8EA` lavender |
| diorama ground | ≈#9BE078 | light green grass, `≈#4A505C` dark grey roads, `≈#8C5E3C` brown island earth |
| alert & score | ≈#FF5A4E | coral-red tags/low-score bubbles, paired with `≈#FFC531` high-score gold |
| dramatic night sky | ≈#101A3B | dark navy sky for mid-story twists or final act endings |
| black caption pill | ≈#141414 | persistent black pills and role badges with gold `#FFC531` keywords |

## Materials & texture
Smooth, toy-like 3D-rendered-look isometric vector art at a ~30-degree angle, with soft ambient shadows, clean matte-smooth surfaces, rounded foliage, and zero gritty paper texture.

## Typography
Bold navy sans-serif display titles (often inside shadowed white hero cards), small monospace corner tags (`● SIMULATION` black-and-gold left, series subtitle right), tiny black role pills (`ROLE A`), crisp white comment bubbles with colored status dots, and persistent black bottom caption pills highlighting one keyword in `≈#FFC531` gold.

## Composition
Centered isometric diorama stage surrounded by generous open sky, grounded by the black-and-gold bottom caption pill and top mono corner tags. Allowed variation across treatments: either pair a pastel isometric city block with an overlapping vertical phone feed, popping comment bubbles, hashtag pills, full-yellow/dark-navy flowchart break frames, and a split-screen finale; or run as a pure modular floating-island diorama using `★ X.X` speech bubbles above tiny pawns, extreme zoom-outs to dozens of islands, and sky transitions (day blue to sunset purple-pink-orange to starry navy night) to mark acts.

## Mood
Charming and toy-like on the surface, shifting smoothly into biting irony or seriousness when the sky darkens or the dark-navy turn arrives.

## Signature elements
- Clean ~30-degree pastel isometric city blocks or floating square earth islands with rounded trees and gridded buildings.
- Tiny faceless round-headed colored pawn-like crowd figures (`3 to 4` on islands or bustling street crowds).
- Floating white/gold/pink speech or comment bubbles (`★ 4.7` ratings or social comments with colored dots).
- Persistent black bottom caption pill with `≈#FFC531` gold keywords and top-corner `● SIMULATION` mono headers.

## Recipe
1. Build modular ~30-degree isometric blocks with `≈#8C5E3C` earth sides, `≈#9BE078` grass or checkered cream tiles, `≈#4A505C` grey streets, pastel boxy buildings, rounded trees, and tiny faceless colored figures with soft shadows.
2. Frame scenes with generous sky (`≈#9ED8FA` blue or `≈#F5EFE2` cream), shifting acts either via full-yellow/dark-navy (`≈#101A3B`) flowchart breaks or smooth crossfades from day sky through sunset gradients to starry night.
3. Layer data cleanly using either a centered vertical phone mockup surrounded by popping white comment bubbles and count-up view cards, or floating `★ X.X` score bubbles (gold high, pink low) and white shadowed title cards above islands.
4. Lock `● SIMULATION` and small mono tags at top corners and a black bottom caption pill with `≈#FFC531` highlighted words.
5. Animate with smooth camera zooms between single dioramas and multi-island panoramas, popping bubbles in sequence, and counting stats upward.

## Don'ts
- Do not render realistic human faces, harsh photographic grit, or hand-drawn sketchy wobble lines.
- Do not flatten the ~30-degree isometric perspective into pure 2D side-views.
- Do not drop the black-and-gold bottom caption pill or soft toy-diorama shading.

## One-frame test
- Does a ~30-degree pastel isometric city block or floating square island anchor the center with tiny faceless figures?
- Are bubbles, title cards, or phone UI overlays clean, rounded, and toy-like without harsh grit?
- Are the top-corner mono `SIMULATION` tag and black bottom pill with gold keyword present?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 clean 30-degree isometric diorama motion graphics frame set against generous soft sky-blue (#9ED8FA) with puffy white clouds, centering a floating square toy-like island with brown earth sides (#8C5E3C), light green grass (#9BE078), pastel pink and blue boxy buildings (#F7B2BD, #9CC8F7), rounded trees, and tiny faceless colorful pawn figures with soft ambient shadows beneath floating gold (#FFC531) and white star-rating speech bubbles, a crisp white shadowed navy-sans title card above, small top-corner SIMULATION mono badge, and a black bottom caption pill with one gold highlighted keyword.
