# Green Chalkboard
Motion graphics
Nostalgic primary-school classroom centered on a chalk-dusted dark green blackboard with colorful handwritten chalk math and a black bottom caption pill.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| chalkboard green | ≈#2E6B4F | primary upper-two-thirds board with diagonal sheen |
| classroom cream | ≈#F3EAD8 | background plaster walls |
| desk & floor wood | ≈#C69C6D | warm light wood desk, board ledge, wooden pointer |
| school crimson | ≈#D9383A | ceiling pennant flags, wall clock rim, student ties |
| pastel chalks | ≈#F5F5F0 | white chalk, plus `≈#F79AC0` pink and `≈#8AD8FF` blue |
| caption pill & highlight | ≈#141414 | black bottom capsule with `≈#FFD23F` yellow keyword |

## Materials & texture
Warm 2D flat classroom cartoon featuring subtle chalk dust and diagonal sheen across the dark green blackboard, thin black vector outlines, and solid matte wood and paper props.

## Typography
Dual typographic system: casual rough-edged handwritten chalk lettering in white, yellow, pink, and light blue across the green board (with key numbers enlarged and circled in yellow chalk), contrasted against crisp 30 to 36 px white bold sans-serif bottom captions inside a black pill with one `≈#FFD23F` yellow keyword.

## Composition
Mostly medium shots framing the dark green chalkboard across the upper two-thirds above the teacher's cluttered wooden desk, grounded permanently by red-and-white triangular ceiling pennants and a round wall clock above the board; occasional wide classroom shots or close-up hand inserts break the rhythm, guided by a wooden pointer stick.

## Mood
Warm, nostalgic, and reassuringly patient, like learning from a favorite elementary school teacher.

## Signature elements
- Permanent classroom set: `≈#2E6B4F` chalkboard with wooden chalk ledge, round wall clock, and red-white ceiling pennant flags.
- Colorful handwritten chalk diagrams with oversized yellow-circled key figures on the board.
- Flat thin-outlined chibi teacher (glasses, mustache, patterned shirt, wooden pointer) and uniformed students.
- Rounded black bottom caption pill (`30 to 36 px` white sans) with one `≈#FFD23F` yellow word.

## Recipe
1. Build one reusable classroom stage: `≈#F3EAD8` cream walls, `≈#C69C6D` wood floor/desk, `≈#2E6B4F` textured chalkboard with diagonal sheen and chalk/eraser ledge, red-white ceiling pennants, and a round wall clock.
2. Fill the upper two-thirds board with casual multicolored chalk writing (white, yellow, pink `≈#F79AC0`, light blue `≈#8AD8FF`), enlarging and circling hero numbers in yellow chalk.
3. Draw thin-outlined flat chibi characters (teacher in glasses and patterned shirt; three seated students in white shirts and red ties), animating only arms, pointer stick, and facial expressions.
4. Anchor a rounded black bottom caption pill with 30 to 36 px bold white sans and one `≈#FFD23F` yellow keyword.
5. Animate chalk strokes writing onto the board, sweep the wooden pointer smoothly with ease curves, and cut calmly between medium board shots, full classroom views, and hand close-ups.

## Don'ts
- Do not switch locations away from the established classroom or strip away the clock and ceiling pennants.
- Do not set board text in sterile geometric fonts instead of rough-edged chalk handwriting.
- Do not rush cuts before viewers can read the chalk annotations.

## One-frame test
- Does a chalk-textured `≈#2E6B4F` blackboard beneath ceiling pennants and a round clock anchor the shot?
- Are board notes handwritten in multicolored pastel chalk with one enlarged circled number?
- Does a rounded black bottom pill highlight one keyword in `≈#FFD23F` yellow?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 nostalgic 2D flat classroom motion graphics frame featuring a chalk-dusted dark green blackboard (#2E6B4F) with a subtle diagonal sheen and wooden ledge filling the upper two-thirds against cream walls (#F3EAD8), topped by red-and-white triangular ceiling pennants and a round red-rimmed wall clock, colorful handwritten chalk diagrams and a large yellow-circled chalk number guided by a wooden pointer stick held by a thin-outlined chibi teacher in glasses beside a wooden desk, and a black rounded bottom caption pill with 34 px bold white sans text and one yellow (#FFD23F) highlighted keyword.
