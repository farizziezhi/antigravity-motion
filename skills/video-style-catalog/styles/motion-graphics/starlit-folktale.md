# Starlit Folktale
Motion graphics
Permanent starry deep-navy night sky over layered village silhouettes blending expressive mythic characters with crisp white orbit diagrams.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| night sky | #0F2244 | continuous deep navy backdrop across nearly every scene |
| night clouds | #1B3A66 | layered flat-soft clouds drifting in the upper sky |
| coral badge | #FF7A59 | orange-coral pill labels and bottom keyword pills |
| moon stone | #D8D2C4 | muted cream-grey primary lunar/celestial orb |
| gold & teal | ≈#F5C542 | golden crown/window glow, paired with `≈#2EB8A6` spiky hair |
| silhouette night | ≈#081326 | layered dark shoreline village, palms, boats, shrines |

## Materials & texture
Smooth digital vector illustration with subtle soft sky gradients and multi-plane dark foreground silhouettes that create atmospheric nocturnal depth without paper grain.

## Typography
Bold crisp white/cream sans-serif headlines paired with `#FF7A59` orange-coral pill labels holding black uppercase text, plus small white pill annotations (`NO BODY`). Bottom captions appear in white sans-serif with one active keyword boxed inside an `#FF7A59` coral pill.

## Composition
One continuous nocturnal world anchored across every scene: layered dark village silhouettes hugging the bottom horizon, vast `#0F2244` starry sky with `#1B3A66` clouds filling the upper two-thirds, and clean scientific orbit diagrams or celestial grids floating in the empty upper sky.

## Mood
Enchanted, serene, and wonder-struck, bridging fireside nighttime folklore smoothly into calm astronomical science.

## Signature elements
- Unchanging `#0F2244` starry night sky and layered dark village horizon acting as a persistent stage.
- Contrast between an oversized expressive mythic figure (orange face, gold crown, spiky teal hair) and thin white geometric orbit diagrams.
- `#FF7A59` orange-coral label pills and matching `#FF7A59` bottom caption keyword pills.
- Multi-orb celestial grid (such as twelve aligned `#D8D2C4` full moons) arranged cleanly across the upper night sky.

## Recipe
1. Lock one continuous nocturnal world across scenes: `#0F2244` starry sky, `#1B3A66` layered clouds, and `≈#081326` dark village silhouettes (houses, shrines, boats, palms) along the lower horizon.
2. Keep bright accents disciplined: `#D8D2C4` cream-grey for the main moon orb and `#FF7A59` coral exclusively for label pills and active caption keyword boxes.
3. Combine three distinct visual registers in sequence: calm detailed village silhouettes, a huge expressive mythic character head/bust, and crisp thin-lined white orbital ellipses or celestial grids.
4. Set headlines in bold white sans, category badges in `#FF7A59` pills with black text, and bottom captions in white with one `#FF7A59` boxed word.
5. Animate slowly and gracefully with creeping eclipse shadows, gradual draw-on orbital paths, and gentle sky drifts.

## Don'ts
- Do not switch away from the `#0F2244` night world to bright daylight scenes between shots.
- Do not add bright neon glow effects or heavy comic-book ink borders.
- Do not clutter the dark lower silhouettes with competing high-saturation foreground colors.

## One-frame test
- Does the continuous `#0F2244` starry sky with `#1B3A66` clouds and dark bottom village horizon anchor the frame?
- Does the scene pair folklore character shapes or `#D8D2C4` moons with clean white science diagrams?
- Are `#FF7A59` coral pills used for labels and the single highlighted bottom caption word?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 enchanting nocturnal motion graphics frame on a continuous deep navy starry sky (#0F2244) with soft dark-blue clouds (#1B3A66) above a layered dark silhouette village horizon (#081326) with palms and boats, centering a cream-grey moon (#D8D2C4) alongside crisp thin white elliptical orbit lines and a large expressive mythological demon head with a golden crown and spiky teal hair, bold white sans title, orange-coral (#FF7A59) label pill with black text, and a white bottom caption with one word inside an orange-coral pill.
