# Block Arcade
Motion graphics
Bright saturated voxel-style 2D cartoon characters with thick black outlines, black top chapter pills, and stacked chat-bubble punchlines.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| arcade sky blue | #7EC8F7 | bright daytime sky with simple white clouds |
| mint green | #B8E6C8 | alternate clean stage backdrop |
| checkerboard cream | #F5E6C8 | retro tiled floor planes |
| alert red | #E63946 | `MODE: RANDOM` / `PAUSE` badges and props |
| prize gold | #FFC93C | golden reward cards and keyword highlights |
| thick ink black | ≈#141414 | 4 to 6 px outlines, top pill badges, text borders |

## Materials & texture
Crisp flat saturated digital 2D game cartoon with zero paper texture, clean color fills, and bold 4 to 6 px black outlines bounding every character and prop.

## Typography
Heavy rounded white sans-serif bottom captions wrapped in thick black outlines (`≈#141414`), spotlighting one keyword in bright pink (`≈#FF5C93`) or `#FFC93C` gold. Black top pill badges (`★ PULL 9`, `⚡ BOOST`, `⏸ PAUSE`) mark each chapter.

## Composition
Consistent three-tier vertical arcade stack: black chapter badge centered at top, blocky protagonist facing forward in the lower middle beside a game machine or animal mascot, and large black-outlined bottom captions across the lower edge.

## Mood
Upbeat, mischievous, and dopamine-fast, making psychological or mechanical loops feel like a lively arcade game rather than a lecture.

## Signature elements
- Block-headed flat cartoon characters and simple animal mascots wrapped in 4 to 6 px black outlines.
- Bright `#7EC8F7` sky, `#B8E6C8` mint, and `#F5E6C8` checkerboard game stages.
- Black icon-tagged top chapter pills (`★`, `⚡`, `⏸`) and stacked speech-bubble popups.
- Heavy white black-outlined bottom captions with pink or gold keyword highlights.

## Recipe
1. Build bright flat game stages using `#7EC8F7` sky with simple white clouds, `#B8E6C8` mint rooms, or `#F5E6C8` checkerboard floors with zero grain.
2. Draw square-headed characters with dot eyes and geometric bodies, plus one small animal mascot and game props (reward dispensers, golden cards), all outlined in 4 to 6 px `≈#141414` black.
3. Anchor a small black icon-prefixed badge pill at the top center to signal each beat.
4. Set large bottom captions in bold rounded white sans with thick black outlines, coloring one keyword pink (`≈#FF5C93`) or `#FFC93C` gold.
5. Illustrate mechanisms through physical game objects rather than dry charts, animating with light squash-and-stretch and rapid stacking speech bubbles.

## Don'ts
- Do not render muted dark essays, thin delicate outlines, or data-dashboard graphs.
- Do not add textures, soft airbrush gradients, or realistic human anatomy.
- Do not replace the black-outlined white bottom captions with subtle tiny subtitles.

## One-frame test
- Do every blocky character and prop share bold 4 to 6 px black outlines on bright `#7EC8F7`/`#B8E6C8` stages?
- Is there a black icon-prefixed chapter badge at the top?
- Are bottom captions huge rounded white letters with black outlines and a bright pink or gold keyword?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 bright 2D blocky arcade cartoon motion graphics frame on a sky-blue (#7EC8F7) stage with puffy white clouds and a cream checkerboard floor (#F5E6C8), featuring a square-headed dot-eyed kid character in a green shirt beside a golden prize dispenser machine and a grey bird mascot, all wrapped in thick 5 px black outlines, a small black icon chapter pill at top center, stacked white speech bubbles, and large black-outlined white rounded-sans bottom captions with one gold (#FFC93C) highlighted word.
