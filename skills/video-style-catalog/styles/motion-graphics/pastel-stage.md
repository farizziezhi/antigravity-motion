# Pastel Stage
Motion graphics
Friendly big-headed flat chibi characters standing on a cream floor line inside a large pastel backdrop circle with soft white caption pills.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| warm cream canvas | #F7EBD8 | primary backdrop across almost all scenes |
| stage circle blue | ≈#B8E0F6 | oversized central pastel disc (alternates with yellow/lavender) |
| teal accent | ≈#48B5A3 | hero character shirt and calm secondary props |
| coral red accent | ≈#F26A4B | price bars, top label pills, caption keyword highlight |
| mustard yellow | ≈#F4C542 | sun props and alternate pastel stage discs |
| lavender purple | ≈#C5B3E6 | study/interior stage circle variant |
| condition navy | ≈#1D2B44 | single dark-backdrop scene reserved for caveats or rules |

## Materials & texture
Clean, smooth digital flat vector finish with zero paper grain, minimal outlines, and very subtle soft shading on everyday objects.

## Typography
Thick rounded extra-bold sans-serif (mixed upper/lowercase) inside colored top capsule labels and bold white figures over colored price bars. Bottom captions always sit inside a clean white pill (`≈#FFFFFF`) with dark text and one coral/red (`≈#F26A4B`) highlighted keyword.

## Composition
Highly uniform stage layout: `#F7EBD8` cream backdrop, one giant pastel circle centered behind the action, a darker cream ground line across the lower third, one big-headed chibi character plus one prop in the center, a colored pill label above, and tiny geometric doodles (pluses, crosses, squiggles, dots) floating around the perimeter.

## Mood
Cozy, warm, and reassuring, presenting grown-up topics with the gentle clarity of a picture book.

## Signature elements
- Giant pastel backdrop circle (`≈#B8E0F6`, `≈#F4C542`, or `≈#C5B3E6`) framing the central character on `#F7EBD8` cream.
- Big-headed flat chibi characters with dot eyes, rosy cheeks, and simple blocky bodies.
- White bottom caption pill featuring a single coral-red (`≈#F26A4B`) keyword highlight.
- Floating minimalist doodle confetti (pluses, waves, tiny dots) around the circle edge.

## Recipe
1. Set a `#F7EBD8` cream background crossed by a darker cream ground line in the lower third, centered behind a large pastel circle (`≈#B8E0F6`, `≈#F4C542`, or `≈#C5B3E6`).
2. Draw characters as big-headed flat chibis with dot eyes and blush marks alongside clean flat props (shelves, boxes, plants) without heavy outlines or paper textures.
3. Restrict fills to pastel teal, coral, mustard, and lavender, reserving one full `≈#1D2B44` navy frame solely for a condition or rule turn.
4. Set rounded extra-bold sans headers inside colored top pills and place captions in white bottom pills with one `≈#F26A4B` highlighted word.
5. Animate with gentle ease-out pop-ins, soft idle sways, and smooth growing bar charts, holding each scene roughly 8 to 9 seconds.

## Don'ts
- Do not add heavy black comic outlines, gritty textures, or dark thriller lighting outside the single navy caveat frame.
- Do not crowd the frame with multiple competing characters or dense multi-column layouts.
- Do not drop the large centered pastel backdrop circle.

## One-frame test
- Does a giant pastel circle sit centered on `#F7EBD8` cream above a simple floor line?
- Are characters big-headed dot-eyed chibis without heavy black outlines?
- Does the white bottom caption pill highlight one keyword in coral-red?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 cozy flat vector motion graphics frame on a warm cream canvas (#F7EBD8) with a subtle darker cream floor line, featuring a giant pastel sky-blue circle (#B8E0F6) behind one cute big-headed flat chibi character with dot eyes and rosy cheeks wearing teal (#48B5A3) next to a simple potted plant, tiny floating plus and wave doodles, a colored rounded-sans top label pill, and a white bottom caption pill with one coral-red (#F26A4B) highlighted keyword.
