# Number Line
Motion graphics
Minimalist warm-cream 2D vector world split across a continuous horizontal numbered ruler where stick figures pan from zero upward.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| warm off-white | #F6F1E7 | clean un-textured background canvas |
| coral error pill | #FF5A4E | `ERROR` / `UNDEFINED` pills and zero-value warnings |
| subtitle grey | #9A9A9A | inactive words in bottom sentences |
| bright cobalt | ≈#2B7FFF | calculator buttons, secondary numbers, supporting figures |
| turmeric & teal | ≈#F4B400 | yellow funnel/underline highlights, `≈#2EC4B6` guide stick figure |
| crisp ink black | ≈#141414 | 3 to 5 px stick figures, outlines, active subtitle words |

## Materials & texture
Pure flat digital vector whiteboard feel with zero paper grain, zero drop shadows, and uniform crisp outlines (4 to 5 px for stick figures, 3 to 4 px for flat machines and calculators).

## Typography
Bold clean sans-serif equations at 90 to 140 px (`[A ÷ B =]`) anchored below the ruler line, with key answers boxed inside `#FF5A4E` red pills in white uppercase (`ERROR`). Bottom subtitles sit at 28 to 32 px: spoken keywords turn bold black with a `≈#F4B400` yellow underline while surrounding words stay `#9A9A9A` grey.

## Composition
Signature 720x1280 split layout divided at ~62% screen height by a continuous thin horizontal ruler marked with grey ticks and sequential integers (`0, 1, 2 ... 9`). Stick-figure scenes play out above the ruler, oversized equations sit in the lower middle, and grey-to-black underlined subtitles rest at the bottom.

## Mood
Playful, puzzle-like, and classroom-clear, pairing rigorous logic with lighthearted visual slapstick.

## Signature elements
- Continuous horizontal ruler line at ~62% height with numbered ticks panning left-to-right across scenes.
- 4 to 5 px thin-lined stick figures with white circle heads, two dot eyes, and a single teal `≈#2EC4B6` guide hue.
- Giant 90 to 140 px centered equations paired with `#FF5A4E` coral answer pills.
- 28 to 32 px `#9A9A9A` grey bottom subtitles lighting up active keywords in black with a yellow underline.

## Recipe
1. Set a 720x1280 `#F6F1E7` flat cream canvas divided horizontally at ~62% height by a thin ruler line labeled with sequential grey numbers (`0` through `9`).
2. Draw the recurring guide as a 4 to 5 px stick figure with a white circular head, two dot eyes, and `≈#2EC4B6` teal accent, paired with `≈#2B7FFF` or `#FF5A4E` supporting figures.
3. Construct chunky flat props (calculators, funnel machines, signs) using 3 to 4 px black outlines and flat color fills with zero gradients.
4. Place 90 to 140 px bold sans equations below the ruler (`#FF5A4E` pill for error states) and 28 to 32 px subtitles at the bottom (`#9A9A9A` grey turning black + yellow underline on active words).
5. Pan the camera horizontally along the ruler between stations, popping props in cleanly and animating numeric counters.

## Don'ts
- Do not add paper textures, soft gradients, drop shadows, or heavy shaded cartoon anatomy.
- Do not omit the continuous horizontal numbered ruler line dividing the frame.
- Do not wrap bottom subtitles inside dark black or white capsule pills.

## One-frame test
- Does a thin numbered horizontal ruler at ~62% height divide the `#F6F1E7` cream canvas?
- Are scenes acted out by 4 to 5 px dot-eyed stick figures and flat 3 to 4 px outlined props?
- Do bottom subtitles shift from `#9A9A9A` grey to yellow-underlined black under a giant equation?

## Base prompt
Vertical 720x1280 minimalist flat vector motion graphics frame on clean warm cream (#F6F1E7), divided at 62% height by a thin horizontal ruler line with grey numbered ticks (0, 1, 2, 3), showing a 4 px black-line stick figure with a round white head, dot eyes, and teal accent (#2EC4B6) beside a flat cobalt-blue (#2B7FFF) and yellow (#F4B400) funnel machine above the line, a giant 110 px bold sans math equation with a coral-red (#FF5A4E) ERROR pill below the line, and small grey (#9A9A9A) bottom subtitles with one black yellow-underlined keyword.
