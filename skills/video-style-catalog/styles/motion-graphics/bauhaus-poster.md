# Bauhaus Poster
Motion graphics
Flat Swiss-Bauhaus geometric infographic built from oversized condensed numbers, strict primary color blocks, and crisp black caption bars.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| grid cream | #F2EFE6 | default explanation canvas with barely visible grid |
| bauhaus red | #E8442E | full-bleed pivot/risk scenes and geometric blocks |
| bauhaus blue | #1D4ED8 to #2547C4 | closing full-bleed blocks and primary shapes |
| bauhaus yellow | #F5C400 | geometric accents and bottom caption keyword highlight |
| poster ink | ≈#111111 | oversized hero figures, bold titles, bottom caption bar |

## Materials & texture
Ultra-clean flat digital print look with zero paper grain, where a fine grid on the `#F2EFE6` cream background is barely perceptible and every form is razor-sharp vector geometry.

## Typography
Extra-bold condensed uppercase sans-serif headers alongside gigantic black or white hero numerals acting as visual centerpieces. A tiny top disclaimer header (`■ FACTS & FIGURES: ILLUSTRATION • NOT ADVICE`) anchors the layout, and bottom captions sit inside a solid black bar with `#F5C400` yellow keyword highlights.

## Composition
One bold centered idea per frame surrounded by generous negative space. Narrative turns snap cleanly from `#F2EFE6` cream explanation frames to full-bleed `#E8442E` red warning blocks and `#1D4ED8` blue closing frames.

## Mood
Assertive, punchy, and poster-loud, turning a sudden switch to `#E8442E` red into an unmistakable visual warning.

## Signature elements
- Strict primary-plus-cream palette (`#F2EFE6`, `#E8442E`, `#1D4ED8`, `#F5C400`, black) with zero extra hues.
- Giant condensed number or formula serving as the central graphic motif across scenes.
- Pure flat geometry (circles, squares, triangles, bars, arrows, colorful numbered tiles) with no characters.
- Solid black bottom caption bar with `#F5C400` highlighted keywords.

## Recipe
1. Restrict the entire piece to `#F2EFE6` cream grid paper, `#E8442E` red, `#1D4ED8`–`#2547C4` blue, `#F5C400` yellow, and `≈#111111` black; keep one dominant color block per frame.
2. Center one oversized extra-bold number or equation in black or white, supported only by flat geometric primitives and crisp timelines.
3. Place a small uppercase disclaimer header at the top and a full-width black caption bar at the bottom with `#F5C400` keyword highlights.
4. Sequence acts by canvas color: explain on `#F2EFE6`, flip to full `#E8442E` for risk/reversal, and close on `#1D4ED8` with one geometric shape and one big word.
5. Animate with snappy pops and sharp-eased slides (no soft bounce), hard-cutting between color blocks every 6 to 8 seconds.

## Don'ts
- Do not add characters, organic illustrations, drop shadows, or textures.
- Do not introduce secondary colors outside cream, red, blue, yellow, black, and white.
- Do not use soft rubbery easing or slow cross-dissolves between acts.

## One-frame test
- Does the frame use only cream `#F2EFE6` (or full red/blue), primary accents, and black?
- Is one giant condensed numeral or geometric diagram the unmistakable centerpiece?
- Are characters, textures, and soft shadows completely absent?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 flat Swiss-Bauhaus geometric motion graphics poster on faint-grid cream (#F2EFE6), centered around a gigantic extra-bold black numeral flanked by crisp flat red (#E8442E), cobalt blue (#1D4ED8), and yellow (#F5C400) circles, bars, and triangles, with an uppercase condensed title, a tiny top disclaimer line, and a solid black bottom caption bar featuring a yellow (#F5C400) highlighted keyword.
