# Atlas Cartography
Motion graphics
Printed paper-atlas cartography over muted blue-grey oceans and cream landmasses with a red dividing line, north arrow, and scale bar.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| muted sea | ≈#C5D5E0 | calm pale blue-grey ocean fill |
| cream landmass | ≈#EFE6D0 | primary mapped islands and continents |
| distant terrain | ≈#D2C1A5 | muted light brown for outer background regions |
| region highlight | ≈#F4D35E | soft yellow fill for currently referenced areas |
| dividing border | ≈#D93829 | thin crisp red line splitting geographic zones |
| caption navy & gold | ≈#13263E | dark navy bottom pill with `≈#FFD23F` keyword highlight |

## Materials & texture
Printed encyclopedia atlas aesthetic featuring subtle matte paper grain and fine latitude-longitude graticule grid lines spanning across the ocean.

## Typography
Bold classic book-style serif headlines at top left paired with small uppercase sans-serif geographic labels (`REGION A`, `ISLAND B`) and crisp numeric callouts. Bottom captions sit inside a dark navy pill (`≈#13263E`) with `≈#FFD23F` yellow keyword highlights.

## Composition
Full-bleed cartographic map fills every scene from edge to edge: serif headline at top left, a consistent legend box below or beside it, a scale bar and north arrow locked at top right, and the navy caption pill anchored at the bottom.

## Mood
Scholarly, calm, and documentary-steady, like an unhurried natural-history or geography lesson.

## Signature elements
- Full-bleed paper-textured map with fine latitude/longitude grid lines across `≈#C5D5E0` seas.
- Persistent top-left legend box and top-right scale bar with north arrow on every frame.
- Thin red `≈#D93829` geographic boundary line cutting across regions.
- Tiny circular or vintage spot markers (such as a sailing ship, bird emblem, or portrait badge) pinned to map coordinates.

## Recipe
1. Fill the entire canvas with a muted vector map (`≈#C5D5E0` sea, `≈#EFE6D0` primary land, `≈#D2C1A5` outer land) overlaid with fine latitude/longitude grid lines and subtle paper grain.
2. Lock a small legend block at top left and a scale bar plus north arrow at top right across all scenes so the frame always reads like an atlas page.
3. Highlight active regions smoothly in soft yellow (`≈#F4D35E`), trace boundaries with a thin `≈#D93829` red line, and place small minimal spot icons on key coordinates.
4. Set headlines in bold book serif at top left, map place names in tiny uppercase sans, and bottom captions in a `≈#13263E` navy pill with `≈#FFD23F` highlighted keywords.
5. Animate with slow camera pans and gentle zooms, stroke draw-ons for red boundary lines, and smooth region color fills at a calm documentary pace.

## Don'ts
- Do not break away from the map into unrelated character cartoons or dark UI dashboards.
- Do not use neon oceans, glossy 3D terrain bevels, or heavy comic outlines.
- Do not omit the latitude/longitude grid, top-right scale/north arrow, or legend box.

## One-frame test
- Does a full-bleed paper-textured map with fine ocean grid lines fill the entire frame?
- Are the legend box, north arrow, and scale bar locked consistently into the top corners?
- Do regions highlight in soft yellow beside a thin red line and dark navy caption pill?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 printed encyclopedia atlas motion graphics frame with subtle paper grain and fine latitude-longitude grid lines over pale blue-grey ocean (#C5D5E0), cream and muted tan landmasses (#EFE6D0, #D2C1A5), one region highlighted in soft yellow (#F4D35E) bisected by a thin red boundary line (#D93829), tiny ship and circular bird map pins, bold book-serif title and legend box at top left, north arrow and scale bar at top right, and a dark navy (#13263E) bottom caption pill with a yellow keyword.
