# Brutalist Type
Motion graphics
Gigantic condensed uppercase typography filling full-bleed alternating color blocks with one high-contrast accent word and zero decoration.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stark black | ≈#0D0D0D | primary full-screen backdrop |
| fiery orange-red | ≈#FF4518 | alternating backdrop, vertical bar, keyword highlight |
| electric blue | ≈#1E4BFF | alternating backdrop and secondary keyword contrast |
| pale cream | ≈#F4EFE4 | alternating light full-bleed backdrop |
| bone white | ≈#F7F5F0 | solid and outlined giant display lettering |

## Materials & texture
Pure digital flat blocks with zero grain, zero gradients, zero shadows, and zero decorative textures. Sharp rectangular bars and strokes only.

## Typography
Extra-bold condensed uppercase sans-serif set at monumental scale spanning screen width, alternating between solid fills and hollow outlined strokes. One keyword per scene turns `≈#FF4518` orange-red or `≈#1E4BFF` electric blue, often underscored by a thick accent bar. A tiny underlined topic kicker stays fixed at the top-left corner across every frame.

## Composition
Left- or center-aligned giant text fills 60 to 80% of frame height with stark negative space above and below, accented only by a vertical `≈#FF4518` rule or horizontal keyword bar. An isolated tilted minimal graphic (such as a single card) appears at most once.

## Mood
Blunt, confrontational, and manifesto-loud, reading like a shouted street-protest poster.

## Signature elements
- Monumental condensed uppercase text occupying 60 to 80% of the vertical frame height.
- Mix of solid-filled and hollow-stroked display lines within the same headline block.
- Hard full-bleed background swaps between black, orange-red, electric blue, and pale cream.
- Single persistent small underlined kicker anchored at top left in every frame.

## Recipe
1. Alternate full-bleed flat backgrounds cleanly between `≈#0D0D0D`, `≈#FF4518`, `≈#1E4BFF`, and `≈#F4EFE4` on hard cuts.
2. Keep one small underlined uppercase kicker pinned to the top-left corner across all scenes as the sole constant anchor.
3. Set copy in monumental condensed extra-bold uppercase spanning 60 to 80% of screen height, mixing solid bone-white (`≈#F7F5F0`) or black lines with hollow stroke-only lines.
4. Highlight exactly one keyword per frame in contrasting `≈#FF4518` or `≈#1E4BFF`, paired with a thick underline bar or vertical side rule.
5. Omit characters and decorative shapes; animate words with hard slams or fast scale-ins and type out the closing line over rapid backdrop cuts.

## Don'ts
- Do not add character illustrations, icons, paper textures, gradients, or drop shadows.
- Do not highlight multiple unrelated words in rainbow colors on the same frame.
- Do not shrink headlines into small captions surrounded by decorative clutter.

## One-frame test
- Does giant condensed uppercase type occupy 60 to 80% of the frame height?
- Is the scene built strictly from flat solid color blocks, solid/outlined type, and a single accent rule?
- Does the tiny underlined top-left kicker remain as the only constant corner element?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 brutalist kinetic-typography motion graphics frame on a flat pitch-black backdrop (#0D0D0D), filled to 75% height with monumental extra-bold condensed uppercase lettering mixing solid bone-white (#F7F5F0) and hollow outline-stroke lines, one keyword in fiery orange-red (#FF4518) with a thick underline bar and vertical orange side accent, a tiny underlined uppercase series kicker at top left, zero illustrations, and zero textures.
