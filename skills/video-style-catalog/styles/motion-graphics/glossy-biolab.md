# Glossy Biolab
Motion graphics
Softly glowing violet-and-pink glass vessels filled with bubbling fluids and color-coded particles on deep twilight purple gradients.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deep biolab base | #1B1035 to #2A1B4D | dark purple-blue radial backdrop with soft center glow |
| signature violet | #8B5CF6 | primary vessels, ducts, and caption highlights |
| bio pink | #FF5C8A | secondary vessels, active particles, question backdrop |
| fluid cyan | #38D6FF | strictly assigned to water or clear liquid flow |
| solute yellow | #FFC93C | strictly assigned to one solute type and big question marks |
| warm orange scale | ≈#FF8A3D | multi-step color scale across test tubes |

## Materials & texture
Smooth, glossy modern science-lab vector finish featuring translucent glass chambers (thin stroke + ~10% white fill), rising fluid bubbles, soft ambient backlighting, and floating dot particles.

## Typography
Rounded bold sans-serif titles in top pills paired with giant crisp white hero figures centered inside glass containers. Every bottom caption sits inside a dark translucent rounded-full capsule (`≈#120B24`) with `#FF5C8A` or `#8B5CF6` keyword highlights, plus a small disclaimer line (`ILLUSTRATION • NOT ADVICE`) beneath frames making technical or health claims.

## Composition
Calm symmetrical framing with one central vessel, ring, duct, or test-tube row per screen, top pill title above, and rounded-full capsule caption below; a mid-video full-bleed pink-purple gradient frame with a giant `#FFC93C` question mark breaks the rhythm before the answer.

## Mood
Serene, soothing, and futuristic, transforming technical science into an approachable luminous night laboratory.

## Signature elements
- Translucent glass vessels filled with `#8B5CF6`-to-`#FF5C8A` gradient liquid and rising bubbles.
- Strict one-color-per-substance particle coding accompanied by a compact two-line dot legend.
- Five-level color-graded test-tube rack serving as a visual intensity scale.
- Dark rounded-full bottom caption pills with `#FF5C8A` or `#8B5CF6` keyword highlights.

## Recipe
1. Lay down a `#1B1035` to `#2A1B4D` purple-blue gradient with a soft ambient glow behind the central object; never use white backgrounds.
2. Build glass vessels from thin crisp outlines and ~10% opacity white fills, holding bubbling `#8B5CF6`–`#FF5C8A` liquid and floating dot particles.
3. Enforce strict color-coding (one hue per substance, keeping `#38D6FF` and `#FFC93C` exclusive) and display a compact legend beside multi-particle scenes.
4. Center giant white rounded-sans numbers inside containers, place captions in blurred dark rounded-full pills with `#FF5C8A`/`#8B5CF6` highlights, and add small disclaimers where needed.
5. Animate fluids filling smoothly, particles drifting along filtered paths, tubes shifting hues step by step, and insert one full-bleed pink-gradient `#FFC93C` question pause before dissolving to the payoff.

## Don'ts
- Do not use white or bright daylight backgrounds, hard flat edges, or paper grain.
- Do not reassign a substance's dot color halfway through a sequence.
- Do not draw realistic anatomical gore or sharp-cornered rectangular caption boxes.

## One-frame test
- Does the scene glow softly over a `#1B1035`–`#2A1B4D` dark violet backdrop?
- Are glass chambers translucent (~10% white fill) with bubbling gradient fluid or color-coded dots?
- Is the bottom caption enclosed in a dark rounded-full capsule with pink or violet highlights?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 glossy night-biolab motion graphics frame over a deep purple-blue gradient (#1B1035 to #2A1B4D) with soft center glow, featuring a translucent rounded glass vessel with 10% white fill containing bubbling violet-to-pink (#8B5CF6 to #FF5C8A) liquid, floating cyan (#38D6FF) and yellow (#FFC93C) dot particles, a huge white rounded-sans hero percentage centered inside, a top title pill, and a dark translucent rounded-full bottom caption pill with one pink highlighted keyword.
