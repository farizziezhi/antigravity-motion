# Pastel Clay
Motion graphics
Tactile matte 3D claymation objects and a wide-eyed yellow blob mascot floating on minimalist pastel backgrounds with sculpted 3D typography.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| warm cream clay | ≈#F5EBDC | default minimal background and sculpted dough forms |
| pale ice blue | ≈#DCEEF7 | alternate cool pastel background |
| mascot yellow | ≈#FFD54F | wide-eyed clay blob character and title accents |
| clay teal | ≈#4DB6AC | sculpted vessels, appliances, and title lines |
| clay lavender | ≈#B39DDB | secondary pastel spheres and balloon networks |
| clay coral | ≈#FF7F6B | warm props, title bars, and `≈#E63946` caption keyword |

## Materials & texture
Soft matte 3D plasticine clay with gentle subsurface scattering, subtly rounded edges, slight fingerprint-like surface imperfection, and soft grounded studio shadows. Never sharp-edged or glossy plastic.

## Typography
Chunky rounded 3D clay-like display letters stacked at the top, each line colored in a distinct pastel (orange, teal, lavender, yellow) with soft shadows. Bottom captions sit inside a clean white pill (`≈#FFFFFF`) with one bright red (`≈#E63946`) highlighted keyword.

## Composition
Extremely minimal centerpiece staging: one large sculpted clay object or mascot centered on an uncluttered flat pastel backdrop with tiny scattered clay crumbs or dots, 3D title stacked above, and white caption pill anchored below.

## Mood
Cute, tactile, and comforting like an educational stop-motion toy box you want to touch.

## Signature elements
- Matte 3D clay material with soft subsurface scattering, rounded seams, and subtle organic imperfections.
- Wide-eyed yellow clay blob mascot (`≈#FFD54F`) with oversized googly eyes.
- Multi-colored stacked 3D rounded display lettering above the scene.
- Ultra-clean pastel background with huge negative space and a white/red-keyword bottom caption pill.

## Recipe
1. Render all assets in matte 3D clay with gentle subsurface scattering, rounded geometry, and soft ambient shadows; eliminate every sharp edge.
2. Place scenes on solid warm cream (`≈#F5EBDC`) or pale ice blue (`≈#DCEEF7`) backdrops with generous empty margins and minimal crumb particles.
3. Restrict object colors to warm pastel yellow (`≈#FFD54F`), teal (`≈#4DB6AC`), lavender (`≈#B39DDB`), and coral (`≈#FF7F6B`), anchored by a googly-eyed yellow mascot blob.
4. Set stacked top titles in chunky multicolored 3D clay lettering and bottom captions in a clean white pill with one `≈#E63946` red keyword.
5. Animate with playful squash-and-stretch, slow swelling/puffing expansion, and gentle wobbles at an unhurried one-object-per-scene pace.

## Don'ts
- Do not render 2D flat vector outlines, hard specular plastic gloss, or sharp metallic corners.
- Do not clutter the pastel background with busy scenery, grids, or extra props.
- Do not use harsh neon hues or high-speed snappy cuts.

## One-frame test
- Do every prop and title letter look sculpted from matte soft-edged plasticine clay?
- Does a single large pastel clay object or wide-eyed yellow blob dominate an empty backdrop?
- Is the bottom caption a white pill highlighting one keyword in red?
