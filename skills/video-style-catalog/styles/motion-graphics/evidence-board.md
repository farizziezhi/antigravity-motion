# Evidence Board
Motion graphics
Cream archival paper cutouts pinned crookedly onto textured brown corkboard with red string connections and tilted rubber stamps.

## Palette
| role | hex | note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| corkboard base | #A67C52 to #8B5E3C | full-frame textured brown cork background |
| paper cards | #F5EFE0 | cream archival slips and caption strips |
| caption highlight | #FFD84D | yellow marker behind one active keyword |
| accent & stamps | ≈#B83A2D | pins, linking string, tilted stamps, top corner tag |
| frame border | ≈#111111 | deep black edges and vignette on bookend scenes |

## Materials & texture
Physical analog corkboard surface with visible grain, lifted cream paper cards casting soft drop shadows, round pushpins, thin connecting threads, and slightly translucent ink stamps. Nothing looks flat or clean digital.

## Typography
Three consistent tiers: bold black serif display titles, small monospace typewriter text for document lists and mugshot labels, and a lower paper-strip typewriter caption with one keyword highlighted in `#FFD84D`. Tilted condensed red stamps sit at roughly 85% opacity, and a small red corner tag (such as `FILE • FACT`) sits at the top right.

## Composition
One central evidence piece (such as a calendar, silhouette card, clipping, or list) sits in the middle over generous corkboard breathing room, anchored above the bottom paper-strip caption. New cards pin alongside earlier ones without messy piling.

## Mood
Lighthearted detective mystery: official archival presentation on the surface, playful irony in the visual details.

## Signature elements
- Red round pushpins connected by thin red threads across pinned paper cards.
- Tilted condensed red ink stamps (`≈#B83A2D`, ~85% opacity) stamped over documents.
- Plain black character silhouettes with one identifying headgear or prop over a vertical height chart.
- Cream `#F5EFE0` paper slips tilted 1 to 3 degrees with soft drop shadows.

## Recipe
1. Fill the canvas with brown corkboard texture (`#A67C52` to `#8B5E3C`), adding a dark black vignette (`≈#111111`) on opening and closing frames.
2. Place content on cream paper cards (`#F5EFE0`) tilted 1 to 3 degrees with soft drop shadows, pinned with red circles and linked by thin red lines (`≈#B83A2D`).
3. Render figures strictly as solid black silhouettes with one simple prop outline against a vertical measurement scale and a mono `ITEM #N` label below.
4. Set headlines in heavy black serif, document metadata in small monospace, red condensed stamps at 85% opacity, and bottom captions on a white/cream strip with `#FFD84D` keyword highlighting.
5. Animate each new card with a pin-down pop (scale 1.05 to 1 with slight rotation settle) and cut directly between evidence items.

## Don'ts
- Do not use clean flat vector backgrounds without corkboard grain or paper shadows.
- Do not render detailed facial features, realistic shading, or complex character animation.
- Do not stack cards into an unreadable pile that hides the corkboard spacing.
- Do not introduce neon colors or glossy UI gradients outside the cork, cream, black, red, and yellow palette.

## One-frame test
- Is the background unmistakably textured brown corkboard with generous breathing room?
- Are all foreground elements tilted cream paper cards, pins, red string, or silhouettes?
- Does the bottom caption appear as a paper strip with one `#FFD84D` highlighted keyword?

## Base prompt
Vertical 9:16 analog detective motion graphics frame on a full-bleed textured brown corkboard (#A67C52), featuring a single cream archival paper card (#F5EFE0) tilted 2 degrees with a soft drop shadow, fastened by a red pushpin and thin red thread (#B83A2D), a plain black silhouette over a vertical measurement grid, bold black serif headline, small typewriter metadata, a tilted red rubber stamp at 85% opacity, and a bottom paper-strip caption with one yellow (#FFD84D) highlighted word.
