# Spotlight
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 3 (Annotation over video)
Tightly framed face lit by a soft radial spotlight against near-black green, paired with delicate white serif subtitles and warm yellow numerals.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Dark green-black | `≈#0E1512` | Vignetted backdrop and full-screen cards |
| Primary white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Thin serif subtitles, line icons, body words |
| Warm stage yellow | `≈#F7C948` | Giant numerals and fading closing keyword |

## Materials & texture
Smooth theatrical darkness created by a radial vignette tapering into `≈#0E1512` with no hard edges or flat color blocks. Text and thin white line icons sit directly over darkness with crisp contrast.

## Typography
Thin, unbolded `#FFFFFF` serif subtitles in the lower third distinguish the narration from standard sans styles. Full-screen cards build a strict three-tier hierarchy: small white italic serif lead-in, giant extra-bold sans phrase (`≈#F7C948` numeral + `#FFFFFF` word), and small white sans icon labels below.

## Composition
Close-up chest-up framing tighter than a medium shot so the lit face anchors the center of the spotlight. Alternates with dark `≈#0E1512` full-screen emphasis cards and groups of three thin white line icons placed cleanly above the head.

## Mood
Intimate, theatrical, and focused, directing the viewer's eye like a single stage spotlight.

## Signature elements
- Soft radial spotlight vignette darkening the surrounding room into `≈#0E1512` while leaving only the face and shoulders illuminated.
- Unbolded small `#FFFFFF` serif lower-third subtitles over close-up footage.
- Three-tier `≈#0E1512` full-screen emphasis cards: white italic serif kicker, giant extra-bold sans numeral in `≈#F7C948` + white noun, and a row of thin white labeled line icons.
- Supporting icon rows always grouped in threes above the speaker's head, plus a closing card whose final yellow keyword partially fades out.

## Recipe
- Frame close-up (chest up), darken the surroundings to `≈#0E1512`, and apply a smooth radial spotlight vignette centered on the face.
- Set lower-third narration subtitles in small, regular-weight `#FFFFFF` serif without any box or bolding.
- Build dark `≈#0E1512` full-screen cards with three stacked tiers: `#FFFFFF` italic serif lead-in (`"each day holds"`), giant extra-bold sans with `≈#F7C948` number + `#FFFFFF` word (`"24 hours"`), and labeled thin white line icons beneath (`"sleep"`, `"work"`).
- Overlay supporting icons over footage strictly in groups of three thin `#FFFFFF` line icons with small sans labels above the head (`"rest / meals / transit"`).
- End on a `≈#0E1512` card pairing white italic serif above extra-bold sans where the last `≈#F7C948` word gradually fades to half-transparent.

## Don'ts
- Never use bright daylight rooms or flat unvignetted walls.
- Never set lower-third narration subtitles in bold sans-serif.
- Do not place icons singly or inside filled dark pill containers.
- Avoid burnt-orange accents, horizontal segmented bars, or giant numerals masking tinted video footage as in Graphite.

## One-frame test
- Does a soft radial spotlight illuminate the close-up face against a deep `≈#0E1512` surround?
- Are the lower-third subtitles delicate regular-weight white serif rather than sans?
- Do graphic cards combine italic serif kickers, `≈#F7C948` giant numerals, and grouped-by-three thin white line icons?

## Base prompt
Intimate theatrical talking-head video treatment featuring a close-up chest-up speaker illuminated by a soft radial spotlight against a deep near-black green (#0E1512) vignette. Lower-third subtitles are small regular-weight white serif with no background box. Dark full-screen emphasis cards layer a small white italic serif lead-in above giant extra-bold sans numerals in warm yellow (#F7C948) paired with white words and thin white line icons below. Over footage, groups of three thin white labeled line icons float cleanly above the head, closing on a dark card where the final yellow keyword fades softly into darkness.
