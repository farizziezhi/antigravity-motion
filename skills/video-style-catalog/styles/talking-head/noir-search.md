# Noir Search
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 3 (Annotation over video)
Darkened footage shifting to cool desaturated tones, framed by dark translucent search bars, a floating pink outline heart, a magnified word map, and a glowing-arc title card.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pitch black card | `≈#0A0A0A` | Full-screen scattered word-map canvas |
| Cool desaturated grade | `≈#18202C` | Late-half cooled low-saturation room tint |
| Word-map gray | `≈#7A7F8A` | Scattered background words at varied opacity |
| Keyword yellow | `#FFD21F` | Magnified word, kicker line, subtitle accent |
| Outline heart pink | `#FF5C8A` | Small floating stroke-only heart near edge |
| Primary white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Giant title with period and base subtitles |

## Materials & texture
Moody nocturnal contrast mixing dark translucent UI glass pills (`search bars`) with raw cinema-style color grading that shifts from natural room tones into a cold, desaturated blue-black wash in the second half.

## Typography
Clean sans-serif UI text inside typing search bars, scattered low-opacity `#7A7F8A` words across a pitch-black map, and a dramatic movie-poster title card: widely tracked `#FFD21F` uppercase kicker above one massive `#FFFFFF` extra-bold sans word ending in a period (`"Signal."`). Subtitles are `#FFFFFF` sans with `#FFD21F` highlights.

## Composition
Frames the talking-head from above with dark translucent search pills (eventually stacked `3` high above the speaker's head) and edge-drifting `#FF5C8A` outline hearts, alternating with a pitch-black word-map card and a darkened desaturated title overlay.

## Mood
Mysterious, intimate, and nocturnal, inviting the viewer to peek inside a quiet digital investigation.

## Signature elements
- Typed search-bar overlays: dark semi-transparent rounded pills with a magnifying glass icon at top-frame (`"how to understand..."`), stacking up to `3` bars above the head at peaks.
- Black word-map card: `≈#0A0A0A` screen scattered with muted `#7A7F8A` words at mixed sizes/opacities while a magnifying glass enlarges one `#FFD21F` keyword.
- Cinematic title card over darkened, cool-desaturated footage: tracked `#FFD21F` kicker (`"THE HIDDEN COST"`), giant `#FFFFFF` extra-bold word with period (`"Signal."`), and a thin glowing arc curving overhead.
- Small `#FF5C8A` pink stroke-only heart icon floating lightly near the frame edge as a contrast accent.

## Recipe
- Overlay a dark semi-transparent search pill with a magnifying glass icon in the upper frame showing partially typed text; stack `2` to `3` search bars above the speaker's head for emphasis beats.
- Float one small `#FF5C8A` outline heart gently near a lateral frame edge.
- Build one `≈#0A0A0A` word-map card scattering `#7A7F8A` terms at varied scales (`20%` to `70%` opacity), centered on a magnifying glass lens zooming one `#FFD21F` keyword.
- Shift the second-half talking-head color grade to cool, desaturated blue-black tones and overlay a cinematic title card (`#FFD21F` tracked kicker, giant `#FFFFFF` period-ending word, thin glowing overhead arc).
- Keep lower-third subtitles `#FFFFFF` sans with `#FFD21F` yellow keyword highlights.

## Don'ts
- Never leave the second-half color grade warm or saturated during the cinematic title card.
- Do not replace the thin glowing arc and giant period-ending title with busy multi-column layouts or checklists.
- Never fill the floating pink `#FF5C8A` heart solid; keep it strictly stroke-only.
- Avoid bright white daylight canvases or flat playful pastel stickers.

## One-frame test
- Are dark translucent typing search bars stacked at the top of the frame or a `#FF5C8A` outline heart floating near the edge?
- Does the edit feature either a scattered `#7A7F8A` word-map with a `#FFD21F` magnified word or a cool desaturated title frame with a thin glowing overhead arc?
- Are narration subtitle keywords highlighted in `#FFD21F` yellow?

## Base prompt
Cinematic nocturnal talking-head video treatment framing the speaker beneath dark translucent pill-shaped search bars with typing text and a small floating pink (#FF5C8A) outline heart near the frame edge. Includes a pitch-black (#0A0A0A) word-map screen scattered with faint gray terms at varied sizes where a magnifying glass enlarges one yellow (#FFD21F) word, plus a dramatic second-half shift to a cool, desaturated blue-black grade topped by a tracked yellow kicker, a massive white period-ending sans title, and a thin glowing arc curving overhead. White lower-third subtitles highlight keywords in yellow.
