# Noted
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 5 (Stickers and paste-ups)
Ruled white notebook cards taped over a completely blurred talking-head with yellow-highlighted titles, tracked top labels, and black keyword boxes.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Notebook paper | `#F7F3EA` | Ruled paper card with cream masking tape |
| Margin line red | `#D43D2A` | Vertical left-side notebook margin rule |
| Ruled line blue | `≈#A8C5E8` | Pale horizontal notebook lines across card |
| Highlighter yellow | `#FFD400` | Sole accent color for titles and subtitles |
| Ink black | `≈#141414` | Card text, giant initial word, keyword boxes |
| Subtitle white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Lower-third narration text over raw shot |

## Materials & texture
Authentic physical stationery feel: `#F7F3EA` paper cards ruled with `#D43D2A` vertical margins and `≈#A8C5E8` horizontal lines, anchored at the top by semi-matte cream paper tape. Behind paper cards, the talking-head shot blurs completely out of focus.

## Typography
Clean handwritten-study or crisp sans-serif hierarchy: widely tracked small uppercase labels across the upper frame (`"BEFORE"`, `"NOW"`, `"THE WORKFLOW"`), black ink keywords wrapped inside solid black boxes with white text, one oversized black title backed by a `#FFD400` highlighter swipe, and numbered `01` / `02` / `03` lists on ruled paper. Subtitles stay small `#FFFFFF` sans with `#FFD400` highlights on keywords.

## Composition
Leaves the natural room and speaker unedited during spoken beats, then racks the entire talking-head layer into heavy blur whenever a sharp ruled `#F7F3EA` notebook card is taped into center focus. Closes with one brief full-screen visual insert labeled by a dark pill (`"RESULT"`).

## Mood
Tidy, quiet, and studious, resembling neatly organized study notes pinned over a desk.

## Signature elements
- Taped ruled notebook card (`#F7F3EA` paper, `#D43D2A` vertical margin, `≈#A8C5E8` horizontal lines, cream paper tape at top) holding a `#FFD400`-highlighted header and `01`/`02`/`03` numbered items.
- Complete background blur of the talking-head shot behind the tack-sharp paper card so only the note remains readable.
- Widely letter-spaced small uppercase section labels pinned near the top edge, paired with solid black rectangular keyword boxes containing white text.
- Oversized black opening word backed by one `#FFD400` highlighter stroke, plus lower-third white subtitles with `#FFD400` keyword highlights.

## Recipe
- Keep the natural talking-head room intact during regular shots; overlay small tracked uppercase labels along the top margin (`"BEFORE"`, `"NOW"`).
- Highlight key spoken words in lower-third `#FFFFFF` subtitles strictly with `#FFD400`, or box select top-area keywords in solid `≈#141414` rectangles with `#FFFFFF` text.
- For list beats, blur the entire talking-head frame heavily and layer a sharp `#F7F3EA` ruled paper card (`#D43D2A` vertical left rule, `≈#A8C5E8` horizontal lines, cream tape strip at top center) with a `#FFD400`-highlighted title (`"PROCESS"`) and 3 numbered steps (`"01 outline"`, `"02 draft"`, `"03 refine"`).
- Add one closing full-screen visual insert topped by a dark rounded pill (`"RESULT"`).

## Don'ts
- Never introduce accent colors beyond `#FFD400` (aside from the stationery `#D43D2A` and `≈#A8C5E8` card rules).
- Never leave the speaker in sharp focus behind an overlaid ruled notebook card.
- Do not tilt labels wildly like playful pop stickers; keep cards and tracked headers tidy and aligned.
- Avoid replacing the natural room background with solid digital color fields or dark vignettes.

## One-frame test
- Is a ruled `#F7F3EA` notebook card (`#D43D2A` margin, `≈#A8C5E8` lines, cream paper tape) sharp over a heavily blurred talking-head shot?
- Are `#FFD400` highlighter strokes and black-box white-text labels the only text emphasis devices?
- Are top-edge chapter markers set in small, widely tracked uppercase letters?
