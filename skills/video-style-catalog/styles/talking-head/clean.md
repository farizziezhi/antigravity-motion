# Clean
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 3 (Annotation over video)
Bright daylight talking-head footage in open white space with stacked yellow sans keywords, white line-icon rows above the head, and one cream serif-sans card.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Daylight cream | `≈#F5F2EA` | Bright room backdrop and full-screen card |
| Subtitle white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Lower-third captions and thin line icons |
| Muted card gray | `≈#6E737B` | Lead-in line on the full-screen cream card |
| Keyword yellow | `≈#F5C518` | Oversized uppercase sans keywords over footage |
| Card accent green | `≈#1E7A4C` | Single highlighted word on the cream card |

## Materials & texture
Airy, ungraded daylight footage with open background space and zero dark vignettes. Overlays stay crisp and weightless with only a subtle soft shadow behind small white subtitles for legibility.

## Typography
Two-layer contrast over video: small `#FFFFFF` sans subtitles in the lower third paired with oversized uppercase `≈#F5C518` extra-bold sans keywords (`2` to `3x` subtitle size) stacked directly above or between caption lines. The single cream card mixes `#6E737B` lead-in text with italic serif and bold sans featuring one `≈#1E7A4C` word.

## Composition
Medium shot framed from chest up with generous empty bright wall space above the speaker's head to host clean horizontal rows of thin white line icons. Segments breathe via one single-frame full-screen blur transition and one `≈#F5F2EA` full-screen card.

## Mood
Light, airy, and informative, keeping the edit unobtrusive and uncluttered.

## Signature elements
- Two-tier caption stack: small `#FFFFFF` soft-shadowed sans subtitles topped by `2` to `3x` larger uppercase `≈#F5C518` extra-bold sans keywords (`"JOURNEY"`, `"SIGNAL"`).
- Horizontal row of thin `#FFFFFF` line icons with small centered labels floating cleanly in the headroom above the speaker.
- One full-screen `≈#F5F2EA` card with small gray lead-in text, one thin line icon, and a mixed italic-serif + bold-sans phrase highlighting one word in `≈#1E7A4C`.
- Single-frame full-image motion/lens blur over the speaker acting as a quiet segment breath.

## Recipe
- Shoot or select bright footage against a clean light wall (`≈#F5F2EA` range); avoid darkening edges or adding vignettes.
- Place small `#FFFFFF` sans subtitles with a soft drop shadow in the lower third, and stack `2` to `3x` larger uppercase `≈#F5C518` extra-bold keywords directly above or between lines.
- Place 3 evenly spaced thin `#FFFFFF` line icons with small sans labels in the empty headroom above the speaker (`"commuter"`, `"courier"`, `"traveler"`).
- Insert one full-screen `≈#F5F2EA` card containing small gray lead-in text (`"at first you assume,"`), one thin directional icon, and a large mixed italic-serif + bold-sans phrase coloring one keyword `≈#1E7A4C`.
- Transition between major beats with a single full-frame blur on the talking-head shot.

## Don'ts
- Never add dark vignettes, moody color grades, or heavy black boxes behind subtitles.
- Never cover the speaker's face with yellow color washes or full-screen yellow cards.
- Keep list icons thin, white, and spaced cleanly above the head rather than stacked in colored pills.
- Do not replace talking-head footage with full-screen mobile chat threads, search lists, or blue rounded notification screens as in Interface.

## One-frame test
- Is the background bright and open with generous clean headroom for thin white line icons?
- Do oversized uppercase `≈#F5C518` sans keywords stack cleanly over small soft-shadowed white subtitles?
- Does the single graphic break use a `≈#F5F2EA` cream card with mixed serif-sans typography and one `≈#1E7A4C` accent word?
