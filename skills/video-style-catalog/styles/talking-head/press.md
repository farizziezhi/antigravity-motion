# Press
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 2 (Video inside a layout)
Pale cream broadsheet newspaper layouts with grey greeking columns, hairline rules, an inset video photo, and black highlight bars.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Paper cream | `#F1EEE7` | Broadsheet page background and late frame border |
| Ink black | `#1A1A1A` | Serif headlines, rules, highlight strip fill |
| Column gray | `#C9C4B8` | Horizontal greeking bars and thin column rules |
| Strip white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Text seated inside black highlight strips |
| Accent pale gold | `≈#C9A96A` | Floating italic serif emphasis words in late half |

## Materials & texture
Matte uncoated newsprint paper feel with zero gloss, zero gradients, and zero bright digital colors. Depth comes purely from editorial grid alignment and typographic scale.

## Typography
Classic editorial serif (Playfair Display or Times style) for cropped oversized headlines and floating italic emphasis words, paired with small clean sans labels and abstract gray greeking bars simulating body copy. In the second half, standard subtitles give way to floating cream-gold italic serif phrases.

## Composition
First half places talking-head footage inside a structured multi-column newspaper layout as a small rectangular editorial photo beside greeking columns and hairline dividers. Second half transitions to full-bleed talking-head footage framed by a thin `#F1EEE7` paper border.

## Mood
Reflective, measured, and print-editorial, like a quiet Sunday broadsheet opinion column.

## Signature elements
- Multi-column `#F1EEE7` newspaper page using horizontal `#C9C4B8` greeking bars and thin vertical/horizontal rules, with talking-head video inset as a column photo.
- Oversized `#1A1A1A` serif headline intentionally cropped by the frame edge.
- Inverted black highlighter strips (`#1A1A1A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text) for key emphasis lines.
- Thin `#F1EEE7` paper border around full-bleed footage in the second half, paired with floating `#C9A96A` italic serif words instead of standard subtitles.

## Recipe
- Build a broadsheet grid on `#F1EEE7` using `#1A1A1A` ink and `#C9C4B8` hairline dividers; populate 2 to 3 columns with horizontal `#C9C4B8` greeking bars.
- Place an oversized `#1A1A1A` serif headline across the top, allowing letters to crop off the frame edge, and embed the talking-head video as a rectangular column photo beside greeking.
- Highlight key statements as `#1A1A1A` solid bars with crisp `#FFFFFF` text (`"yet on the other side / we fear falling behind"`).
- Transition mid-edit to full-bleed footage inside a thin `#F1EEE7` paper margin, replacing standard subtitles with 1 to 2 floating `≈#C9A96A` italic serif words (`"ahead"`, `"moving forward"`).

## Don'ts
- Never add vivid neon accents, yellow highlighters, or glossy gradients.
- Never replace the inset column video with full-bleed unframed raw footage in the opening layout.
- Do not render standard white sans lower-third subtitles during the italic serif finale.
- Avoid stripping away newspaper columns, greeking lines, hairline rules, and cream borders to leave only floating gold words and a circular face crop on darkness as in Calm.

## One-frame test
- Does the opening layout read immediately as a `#F1EEE7` printed newspaper page with greeking bars, hairline rules, and video inset as a photo?
- Are emphasis bars inverted (`#1A1A1A` fill, `#FFFFFF` text) rather than bright yellow?
- Does the closing segment frame full-bleed video with a cream paper border and floating pale-gold italic serif words?

## Base prompt
Print-editorial talking-head video treatment styled as a pale cream (#F1EEE7) Sunday broadsheet newspaper. Classic black serif headlines crop boldly at the frame edge above hairline gray (#C9C4B8) column rules, horizontal greeking bars simulating article columns, and talking-head footage inset neatly as a column photograph. Key statements sit inside crisp black (#1A1A1A) highlight bars with white text. The second half shifts to full-bleed speaker footage framed by a thin cream paper border with floating pale-gold (#C9A96A) italic serif words replacing standard subtitles. Flat matte paper finish, zero vivid accents.
