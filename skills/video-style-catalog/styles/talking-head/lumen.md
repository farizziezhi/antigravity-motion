# Lumen
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 3 (Annotation over video)
Intact single-shot room darkened with a soft edge vignette, alternating glowing mint-green and purple chapter numbers, floating white icon chips, and orange subtitle highlights.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Soft edge vignette | `≈#121216` | Subtle dark falloff framing the intact room |
| Mint chapter glow | `#3DFF9E` | Outer glow and numeral color for chapter A |
| Purple chapter glow | `#A78BFA` | Outer glow and numeral color for chapter B |
| Subtitle orange | `#FF8A1E` | Keyword highlight color in narration subtitles |
| Chip & primary white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Circular icon chips, subtitles, chapter sublabel |

## Materials & texture
Natural room footage kept intact throughout, warmed and focused by a gentle perimeter vignette. Graphic overlays feel luminous and atmospheric through soft outer glows on numerals and subtle translucent dark badges.

## Typography
Bold sans-serif chapter numerals (`"03"`, `"04"`) emitting vivid outer glows, paired directly beneath with small widely tracked uppercase `#FFFFFF` sublabels (`"FOCUSED SHIFT"`). Lower-third subtitles are `#FFFFFF` sans with single keywords highlighted in `#FF8A1E` orange.

## Composition
Continuous uncut talking-head shot (`0` hard cuts) where all visual shifts come from soft light overlays entering and exiting over the same space. Glowing numerals anchor cleanly near the side or chest while small white circular icon chips float casually around the speaker's head.

## Mood
Warm, refined, and atmospheric, guiding focus softly with light rather than loud cuts.

## Signature elements
- Alternating bi-color outer-glow chapter numbers: giant bold sans numerals switching glow hue per chapter (`#3DFF9E` mint green, then `#A78BFA` soft purple) above tracked `#FFFFFF` uppercase labels.
- Continuous single-shot room preservation with zero hard cuts and a gentle perimeter vignette focusing light onto the speaker.
- Small `#FFFFFF` circular icon chips (magnifier, lightning bolt in accent hues) floating lightly beside the head instead of long text blocks.
- Lower-third `#FFFFFF` subtitles with `#FF8A1E` orange keyword highlights and an occasional dark translucent chest pill with a status dot.

## Recipe
- Keep the original talking-head room visible at all times with zero hard cuts; apply a soft `≈#121216` edge vignette (`≈25%` to `40%` at corners) to draw focus inward.
- Animate each chapter beat in and out as an overlay using a giant bold sans index number (`"03"`) with a soft outer glow (`#3DFF9E` on one beat, `#A78BFA` on the next) and small widely tracked `#FFFFFF` uppercase title underneath.
- Float 1 to 2 small `#FFFFFF` circular icon chips containing colored vector icons near head height for visual punctuation.
- Optional single dark semi-transparent status pill with a small warm status dot near chest level.
- Set lower-third narration subtitles in `#FFFFFF` sans, highlighting keywords strictly in `#FF8A1E` orange.

## Don'ts
- Never cut away to opaque solid-colored backgrounds or replace the natural room.
- Do not use hard cuts to drive pacing; transition purely via overlay fades and light shifts.
- Never highlight subtitle keywords in red or yellow; keep subtitle highlights `#FF8A1E` orange.
- Avoid solid royal-blue poster backdrops, hard cuts, dual-tone yellow/white outline headlines, and red subtitle highlights as in Poster.

## One-frame test
- Does the natural room stay intact under a soft perimeter vignette with zero hard cuts?
- Do giant chapter numerals emit a soft `#3DFF9E` or `#A78BFA` outer glow above small tracked white uppercase text?
- Are floating white circular icon chips present near the head and keywords highlighted in `#FF8A1E` orange?

## Base prompt
Atmospheric, refined talking-head video treatment preserving the intact natural room in a continuous uncut shot framed by a gentle dark perimeter vignette. Large bold sans-serif chapter numerals float over the scene with a luminous outer glow that alternates per chapter between mint green (#3DFF9E) and soft purple (#A78BFA), paired with small widely tracked white uppercase sublabels underneath. Small white circular chips holding colored minimal icons drift near the speaker's head, while lower-third white sans subtitles highlight key words in warm orange (#FF8A1E). Smooth light-driven overlays, zero hard cuts.
