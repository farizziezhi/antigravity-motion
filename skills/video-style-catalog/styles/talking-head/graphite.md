# Graphite
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 1 (Full graphic screen replaces video)
Dark matte charcoal graphic screens with giant numerals, horizontal segmented bars, and orange-tinted footage masked inside oversized numbers.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal canvas | `#1E2226` | Matte background for graphic screens |
| Mask void black | `≈#0A0A0A` | Outer field around video-masked numbers |
| Pill surface | `#2A2E33` | Dark container for category pills |
| Primary text | `#FFFFFF` | Labels, active bar segment, subtitles |
| Inactive bar segment | `≈#5A6068` | Unhighlighted portion of segmented bar |
| Accent orange | `#E85C2B` | Sole accent color, used sparingly and large |

## Materials & texture
Flat matte surfaces with zero grain, zero gradients, and zero drop shadows. Visual separation comes strictly from dark-light contrast and generous empty space.

## Typography
Bold geometric sans-serif for oversized numbers spanning a third of frame height or nearly full width. Small white sans labels sit beneath bars and inside pills. Subtitles use plain lowercase white sans without any box or pill in the lower third.

## Composition
Alternates cleanly between full-frame talking-head shots and full-screen dark graphic cards containing only numbers, segmented bars, and labels. Video and graphics never mix in one frame except during the video-masked numeral and floating pill moments.

## Mood
Serious, measured, and analytical, like a dark technical magazine infographic.

## Signature elements
- Text-as-video-mask: a giant numeral filled with orange-tinted talking-head footage against a pitch-black surround.
- Giant single numeral per segment (roughly one-third screen height) paired with a short uppercase orange kicker above it.
- Horizontal segmented bar with muted gray and crisp white active blocks, each carrying a small two-line label underneath.
- Group of two to three dark pill tags containing an orange line icon and small white label overlaid on talking-head footage.

## Recipe
- Alternate full-frame talking-head shots with `#1E2226` full-screen graphic cards; restrict accent color strictly to `#E85C2B`.
- Place one giant bold sans numeral (`≈1/3` frame height) per segment with a small `#E85C2B` uppercase label above and one horizontal segmented bar (`#FFFFFF` active, `≈#5A6068` inactive) below, e.g. `"rest / 8 hrs"` and `"work / 8 hrs"`.
- Build one transition moment where a full-height numeral masks talking-head footage tinted `≈40%` `#E85C2B` while the rest of the frame is `#0A0A0A`.
- Overlay 2 to 3 `#2A2E33` rounded pills (`#E85C2B` line icon + small `#FFFFFF` label such as `"focus"`, `"break"`, `"study"`) above or beside the speaker.
- Keep narration subtitles lowercase `#FFFFFF` sans with no container in the lower third.

## Don'ts
- Never introduce a second accent color beyond `#E85C2B`.
- Do not add textures, gradients, glows, or drop shadows to graphic cards.
- Never place subtitles inside pill boxes or highlight keywords inside the subtitle line.
- Avoid yellow accents, italic serif typography, three-icon rows above the head, or radial face spotlight vignettes used in Spotlight.

## One-frame test
- Is the graphic screen pure flat `#1E2226` with `#E85C2B` as the sole accent?
- Is there one giant geometric number (or video-masked numeral) with a horizontal segmented bar or `#2A2E33` orange-icon pills?
- Are narration subtitles plain lowercase `#FFFFFF` sans with no background container?

## Base prompt
Minimalist dark editorial talking-head video treatment alternating between clean full-frame speaker shots and flat matte charcoal (#1E2226) graphic screens. Giant bold geometric sans-serif numerals fill a third of the frame above horizontal segmented bars in white and muted slate, with bright orange (#E85C2B) as the sole accent color. Include a single dramatic graphic frame where a giant numeral acts as a silhouette mask revealing orange-tinted speaker footage against pure black (#0A0A0A), plus small dark rounded pills (#2A2E33) with orange line icons overlaid on footage. Lower-third subtitles are plain lowercase white sans-serif with no box, flat matte surfaces, zero gradients, and clean negative space.
