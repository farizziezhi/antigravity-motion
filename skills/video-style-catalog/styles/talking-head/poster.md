# Poster
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 1 (Full graphic screen replaces video)
Full-bleed solid royal-blue poster backdrops with hard cuts, dual-tone white-and-yellow uppercase headlines, fading outline echoes, and red subtitle highlights.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Royal blue backdrop | `#1B2FE8` | Full-bleed solid graphic poster color |
| Headline yellow | `#FFD400` | Second headline line in stacked poster text |
| Primary white | `≈#FFFFFF` | First headline line, lower pill, base subtitles |
| Subtitle red | `≈#E53935` | Keyword highlight color in narration subtitles |
| Pill dark ink | `≈#111318` | Text inside the white secondary pill |

## Materials & texture
Hard-edged flat graphic poster design with zero gradients and zero paper grain. Occasional ultra-subtle curved circular line shapes sit faintly inside the `#1B2FE8` backdrop, while brief talking-head pill moments blur the speaker behind dual stacked badges.

## Typography
Extra-bold uppercase sans-serif spanning full frame width in stacked two-word headlines: line one in `#FFFFFF`, line two in `#FFD400`, followed downward by `3` fading stroke-only outline echoes of line two. Subtitles are `#FFFFFF` sans in the lower third with key words highlighted in `≈#E53935` red.

## Composition
Replaces the real room completely via crisp hard cuts between `#1B2FE8` graphic poster screens, blank `#1B2FE8` breathing frames, and speaker cutouts placed directly in front of the poster color rather than over the original room.

## Mood
Bold, punchy, and graphic-poster driven, where oversized print-style type dominates the speaker.

## Signature elements
- Full-bleed `#1B2FE8` solid royal-blue poster frames replacing the original room via hard cuts.
- Two-line full-width extra-bold uppercase headline (`#FFFFFF` top line, `#FFD400` bottom line) trailing `3` fading stroke-only outline echoes downward.
- Blank solid `#1B2FE8` frames inserted with zero text as clean visual pauses between posters.
- Dual-stacked rounded pills (solid `#1B2FE8` top pill over solid `#FFFFFF` bottom pill) floating over blurred speaker footage, paired with `≈#E53935` red keyword highlights in subtitles.

## Recipe
- Remove the original room on poster segments and cut sharply to full-bleed `#1B2FE8` backgrounds (optionally with faint low-contrast circular arc curves); place the speaker in front via hard cuts rather than translucent overlays.
- Build hero poster frames from one two-line extra-bold uppercase sans phrase (`"FOCUSED"` in `#FFFFFF` over `"SHIFT"` in `#FFD400`), then repeat `"SHIFT"` `3` times downward as thin `#FFD400` outlines stepping down in opacity.
- Insert empty solid `#1B2FE8` frames with no text as rhythmic visual breathers.
- Stack two rounded label pills (`#1B2FE8` upper pill `"FOURTH PRINCIPLE"`, `#FFFFFF` lower pill `"Plan Every Move"`) over heavily blurred speaker footage.
- Render lower-third narration subtitles in `#FFFFFF` sans with `≈#E53935` red keyword highlights.

## Don'ts
- Never blend translucent graphics lightly over the intact original room; use opaque `#1B2FE8` replacements and hard cuts.
- Do not add soft glows, dark edge vignettes, or smooth gradients.
- Never highlight subtitle keywords in yellow or orange; keep subtitle highlights strictly `≈#E53935` red.
- Avoid preserving the original room with zero hard cuts, glowing mint-green and purple chapter numbers, and orange subtitle highlights as in Lumen.

## One-frame test
- Does the frame replace the real room with flat solid `#1B2FE8` royal blue and hard graphic contrast?
- Does the giant headline stack `#FFFFFF` over `#FFD400` with `3` fading outline echoes below?
- Are narration subtitle highlights rendered in `≈#E53935` red rather than orange or yellow?

## Base prompt
Bold graphic-poster talking-head video treatment replacing the real room via crisp hard cuts onto flat full-bleed royal blue (#1B2FE8) backdrops. Giant full-width extra-bold uppercase sans-serif headlines stack a white top word over a bright yellow (#FFD400) second word that echoes three lines downward as fading stroke-only outlines. Includes blank solid blue breather frames, dual-stacked blue-over-white rounded label pills over blurred speaker shots, and lower-third white sans subtitles highlighting key words in vivid red (#E53935). Flat crisp poster ink, zero gradients, zero glow.
