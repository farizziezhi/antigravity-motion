# Lime Punch
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 3 (Annotation over video)
Aggressive close-up punch-ins paired with chunky rounded white caption pills, neon-lime word highlights, numbered checklists, and pale dotted-gradient panels.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Pill & card white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Rounded caption pills and chat card |
| Pale dotted gradient | `≈#E8EDF5` | Light cool backdrop with edge lime glow |
| Primary ink black | `≈#111111` | Extra-bold caption text and dark chat bubbles |
| Neon lime accent | `≈#C6FF00` | Word highlight marker, active number, avatar ring |
| CTA electric blue | `≈#2F4BF0` | Closing pill action button only |

## Materials & texture
High-clarity bright digital finish. Graphic backdrops feature a light `≈#E8EDF5` cool gradient overlaid with a subtle micro-dot pattern and a faint `≈#C6FF00` edge glow. All caption pills and UI cards stay clean, opaque white.

## Typography
Chunky extra-bold black sans-serif inside rounded `#FFFFFF` caption pills (`1` to `3` words per beat, popping word-by-word to speech cadence), with key words backed tightly by a bright `≈#C6FF00` highlighter swipe fitted precisely to the word rather than the whole pill.

## Composition
Dominated by aggressive close-up punch-in framing that fills the screen with the speaker's face on almost every spoken line. Graphics never cover more than half the frame over the speaker, appearing briefly as a numbered checklist beside a small rounded video card, a brief chat panel, and a lime-ringed closing avatar card.

## Mood
Energetic, direct, and emphatic, driven by punchy word-by-word captions and tight facial framing.

## Signature elements
- Chunky `#FFFFFF` rounded caption pills carrying extra-bold `≈#111111` text where only keywords receive a tight `≈#C6FF00` neon-lime highlighter block.
- Aggressive close-up punch-ins keeping the speaker's face dominant across nearly every beat.
- Pale `≈#E8EDF5` dotted-gradient backdrop with subtle `≈#C6FF00` edge glow hosting a numbered checklist (`"1 Trim pauses"`, `"2 Captions"`, active number inside a lime square) next to a small rounded video card.
- Brief white chat card (`black command bubble`, `lime send circle`, `lime 100% progress bar`) and closing card with a lime-bordered circular avatar, bold two-word display (`"STUDIO"` + `"NOTES"` with second word highlighted lime), and a `≈#2F4BF0` pill button.

## Recipe
- Frame speaking lines with aggressive close-up punch-ins; never let graphic overlays obscure more than half the speaker's face.
- Build word-by-word captions (`1` to `3` words at a time) inside `#FFFFFF` rounded pills using extra-bold `≈#111111` sans, wrapping keywords tightly in `≈#C6FF00` highlighter blocks.
- Switch graphic beats to a light `≈#E8EDF5` gradient canvas with a subtle micro-dot grid and faint `≈#C6FF00` perimeter glow.
- Display a numbered checklist (`"1 Trim pauses"`, `"2 Captions"`) with the active number boxed in `≈#C6FF00` beside a small rounded video card, plus one quick white chat card (`"Version 1 ready 100%"`).
- Reserve `≈#2F4BF0` electric blue strictly for the closing pill CTA below a lime-ringed circular portrait and lime-highlighted bold title.

## Don'ts
- Never use dark blurred aurora backdrops or dark translucent caption bars.
- Never highlight entire multi-line caption pills; keep `≈#C6FF00` fitted tightly to individual keywords.
- Do not shrink the speaker into a tiny top window beneath a persistent full-screen chat interface.
- Avoid cream gridded artboards and solid red-orange block wipes as in Flat Artboard, and avoid dark typing-cursor caption bars and full-screen AI chat dominance as in System.

## One-frame test
- Are chunky `#FFFFFF` rounded caption pills displaying extra-bold `≈#111111` words with tight `≈#C6FF00` lime keyword highlights?
- Is the speaker framed in an energetic close-up punch-in or flanked by a numbered lime-boxed checklist on a light dotted `≈#E8EDF5` backdrop?
- Is `≈#2F4BF0` restricted strictly to the closing CTA pill button?

## Base prompt
Energetic caption-led talking-head video treatment using tight close-up punch-in framing and chunky rounded white (#FFFFFF) caption pills with extra-bold black sans-serif words popping in sequence, highlighting keywords tightly in neon lime (#C6FF00). Graphic beats switch to a clean pale blue-gray (#E8EDF5) gradient with a faint dot grid and soft lime edge glow, featuring a numbered checklist with a lime active-number box beside a small rounded video frame, a crisp white chat panel with a lime progress bar, and a closing lime-ringed circular avatar card above an electric blue (#2F4BF0) pill button.
