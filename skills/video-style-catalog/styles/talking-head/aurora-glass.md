# Aurora Glass
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 1 (Full graphic screen replaces video)
Dark blurred purple-magenta-maroon aurora backdrops replacing video with translucent 1 px bordered glass cards, orange-gradient keywords, and twinkling white sparkles.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Aurora deep purple | `≈#3B2A6E` | Primary dark blurred aurora base |
| Aurora dark maroon | `≈#4A1838` | Magenta-maroon secondary aurora glow |
| Orange gradient start | `≈#FF7A2F` | First word gradient start on big keywords |
| Orange gradient end | `≈#FFB347` | First word gradient end on big keywords |
| Teal check badge | `≈#2DD4BF` | Circular check icon on right of glass cards |
| Primary white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Sparkles, card titles, base big words, brackets |

## Materials & texture
Lush dark glassmorphism over heavily blurred drifting aurora light blobs (`≈#3B2A6E`, magenta, deep maroon). Cards use dark semi-transparent glass fills (`≈15%` to `30%` opacity), background blur, and crisp `1 px` bright hairline borders.

## Typography
Extra-bold white sans-serif keywords (`"5 mins"`, `"Endless Loop"`) where the lead word carries a smooth `≈#FF7A2F` to `≈#FFB347` orange gradient. Supporting layers split into small dark translucent top pills prefixed with an orange bullet (`"• just one minute"`) and small dark bottom caption pills.

## Composition
Talking-head footage fills the frame during opening and speaking beats, then disappears completely during graphic segments so drifting aurora gradients and floating glass cards own the full screen. Before/after comparisons use a smartphone mockup linked by a dashed arc.

## Mood
Nocturnal, atmospheric, and polished, prioritizing dark ambient mood over bright instructional grids.

## Signature elements
- Full-screen blurred dark purple-magenta-maroon aurora canvas with slow-drifting soft light blobs replacing talking-head footage entirely.
- Translucent dark rounded glassmorphism cards (`1 px` crisp white edge, gradient square icon on the left, `≈#2DD4BF` circular teal checkmark on the right).
- Two-tone big sans keywords styling the first word in an `≈#FF7A2F` to `≈#FFB347` orange gradient and remaining words in `#FFFFFF`.
- Twinkling white four-point sparkles (`✦`) and camera viewfinder corner brackets on opening speaker shots, plus small dark pills prefixed with an orange dot (`•`).

## Recipe
- Replace talking-head footage completely during graphic beats with a fully blurred dark aurora backdrop (`≈#3B2A6E` to `≈#4A1838`) animated with `2` to `3` slow-moving soft light blobs.
- Place translucent dark rounded glass cards featuring a `1 px` `≈#FFFFFF` hairline border (`≈25%` opacity), a square gradient icon (`purple-pink` or `blue`), bold white title, gray subtext, and a `≈#2DD4BF` circular check badge on the right.
- Render display keywords in extra-bold sans with the lead word filled `≈#FF7A2F` -> `≈#FFB347` and following words `#FFFFFF`; precede moments with dark translucent pills carrying a `•` orange dot.
- Place lower-third captions inside small dark translucent rounded pills rather than floating bare text.
- Sprinkle tiny `#FFFFFF` four-point `✦` sparkles and thin corner viewfinder brackets onto opening speaker frames, and use a smartphone outline with a dashed arc for comparisons.

## Don'ts
- Never use flat solid paper colors, visible grid lines, or hard opaque shadows.
- Never keep talking-head footage visible behind the glassmorphism card screens; let aurora gradients take the full frame.
- Do not render solid flat red-orange keyword blocks or wipe transitions.
- Avoid pale cream artboard canvases, black-bordered white cards, or flat plus-sign scatter markers as in Flat Artboard.

## One-frame test
- Is the graphic frame a full-screen blurred dark purple-maroon aurora with `1 px` bordered dark glassmorphism cards and `≈#2DD4BF` check badges?
- Does the lead word of oversized white sans typography carry an `≈#FF7A2F` to `≈#FFB347` orange gradient?
- Do dark translucent pills use a small leading orange dot (`•`) and opening speaker shots include `✦` sparkles or corner brackets?

## Base prompt
Dark atmospheric talking-head video treatment alternating full-frame speaker shots accented with white four-point sparkles and thin camera corner brackets against full-screen blurred purple-magenta-maroon (#3B2A6E to #4A1838) aurora backdrops with slow-drifting light blobs. Floating dark translucent glassmorphism cards with crisp 1px bright borders display gradient square icons, bold white titles, and teal (#2DD4BF) check circles. Large extra-bold sans keywords shade their lead word in a smooth orange gradient (#FF7A2F to #FFB347), paired with small dark translucent bottom caption pills and orange-dot label pills.
