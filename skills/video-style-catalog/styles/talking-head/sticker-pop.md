# Sticker Pop
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 5 (Stickers and paste-ups)
Tilted black-outlined multi-color sticker pills, diagonal desaturated split-screens, and white-outlined subject cutouts on vivid purple.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Sticker yellow | `≈#FFE100` | Primary tilted sticker fill and caption accent |
| Vivid purple | `≈#5E3DE0` | Solid cutout background and badge sticker fill |
| Light sticker green | `≈#A6F77B` | Secondary tilted label and closing CTA pill |
| White sticker / border | `≈#FFFFFF` | Cutout outline, base captions, neutral stickers |
| Heavy ink black | `≈#111111` | Thick sticker borders, shadows, caption outlines |

## Materials & texture
Tactile handmade collage look mixing raw video, thick white cutout edges, and flat paper-like vector stickers wrapped in bold `≈#111111` black outlines. Zero soft glassmorphism or subtle translucent gradients.

## Typography
Extra-bold `#FFFFFF` sans-serif captions set directly over video with heavy `≈#111111` outlines/drop shadows and zero pill containers; keywords switch to bright `≈#FFE100` yellow. Tilted rounded sticker pills carry bold uppercase black or white sans labels with thick `≈#111111` borders.

## Composition
Playful collage rhythm rotating between raw talking-head shots plastered with tilted multi-color sticker pills, a diagonal white-line split-screen comparing desaturated (`"RAW"`) vs full-color (`"POLISHED"`) footage, and a full cutout of the speaker with a thick white outline pasted onto vivid `≈#5E3DE0` purple. Closes on a messy stacked sticker lockup over pale blue-gray.

## Mood
Playful, busy, and casual, capturing the handmade energy of a creator pasting physical stickers onto footage.

## Signature elements
- Multi-color tilted pill stickers (`≈#FFE100`, `≈#A6F77B`, `≈#5E3DE0`, `#FFFFFF`) rotated a few degrees with thick `≈#111111` black outlines (`"✂ TRIMMED"`, `"CAPTIONS"`, `"ONE CLICK"`).
- Diagonal split-screen divided by a crisp white diagonal line: desaturated grayscale raw footage on the left (`"RAW"` sticker) versus vivid full-color footage on the right (`"POLISHED"` sticker).
- Subject cutout wrapped in a thick `#FFFFFF` sticker outline and pasted onto solid vivid `≈#5E3DE0` purple with two angled badge stickers (`"STEP ONE"`, `"CORE"`).
- Containerless extra-bold `#FFFFFF` captions with heavy black outlines and `≈#FFE100` keywords, ending on crooked stacked stickers (`"STUDIO"` on yellow, `"NOTES"` on purple, `"↓ SEE BELOW"` on light green).

## Recipe
- Render captions directly over video without pill containers using extra-bold `#FFFFFF` sans with thick `≈#111111` stroke/shadow; color keywords `≈#FFE100`.
- Style every badge and callout as an angled rounded pill (`±3°` to `8°` tilt) with a crisp `≈#111111` border (`2` to `3 px`), mixing `≈#FFE100`, `≈#A6F77B`, `≈#5E3DE0`, and `#FFFFFF` across the screen.
- Build one diagonal split-screen divided by a bold `#FFFFFF` line from top-right to bottom-left: left half desaturated grayscale (`"RAW"` white sticker), right half full color (`"POLISHED"` yellow sticker).
- Cut out the speaker with a thick `#FFFFFF` sticker contour and composite onto solid `≈#5E3DE0` purple alongside two tilted stickers.
- Assemble the closing card from overlapping crooked stickers (`"STUDIO"` black on `≈#FFE100`, `"NOTES"` white on `≈#5E3DE0`, `"↓ SEE BELOW"` on `≈#A6F77B`) over a pale neutral backdrop; never straighten elements to a rigid grid.

## Don'ts
- Never straighten sticker labels into rigid horizontal rows or uniform grid alignments.
- Never place lower-third captions inside neat rounded white or dark translucent pill containers.
- Do not restrict stickers to a single monochrome accent color; mix yellow, light green, purple, and white.
- Avoid neat ruled notebook stationery with heavy background blur or serious monochrome tones.

## One-frame test
- Are angled multi-color pill stickers with thick `≈#111111` outlines pasted casually across the frame?
- Does the edit include either a diagonal desaturated-versus-color split-screen or a white-outlined speaker cutout on vivid `≈#5E3DE0` purple?
- Are extra-bold `#FFFFFF` and `≈#FFE100` captions outlined in heavy black with zero background pill box?

## Base prompt
Playful handmade sticker-collage talking-head video treatment featuring extra-bold white sans-serif captions with thick black outlines and yellow (#FFE100) keywords floating without any pill container. Multi-color tilted pill stickers in yellow, light green (#A6F77B), vivid purple (#5E3DE0), and white with bold black borders scatter at slight angles across the shot. Includes a crisp white-line diagonal split-screen comparing desaturated grayscale footage against vivid full color, a thick white-outlined speaker cutout pasted onto solid vivid purple, and a closing card made of crooked stacked stickers. Energetic, tactile, intentionally unaligned collage.
