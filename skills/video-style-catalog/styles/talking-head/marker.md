# Marker
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 1 (Full graphic screen replaces video)
Pitch-black cards with black bold text inside neon-yellow highlighter strips, stacked phrase lists, and giant yellow words over blurred footage.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Void black | `#0A0A0A` | Full-screen graphic background |
| Highlighter yellow | `#F5E942` | Solid highlighter strips and giant words |
| Primary white | `#FFFFFF` | Subtitles, thin line icons, small subtext |
| Strip ink black | `≈#0A0A0A` | Bold text seated inside yellow strips |
| Transition gold (rare) | `≈#C9A96A` | Brief floating italic serif accent only |

## Materials & texture
Stark flat matte surfaces with zero gradients and zero drop shadows. The only softness in the frame is an intentional heavy background blur on talking-head footage behind giant yellow words.

## Typography
Extra-bold sans-serif in black seated tightly inside solid `#F5E942` highlighter bars, paired with full-width uppercase `#F5E942` display words and thin white line icons. Subtitles stay plain lowercase `#FFFFFF` sans in the lower third without any highlight box.

## Composition
Alternates between full-frame talking-head shots (with an occasional small yellow-highlighted keyword or hand-placed numeral) and pitch-black `#0A0A0A` graphic screens holding single or stacked highlighter bars. Full-width blurred video frames serve as the stage for giant uppercase `#F5E942` hook words.

## Mood
Loud, high-contrast, and punchy, like bold highlighter strokes across a black chalkboard.

## Signature elements
- Solid `#F5E942` highlighter bars behind bold `#0A0A0A` text (never yellow text on black), stacked 3 to 4 bars tall with tight gaps for lists.
- Giant uppercase `#F5E942` word spanning full frame width over heavily blurred talking-head video, paired with small white subtext below.
- Thin white line icons (eye, filmstrip, book) centered directly above highlighted phrase blocks as section markers.
- Small `#F5E942` numeral tag anchored near the speaker's hand gesture on raw talking-head shots.

## Recipe
- Restrict the core palette strictly to `#0A0A0A`, `#F5E942`, and `#FFFFFF`.
- Render keyword callouts as `#0A0A0A` bold sans text inside `#F5E942` rectangular strips (`1` phrase per strip; stack `3` to `4` strips tightly for lists such as `"clear story"`, `"tight pacing"`, `"every frame"`, `"has intent"`).
- Place one full-width uppercase `#F5E942` hook word (e.g. `"WHY"`) over heavily blurred talking-head footage with small `#FFFFFF` subtext (`"does it work?"`) underneath.
- Top each `#0A0A0A` highlight card with one small white thin-stroke icon; keep lower-third narration subtitles plain lowercase `#FFFFFF` without highlights.

## Don'ts
- Never render yellow text directly on a black background instead of black text inside a `#F5E942` strip.
- Never apply highlighter bars to the lower-third narration subtitle line.
- Do not introduce gradients, neon outer glows, or extra accent colors.
- Avoid washing the entire talking-head frame in translucent yellow with dark slate lowercase text or using full-bleed solid yellow cards as in Bold.

## One-frame test
- Are key phrases rendered as black bold sans text inside crisp `#F5E942` highlighter strips on `#0A0A0A`?
- Does the giant yellow uppercase word sit over heavily blurred footage rather than a solid yellow background?
- Are narration subtitles plain lowercase white sans with zero highlighter bars?

## Base prompt
High-contrast talking-head video edit built on a strict three-color palette of pitch black (#0A0A0A), neon highlighter yellow (#F5E942), and crisp white (#FFFFFF). Alternates between full-frame speaker shots and stark black graphic cards displaying bold black sans-serif phrases seated inside solid yellow highlighter strips stacked neatly into vertical lists, topped by thin white line icons. Key hook moments blur the speaker footage completely behind a full-width uppercase yellow word and small white subtext. Lower-third narration subtitles remain plain lowercase white sans with no background box, flat matte finish, zero gradients.
