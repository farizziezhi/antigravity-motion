# Typeset
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 2 (Video inside a layout)
Subject cutout on electric purple backdrops layered with italic serif, extra-bold uppercase display words, and fading stacked outline echoes.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Electric purple | `#3D1FD6` | Dominant solid backdrop for cutout segments |
| Halo purple | `≈#4326E0` | Solid circular graphic halo behind head |
| Lavender serif | `#B9A8F5` | Italic serif accent text over electric purple |
| Display white | `#FFFFFF` | Giant uppercase sans words and pause phrases |
| Pause black | `#0A0A0A` | Full-screen chapter divider cards |

## Materials & texture
Solid, flat graphic color fields (`#3D1FD6`, `#FFFFFF`, `#0A0A0A`) with zero gradients or paper grain. Soft drop shadows appear only underneath shrunken rounded video cards on white backgrounds.

## Typography
Three-voice typographic mix per composition: delicate italic serif (`#B9A8F5` or `#111111`) paired with extra-bold uppercase sans (`#FFFFFF`) and vertical stacks of fading stroke-only outline echoes behind the speaker. Emphasis words float as white italic serif instead of plain subtitles.

## Composition
Never leaves raw room footage intact. Every segment reshapes the video via one of three treatments: full subject cutout on solid `#3D1FD6`, solid purple circle halo behind the head, or video shrunk into a rounded card (`≈24 px` radius) on `#FFFFFF`, separated by `#0A0A0A` numbered chapter cards.

## Mood
Bold, experimental, and poster-driven, like an independent graphic design specimen.

## Signature elements
- Vertical outline echo: an extra-bold uppercase word (`"SCALE"`) repeated 2 to 3 times downward in thin white outline at decreasing opacity behind the cutout speaker.
- Subject cutout composited cleanly onto solid `#3D1FD6` with `#B9A8F5` italic serif paired directly above `#FFFFFF` extra-bold uppercase sans.
- Shrunken talking-head video framed as a `≈24 px` rounded card with soft shadow on a pure `#FFFFFF` canvas beneath black italic serif text.
- Full-screen `#0A0A0A` chapter pause cards carrying one `#FFFFFF` italic serif phrase and a small purple chapter index (`"01"`).

## Recipe
- Remove the original room background via cutout and composite the speaker over flat `#3D1FD6`; optionally place a solid `≈#4326E0` circle halo behind the head.
- Build each typographic lockup by stacking `#B9A8F5` italic serif above giant `#FFFFFF` extra-bold uppercase sans, then repeating the uppercase word `2` to `3` lines downward as stroke-only outlines with stepping-down opacity.
- Alternate with `#FFFFFF` screens where the video shrinks into a `≈24 px` rounded card with a soft shadow in the center or corner beneath `#0A0A0A` italic serif text (`"why does it hold?"`).
- Insert `#0A0A0A` full-screen pause cards with one `#FFFFFF` italic serif line and a small `#3D1FD6` chapter numeral (`"01"`).
- Render spoken emphasis words as floating `#FFFFFF` italic serif rather than plain sans subtitles.

## Don'ts
- Never leave the original unedited room visible behind plain lower-third subtitles.
- Do not add gradients, glowing blurs, or realistic 3D bevels to the solid backdrops.
- Never omit the fading outline echo or drop down to a single uniform sans typeface.
- Avoid placing drop shadows on the `#3D1FD6` cutout frames (reserve soft shadows for rounded video cards on `#FFFFFF`).

## One-frame test
- Is the speaker visibly transformed into a cutout on `#3D1FD6`, framed inside a `#FFFFFF` rounded card, or paused by a `#0A0A0A` numbered chapter screen?
- Does the display lockup combine italic serif, extra-bold uppercase sans, and stacked fading outline echoes?
- Are spoken emphasis phrases set in italic serif rather than plain lower-third sans?

## Base prompt
Graphic-design talking-head video treatment where raw footage is completely reshaped into typographic poster compositions. The speaker appears as a clean cutout over solid electric purple (#3D1FD6) or framed behind a solid purple circular halo, layered with lavender (#B9A8F5) italic serif words above giant white extra-bold uppercase sans typography that repeats downward in fading stroke-only outline echoes. Alternates with pure white frames holding the video inside a 24px rounded shadow card under black italic serif text, and pitch-black (#0A0A0A) chapter divider cards marked with a small purple index numeral. Flat solid colors, zero gradients.
