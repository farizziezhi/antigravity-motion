# Podcast
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 3 (Annotation over video)
Three-quarter off-axis speaker against flat dark olive with translucent yellow sans background words and one white italic serif emphasis card.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Flat dark olive | `≈#2A2721` | Evenly lit unvignetted dark studio wall |
| Primary white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Small bold sans subtitles and serif card line |
| Muted card gray | `≈#9CA3AF` | Small sans lead-in line on the dark card |
| Keyword yellow | `≈#F5C518` | Semi-transparent giant uppercase keywords |
| Subtle green accent | `≈#34C759` | Tiny dot or mark beside select keywords |

## Materials & texture
Even, unvignetted dark olive-brown backdrop lit softly from the side, keeping natural conversational depth without heavy contrast masks or textures.

## Typography
Two distinct registers: small bold `#FFFFFF` sans subtitles in the lower third paired with huge `≈#F5C518` extra-bold uppercase keywords layered semi-transparently behind or beside the subtitles. One full-screen dark card introduces a large `#FFFFFF` italic serif punchline below a small `#9CA3AF` sans setup.

## Composition
Medium shot where the speaker sits at a three-quarter profile angle looking left off-camera rather than straight into the lens, evoking an active interview. Sparse line icons float above the head while natural hand gestures carry the frame.

## Mood
Conversational, thoughtful, and unscripted, like an excerpt from a studio interview.

## Signature elements
- Three-quarter profile camera angle with the speaker facing off-camera to the side instead of addressing the lens directly.
- Semi-transparent giant uppercase `≈#F5C518` extra-bold sans keywords (`"EVER"`, `"STEADY"`) layered softly behind lower-middle subtitles.
- Single `≈#2A2721` full-screen emphasis card pairing a small `#9CA3AF` sans setup line with a large `#FFFFFF` italic serif resolution line.
- Flat unvignetted `≈#2A2721` background with only 1 or 2 thin white labeled line icons above the head across the entire edit.

## Recipe
- Frame the speaker in a medium shot at a `3/4` angle facing off-camera left against a flat, softly side-lit `≈#2A2721` dark olive wall with zero vignette.
- Set lower-third narration subtitles in small bold `#FFFFFF` sans with no container box.
- Place giant extra-bold uppercase `≈#F5C518` keywords in the lower-center behind the subtitles at `≈35%` to `55%` opacity, optionally paired with a tiny `≈#34C759` accent mark.
- Insert one `≈#2A2721` full-screen card combining small `#9CA3AF` sans above (`"not missing a better idea,"`) and large `#FFFFFF` italic serif below (`"we simply stopped too soon"`).
- Limit white labeled line icons above the head (`"craft"`, `"focus"`) to 1 or 2 across the whole piece.

## Don'ts
- Never frame the speaker staring dead-center into the camera lens like a lecture.
- Never apply a dark radial spotlight vignette around the speaker's face.
- Do not use italic serif fonts in the standard lower-third subtitles (save serif strictly for the emphasis card).
- Avoid flooding the screen with frequent full-screen cards or dense multi-icon rows.

## One-frame test
- Is the speaker angled in a three-quarter off-camera profile against a flat unvignetted `≈#2A2721` wall?
- Do giant semi-transparent `≈#F5C518` uppercase words sit behind small bold `#FFFFFF` lower-third subtitles?
- Does serif typography appear exclusively on the single dark emphasis card?

## Base prompt
Conversational interview-style talking-head video treatment framed from a medium three-quarter profile angle where the speaker looks off-camera to the left against a flat, softly side-lit dark olive (#2A2721) wall with no vignette. Lower-third subtitles are small bold white sans-serif without boxes, layered in front of giant semi-transparent uppercase yellow (#F5C518) sans keywords. Includes one dark full-screen quote card pairing a small gray sans setup line above a large white italic serif punchline, plus one or two minimal white line icons above the head. Natural, unscripted studio tone.
