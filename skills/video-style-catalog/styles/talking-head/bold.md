# Bold
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 3 (Annotation over video)
Bright talking-head footage interrupted by translucent full-frame yellow color washes, giant dark-slate lowercase words, and solid yellow cards.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Dominant yellow | `≈#F9D423` | Translucent frame wash and full-screen card |
| Bright yellow | `≈#FFD21F` | Secondary solid yellow keyword accent |
| Dark slate text | `≈#3A3F5C` | Giant lowercase word inside the yellow wash |
| Subtitle white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Small lower-third narration text |
| Card black | `≈#111111` | Single-line sans text on solid yellow cards |

## Materials & texture
Bright, crisp daylight footage deliberately disrupted by flat solid yellow cards and semi-transparent yellow color washes. Zero vignettes, zero soft blurs, and zero serif elegance.

## Typography
Strictly sans-serif throughout, with zero serif typefaces anywhere. Extra-bold uppercase `≈#F9D423` keywords sit oversized near the center of the frame, while the yellow-wash moment flips to giant extra-bold lowercase `≈#3A3F5C` text (`"why"`) with small subtext (`"does it happen?"`). Small white sans subtitles anchor the lower third.

## Composition
Medium shot against a light background (same base framing as Clean) dominated by center-frame typography rather than quiet margins. Thin white labeled line icons appear only as secondary support behind aggressive center-frame yellow blocks and full-screen `≈#F9D423` cards.

## Mood
Loud, confrontational, and energetic, designed as a punchy short-form hook.

## Signature elements
- Full-frame semi-transparent `≈#F9D423` yellow wash flooding both speaker and background behind a giant lowercase `≈#3A3F5C` sans word.
- Full-screen solid `≈#F9D423` yellow card holding a single centered line of small `≈#111111` black sans text.
- Center-frame oversized extra-bold uppercase `≈#F9D423` / `≈#FFD21F` keywords (`"ONE RULE"`, `"80"`) dominating the speaker rather than tucking neatly at the bottom.
- Complete absence of serif fonts, vignettes, and soft blurs.

## Recipe
- Start from bright, evenly lit medium-shot talking-head footage on a light wall without vignettes or blurs.
- Insert a full-frame `≈#F9D423` translucent wash (`≈70%` to `85%` opacity) covering the entire shot, centered with giant extra-bold lowercase `≈#3A3F5C` text (`"why"`) and small subtext (`"does it happen?"`).
- Cut to a solid opaque `≈#F9D423` full-screen card with a single centered line of small `≈#111111` sans text (`"how you hold attention"`).
- Place massive extra-bold uppercase `≈#F9D423` keywords squarely in the middle of the frame while keeping lower-third narration subtitles small plain `#FFFFFF` sans.
- Keep white labeled line icons secondary and sparse (`"story / pacing / timing"`).

## Don'ts
- Never use serif or italic serif fonts anywhere in the edit.
- Never apply dark vignettes, heavy background blurs, or moody low-key grading.
- Do not relegate big keywords quietly to the bottom third; keep them huge and central.
- Avoid pitch-black full-screen text cards with stacked black-text-on-yellow highlighter strips as in Marker.

## One-frame test
- Does a yellow-wash frame cover the entire talking-head shot in `≈#F9D423` behind giant `≈#3A3F5C` lowercase sans text?
- Is there an opaque full-screen `≈#F9D423` card with just one small centered `≈#111111` sans line?
- Is every typeface strictly bold or clean sans-serif with zero serifs and zero vignettes?

## Base prompt
Energetic high-contrast talking-head video treatment starting from bright evenly lit footage and colliding with saturated yellow (#F9D423). Key hook moments flood the entire frame with a translucent yellow color wash behind giant extra-bold lowercase dark slate (#3A3F5C) sans-serif text and small subtext, alternating with opaque solid yellow full-screen cards holding one centered line of small black sans text. Over raw footage, huge uppercase yellow keywords anchor the center of the frame above small white lower-third subtitles, with occasional secondary white line icons. Strictly sans-serif throughout, flat punchy contrast, zero serifs, zero vignettes, zero blurs.
