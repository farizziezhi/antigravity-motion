# Flat Artboard
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 4 (Video as a small window inside a UI system)
Pale cream gridded design artboard with corner brackets, red-orange plus marks, solid color-block keyword wipes, and flat dual-phone outlines.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Artboard cream | `≈#F2EFE9` | Flat gridded canvas replacing dark gradients |
| Grid line gray | `≈#D4CFC6` | Thin square artboard grid and corner marks |
| Warm red-orange | `≈#FF3D20` | Active chips, word blocks, `+` registration marks |
| Electric blue | `≈#2F4BF0` | Secondary accent for square card icons |
| Ink black | `≈#111111` | Primary black blocks, borders, check circles |
| Card white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Flat solid cards, label pills, speaker frame |

## Materials & texture
Pure vector flat design resembling an open vector design artboard. Strictly zero gradients, zero drop shadows, and zero blurs; every card, phone frame, and word block is crisp and opaque.

## Typography
Bold sans-serif words seated inside solid rectangular color blocks (`#FFFFFF` text on `≈#111111` black blocks paired with `≈#111111` text on `≈#FF3D20` red-orange blocks), animated via crisp rectangular block wipes rather than fades. Small label pills are `#FFFFFF` with a leading black dot (`"• everything"`).

## Composition
Speaker fills the screen during direct delivery, then shrinks into a small white-bordered card inside a full-screen `≈#F2EFE9` gridded artboard framed by 4 corner brackets and scattered `≈#FF3D20` `+` marks. Side-by-side comparisons show two flat outlined phones linked by a dashed arc.

## Mood
Clean, technical, and structured, like a live designer walkthrough on a digital canvas.

## Signature elements
- Pale cream `≈#F2EFE9` artboard canvas ruled with a fine grid, 4 outer corner brackets, and scattered `≈#FF3D20` `+` crosshairs.
- Color-block word wipes: solid `≈#111111` and `≈#FF3D20` rectangular bars carrying contrasting bold sans words (`"Clean"` / `"design"`).
- Top-row checklist chips where the active step turns solid `≈#FF3D20` (`"✓ Trim pauses"`, `"Captions"`, `"Motion"`, `"Scale"`).
- Two flat side-by-side wireframe smartphone mockups connected by a dashed red-dotted arc, plus solid white offer cards with `≈#2F4BF0` square icons and black check circles.

## Recipe
- Set graphic canvases to flat `≈#F2EFE9` with fine `≈#D4CFC6` grid lines, `4` thin corner brackets, and scattered small `≈#FF3D20` `+` marks; prohibit all blurs, gradients, and soft shadows.
- Shrink the talking-head shot into a small white-framed rectangular card when artboard graphics appear.
- Build key captions from solid color blocks (`#FFFFFF` bold sans on `≈#111111` black, paired with `≈#111111` bold sans on `≈#FF3D20`) animated with crisp sliding block wipes.
- Display step lists as horizontal top chips (active chip in `≈#FF3D20`, inactive in `#FFFFFF` / `≈#111111`), and show before/after states as two flat thin-bordered phones joined by a dashed arc.
- Style top micro-labels as `#FFFFFF` pills prefixed with a black `•` dot, and pair white feature cards with `≈#2F4BF0` square icons and solid black check circles.

## Don'ts
- Never use blurs, glowing halos, soft drop shadows, or color gradients anywhere.
- Never swap the warm `≈#F2EFE9` + `≈#FF3D20` palette for dark aurora backdrops or neon lime highlights.
- Do not animate text with soft opacity fades or bouncy scale pops; use hard block wipes.
- Avoid white caption pills with lime highlighter or light dotted gradients as in Lime Punch, and avoid full-screen AI chat panels with dark typing-cursor bars as in System.

## One-frame test
- Does the graphic frame look like a flat `≈#F2EFE9` design artboard with a visible thin grid, 4 corner brackets, and `≈#FF3D20` `+` marks?
- Are keywords mounted inside solid `≈#111111` and `≈#FF3D20` flat rectangles with zero shadows or gradients?
- Are checklists rendered as horizontal flat chips and comparisons shown as two flat outlined phones?
