# Calm
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 3 (Annotation over video)
Warm dark brown-black frames with widely tracked pale gold italic serif words, generous empty space, and a closing circular face crop.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Warm dark brown | `≈#1A1712` | Soft low-key backdrop and blank pause cards |
| Soft cream | `≈#F0EAE0` | Quiet italic serif lines on dark cards |
| Faded gold | `≈#C9A86A` | Enlarged italic serif keywords and accents |

## Materials & texture
Warm, soft low-key lighting with gentle tonal roll-off and no harsh radial spotlight edges. Surfaces remain completely flat and calm, letting negative space carry the frame.

## Typography
Nearly every piece of on-screen text uses thin, widely letter-spaced italic serif in `≈#F0EAE0` or `≈#C9A86A`. Subtitles stay small and poetic in the lower frame, while keywords enlarge in the same `≈#C9A86A` italic serif in mid-frame (occasionally preceded by one tiny neutral sans lead-in line).

## Composition
Medium-close stable framing bathed in warm shadow against `≈#1A1712` with abundant open negative space. Alternates with bare `≈#1A1712` cards carrying one line of centered italic serif, and closes with the speaker masked into a small central circle surrounded by empty darkness.

## Mood
Reflective, unhurried, and literary, meant to be read quietly rather than shouted.

## Signature elements
- Closing circular face crop: the speaker's face masked into a small centered circle against bare `≈#1A1712` darkness with one line of `≈#F0EAE0` italic serif above it.
- Widely tracked, thin `≈#F0EAE0` / `≈#C9A86A` italic serif subtitles and enlarged `≈#C9A86A` italic serif keywords (`"ahead"`, `"20"`).
- Plain `≈#1A1712` full-screen pause cards holding a single quiet line of `≈#F0EAE0` italic serif (`"we each keep our own"`).
- Near-monochrome warm brown-gold palette with at most one thin line icon per segment.

## Recipe
- Light the speaker softly against a warm dark `≈#1A1712` background; avoid sharp spotlight vignettes or vivid saturated hues.
- Set lower-third subtitles in small, thin, loosely tracked italic serif (`≈#F0EAE0` / `≈#C9A86A`) instead of bold sans.
- Enlarge key words (`"ahead"`) and numerals (`"20"`) in the same `≈#C9A86A` italic serif near mid-frame, leaving wide breathing room around them.
- Insert plain `≈#1A1712` full-screen cards with one short centered italic serif line, and limit line icons to at most one thin glyph per beat.
- Close with a centered circular crop (`≈25%` to `35%` frame width) of the speaker on an empty `≈#1A1712` screen with one italic serif line above.

## Don'ts
- Never use bright neon yellow, high-contrast bold sans shouts, or busy icon clusters.
- Never apply a hard theatrical spotlight vignette around the face.
- Do not crowd the screen with multi-element cards or colored highlight blocks.
- Avoid newspaper broadsheet columns, gray greeking lines, hairline dividers, black highlight bars, or cream paper borders as in Press.

## One-frame test
- Is the frame bathed in warm dark `≈#1A1712` tones with thin, widely tracked `≈#C9A86A` / `≈#F0EAE0` italic serif text and wide negative space?
- Are keywords enlarged in the exact same pale-gold italic serif rather than bold yellow sans?
- Does the ending isolate the speaker inside a small centered circular crop on empty darkness?

## Base prompt
Quiet, literary talking-head video treatment set against a softly lit warm brown-black (#1A1712) background with generous empty negative space and no harsh spotlight vignette. Almost all typography is thin, widely tracked italic serif in soft cream (#F0EAE0) and faded gold (#C9A86A), flowing from small lower-third subtitles to enlarged gold italic keywords mid-frame. Includes bare dark full-screen pause cards holding a single centered italic serif line, at most one delicate line icon per beat, and a signature closing frame cropping the speaker's face into a small centered circle floating in open darkness beneath one serif line.
