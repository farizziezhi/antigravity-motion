# Depth
Talking-head edit · Treatment family 2 (Video inside a layout)
Uncut talking-head layered in front of cropped typographic wallpaper and tiled video mosaics, accented with black hand-drawn doodles and pale blue.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Cream room tone | `≈#EFECE4` | Neutral studio backdrop behind text layers |
| Ink black | `≈#141414` | Hand-drawn doodles, dark pills, outline text |
| Primary white | `≈#FFFFFF` | Plain lower-third subtitles and light type |
| Pale blue accent | `#B9C8E8` | Single solid row inside stacked word walls |

## Materials & texture
Flat editorial magazine feel combining crisp vector type, raw sketchy ink doodles with uneven line weight, and repeated video tiles. Nearly monochrome overall, creating visual depth through Z-axis foreground/background layering rather than blur or shadow.

## Typography
High-contrast typographic wallpaper alternating italic serif (`"endless loop"`) with bold outlined uppercase sans (`"ENDLESS LOOP"`), plus giant `4` to `5`-line repeated questions (`"AGAIN?"`) where one row fills `#B9C8E8` and the rest remain outlines. Subtitles stay completely plain small `#FFFFFF` sans with zero keyword highlights.

## Composition
Continuous uncut shot (`0` hard cuts) where 3 to 4 stacked typographic and visual layers sit behind and beside the subject, intentionally bleeding past frame edges. Corner icon pills and a tiled mini-video mosaic wall anchor select beats behind the speaker.

## Mood
Clever, editorial, and layered, like a tactile magazine spread with literal front-to-back depth.

## Signature elements
- Typographic wallpaper: repeated alternating lines of italic serif and bold stroke-only sans extending behind the subject and deliberately cropped at frame edges.
- Hand-drawn black ink doodles (rough oval halo over the head, sketchy arrow pointing inward, giant hand-drawn `"?"` beside the face) contrasting against precise typography.
- Video-tile mosaic wall: dozens of miniature repeated copies of the talking-head shot tiled behind the foreground speaker.
- Corner icon-plus-pill badges (e.g. small book icon + black `"final page"` pill, blue play icon + `"closing"` pill) and zero-highlight white subtitles.

## Recipe
- Keep a single continuous talking-head shot without cuts; build depth by placing `3` to `4` graphic layers behind the speaker cutout rather than swapping to blank rooms.
- Fill the rear layer with repeating typographic wallpaper alternating italic serif (`"endless loop"`) and bold outline sans (`"ENDLESS LOOP"`) across varied scales, cropped past the left and right edges.
- Stack one `4` to `5`-row giant word block (`"AGAIN?"`) using `#B9C8E8` solid fill on one single row and stroke outlines on all others.
- Add rough hand-drawn `≈#141414` ink doodles (`halo oval`, `pointing arrow`, `large "?"`) around the speaker's head.
- Include one mosaic beat tiling small copies of the shot behind the foreground speaker, small corner icon-plus-pill badges, and plain `#FFFFFF` lower-third subtitles with no color highlights.

## Don'ts
- Never highlight words in yellow, lime, orange, or red inside the lower-third subtitles.
- Do not rely on hard cuts or solid neon backdrops to separate segments.
- Never replace precision serif/sans wallpaper with uniform single-weight text; maintain stroke-versus-fill and serif-versus-sans contrast.
- Keep doodles visibly hand-drawn and imperfect rather than clean geometric vector shapes.

## One-frame test
- Are multiple typographic or tiled-video layers stacked behind the speaker and cropped off the frame edges?
- Do rough black hand-drawn doodles contrast against clean alternating serif and outline sans typography?
- Is `#B9C8E8` pale blue the sole chromatic accent while lower-third subtitles remain completely unhighlighted white?

## Base prompt
Layered editorial talking-head video treatment built from a continuous uncut shot with literal foreground-to-background depth. Behind the speaker, three to four typographic wallpaper layers repeat phrases in alternating italic serif and bold stroke-only sans-serif at varied sizes cropped past the frame edges, alongside a stacked giant word block where one single row fills pale blue (#B9C8E8). Rough black hand-drawn ink doodles, including an oval halo above the head, a sketchy arrow, and a giant question mark, contrast against crisp type. Includes a tiled mini-video mosaic backdrop, small corner icon-plus-pill tags, and plain unhighlighted white lower-third subtitles.
